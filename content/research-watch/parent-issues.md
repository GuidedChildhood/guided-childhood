# Common parent problems, linked to research, our philosophy and the product

Justin, 16 November 2026: tie the common parent issues to research, our
philosophy and the solution. One row per problem. The research column names
the radar stream that watches it (`scripts/radar/streams.mjs`), so new
evidence on a problem lands in the right row. No figure goes in this file:
a number only appears in a post once the radar has cleared it.

The problem wording comes from the parent sweep in
`research/homepage-audience-language.md` and the Mumsnet, Reddit and Facebook
sweep planned on 14 November. Add a row when a new problem keeps coming up.
Every row must end on a real product surface, or it does not belong here.

| "Every night is a fight when the screen goes off" | 19 family and parenting, 04 sleep | The fight is the missing plan, not the child. | "I feel like I'm policing all the time" | 19, 22 interventions | Blocking makes today's decision and teaches nothing for tomorrow | The daily page lead, quests the child can pitch, the check in that shows whether it is getting calmer |

| "The class WhatsApp group is going wild at night" | 12 group chats and peers, 04 sleep | Excitement is healthy; the frenzy needs shaping, not banning | Scripts, DiGi (briefed by the first WhatsApp moment research, 27 August), protected night windows |
