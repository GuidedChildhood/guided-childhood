# Justin's LinkedIn profile as a landing page

Drafted 7 November 2026, from the Chris Donnelly teardown
(`content/linkedin/chris-donnelly-teardown.md`, point 7). The profile button is
our funnel: 165 clicks a year against 13 from links in posts. So the profile
does the selling and every post points at it.

Paste these in. Nothing here goes live until Justin pastes it.

## Headline (LinkedIn allows 220 characters; this is 153)

```
Founder of Guided Childhood. Getting children to 16 ready for a phone and social media, ten minutes a day. Follow for the research on kids, phones and AI
```

Why it is shaped this way: what he does, then the promise in the one line
story's own words (THE-STORY.md section 1), then "Follow for", which is the
line that turns a stranger reading one post into a follower.

## Featured, in this order

1. **Link: guidedchildhood.com/starter-pack**
   Title: `What stage is your child?`
   Description: `Three questions, no sign up. See what your child is ready for next, and the one thing to work on this week.`
2. **The Substack newsletter: https://open.substack.com/pub/justiniphillips**
   The second thing a stranger can own, the follower who is his rather than
   LinkedIn's.
3. **The beta = 0.061 post** (75,842 impressions). The best proof that the
   account is worth following, and the first card in the series.
4. **The schools banner** (`content/linkedin/featured-banner/banner-1200x627.png`),
   already built, for heads and DSLs.

Only the first two get seen without a scroll on a phone, so the order matters
more than the contents of three and four.
