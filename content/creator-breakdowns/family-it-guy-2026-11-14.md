## 2. One page

### What he gets right

- The pattern. Post 11 describes it in order: a message in the game, a gift of Robux, trust, then a move to Snapchat or Discord. The USA Today case he shared in post 20 shows the same thing with Discord. That is the pattern we hold.
- The right first question. "If strangers can message your child, they are at risk" (post 19) and "Who is my child sharing this space with?" (post 8) are our first pathway question.
- The 2026 changes exist and can be got round. He reports the chat age check himself in six posts and gives one example of it misreading an adult. That sits with our point that children still get round it.
- Chat off by default for the youngest. He found it on his own test account and said "which is good" (post 21).
- His guests and his newest posts give pathways. Try the game yourself for an hour and test the chat (35). Watch how your child is afterwards (6, 36). Check the lobbies and the chat settings (7). Ask your child to show you what they are buying (37). These are close to how we work.
- His October 2026 post (42) is close to our three questions: "Check chat settings", "talk about what's safe to share", and "make sure your kids know they can come to you if something feels wrong". Who can message my child, and will they tell me.
- Away from Roblox, nearly every gaming post in the second batch gives a pathway, not a ban: a console over free tablet games (48), "Skills, not rules" (47), devices out of bedrooms (52), "Who's paying for this" (49), a Nintendo DS or a book for the bus (43).
- In April 2025 his own advice was a pathway too: "set parental controls, monitor chats, and have regular conversations" (post 29).

### Where the evidence does not support him

- "Full of predators", "hunting grounds", "at scale", "no safe way". None has a rate behind it, because nobody has published one. The same gap means nobody can call it safe either.
- Every count he gives is a single case or a small count: six arrests, one kidnapping, one county. Each is real as far as we know and each is a count with nothing to compare it to.
- His test is from April 2025 and is still being retold in April 2026 with no date on it. Anything it shows describes Roblox before the 2026 changes.
- "It has not worked" rests on one reporter and comments under his videos.
- His user numbers do not agree: 40 million children, 68 million people, 111 million users, then 100 million children. His own shared source says about 34.9 million children.

### What he leaves out

- Any number for how often a child is approached. He never says there is none.

- The third question, will they tell me. His monitoring answer (Bark) watches the child. It does not build the telling. Post 19 even says "Monitoring alone won't protect them" and stops there.
