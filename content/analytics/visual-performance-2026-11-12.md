## Five lessons, each marked

1. **Measured, small sample:** the opening line moves reach far more than
   anything we can attribute to the visual. Two Australia posts, same story,
   same month: 15,186 against under 1,154, on the opening alone.
2. **Measured, one post:** a single large verified figure on a calm card
   travelled with the best post we have.
3. **Measured, small sample:** a post with no image can lead a week. The
   Manchester post carried none.
4. **Hypothesis:** documents and carousels underperform on this account. Two
   posts, both product led, so topic and format cannot be separated.
5. **Hypothesis:** video is weak here. One post, a year old, on a personal
   subject. No film has been tested on an evidence post.

## The decision framework, for every reviewed post

1. Has the copy passed QA and review? If not, no asset is made.
2. Is there one verified figure that carries the argument on its own?
   Editorial research headline card.
3. Is the story two or three verified figures in relation to each other?
   Evidence infographic.
4. Is the story a sequence, a before and after, or a mechanism? Animated
   editorial graphic, silent, with readable holds.
5. Is the post a personal one and does Justin have a real photograph? Founder
   photograph. Never a generated person.
6. None of the above, or no production time: text only. Text only is a real
   answer.
7. Across the three posts of a week, no format twice unless the evidence says
   it wins.
8. Record the choice, the reason and the file with `decideVisual` and
   `recordAsset`, so the next report can measure it.
