In 2024 it funded printed textbooks. From July 2025, under twos in preschool use analogue materials only. All of that is accurate. Here is what the viral version leaves out. Sweden's own education agency never called it a crisis. Three Swedish researchers went back through how the 2021 reading results were presented. The National Agency for Education emphasised the positives. The government called it a reading crisis. Sweden had in fact moved up the league table and declined less than most countries did.
