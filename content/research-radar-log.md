# Research radar log

One row per sweep. This exists so the repetition test in Phase 2 of
`.claude/skills/research-radar/SKILL.md` is possible: a later session can see
that a topic was looked at and rejected, rather than assuming it was missed.

Killed findings are more useful here than shipped ones. A topic we decided not
to touch, with the reason, saves the next session the whole sweep.
