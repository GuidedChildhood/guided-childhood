# Facebook, family voice, no figures in the body

Homework and chatbots. It is in most houses now, whether we have talked about it or not.

Two pieces of research came out in November. One looked across lots of studies and found something sensible: asking it to explain tends to go better than asking it to do the work. Even that is not certain, and most of the studies were of university students, so we are holding it loosely.

The other asked American parents what they had said to their teenagers about it. A fair few had said nothing at all.

If you want somewhere to start, these are the words from one of our scripts. "Show me how you ask it." Then one question asked the lazy way and the same one asked the curious way, so you can see the difference together.

It takes five minutes, and nobody needs telling off.

What would you ask it first, if you sat down together tonight?
