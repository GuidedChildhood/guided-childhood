# Research: turning Instagram followers into email and stage check sign ups

13 November 2026, for Free Saturday (`content/brand-story/free-saturday.md`).
Researched on the open web; every conversion number in this space comes from
a vendor that sells automation, so treat them as direction, not fact.

## What was found

1. **Comment a keyword, get the link by DM** is the biggest lever for an account our size. Meta allows one automated private reply per comment, within 7 days; more messages only after the parent replies or taps a button, which opens a 24 hour window. So the pattern is two steps: a short first DM with one button, then the link. Vendor data (Inrō, 798k conversations, its own words "not a controlled test"): the link in message two converted 42% against 29% in message one; a first DM under 120 characters 39% against 22%; one button 32% against 2% for three. Needs a Meta approved tool (ManyChat has a free tier).
2. **Keyword DM triggers in Stories**, with the link sticker as backup. When the parent messages first, the 24 hour window opens. Business Suite's own DM keyword replies are free but match exactly. Link sticker taps are often put at 1 to 3% of viewers, with no primary source.
3. **Build to be sent and saved.** Mosseri (January 2025): watch time, likes and sends rank highest, sends most for reach beyond followers. Socialinsider 2025: carousels top engagement (0.55%) and saves. The pages on the slides should be usable without the download.
4. **A fixed weekly slot, one bio URL, a pinned post.** No published evidence that a weekly freebie slot builds repeat opt ins. Buffer's consistency study (all channels) found accounts posting in 20 or more of 26 weeks got about five times the engagement per post. Run Free Saturday as a test with its own src tags.
5. **Facebook and broadcast channels**: low impact; local parent groups mostly ban self promotion.

Small budget, if ever: promote only the organic winner through Ads Manager (not the Boost button), about £3 to £5 a day for a week, to the landing page so the stage check step stays. Meta began testing "Reply to Keywords" on Instagram ads on 25 August 2026. No UK cost per lead for parenting printables was found; WordStream's 2025 lead ads average (US$27.66) is US and cross industry.

## Examples confirmed

- Big Life Journal: "Comment PLAN", "Comment CHEER", screen time freebies (via Particl, 2025).
- Talking Point Cards: "Comment KINDNESS" printable (via Particl).
- Smartphone Free Childhood: one viral Instagram post took its WhatsApp group from 3 to over 1,000 members in hours.
- Not verified this pass: Good Inside, Busy Toddler, The Mom Psychologist, Unmumsy Mum.

## Compliance that changed the build

- **PECR**: marketing email needs consent. The ICO says consent is unlikely to be valid as a condition of getting something, and the soft opt in covers buyers, not free downloads. So the gate now has an optional unticked box and the printable goes either way (migration 396).
- **CAP Code 3.24**: say what "free" costs next to the claim: "just your email".
- Since 5 February 2026 (DUAA 2025) PECR fines reach £17.5m or 4% of turnover.
- Meta: DM automation only through an approved API tool; instant forms need a privacy policy link and our own consent box.
