The line almost nobody quoted

"Blaming social media might distract us from the real causes of the mental health crisis in young people."

Candice Odgers, who has followed thousands of children since 2008.

2

The signal runs backwards

Distress drives the scrolling, not the other way round.

A struggling child reaches for the phone more, the way a struggling adult does. A UK study of 3,340 teenagers found exactly that, and said it probably runs both ways.

Illustrative shares, consistent with the major longitudinal work.

Why the response keeps missing

The phone is visible and rich. The real drivers sit in the dark.

Old, expensive, awkward to film. So we fight the phone, and the poverty and the empty CAMHS waiting rooms stay exactly where they were.
