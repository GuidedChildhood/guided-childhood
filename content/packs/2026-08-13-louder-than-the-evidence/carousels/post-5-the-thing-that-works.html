The new study everyone is about to misuse

5,227 children. Opening an account at 11 rather than 14 tracked with lower maths and reading.

Nature Human Behaviour, this month. Read it properly and it says something better than a ban.

2

The authors' own conclusion

1. Delay.
2. Better platform design.
3. Digital education.

Together, not a ban on its own. And the strongest thing they found was the phone bleeding into study, meals and sleep. The pattern, not the clock.

3

Not a wall

You cannot ban your way to a capable 16 year old. You grow one.

Slowly, from about age four, one stage at a time, so that when the apps arrive, and they will, they meet a child who has learned to see the machinery and steer it.

4

The belief under all of it

The real drivers are poverty, adversity, and a parent's own mental health.

Social media takes the blame because it is visible and rich. I cannot fix the real drivers from an app. But I can refuse to point at a frightened parent and call it a solution.

Louder Than The Evidence, the close

Not panic, and not a wall. Preparation, and trust, built early.

A child who trusts you is safer than a child with every parental control ever made. Connection is the protection.
