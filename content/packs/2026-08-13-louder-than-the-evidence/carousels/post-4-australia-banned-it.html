The part nobody prepares you for

The clearest early effect was not less use. It was less visibility.

Parents who did not know their child was on social media rose from 23% to 33%. You have not removed the risk. You have removed your view of it.

Odgers, harder than I would dare

"We are taking away the places they gather, because adults broke them, and it is the children we punish for it."

For an isolated child who found their only community online, a ban does not take a toy. It takes a lifeline.

The thing that actually protects

The open conversation with a parent is the first casualty.

The moment it goes underground, they stop telling you. A child who trusts you is safer than a child with every control ever made. Connection is the protection.
