## How to export and post

1. Open the file in Chrome.
2. File, Print. Destination Save as PDF. Margins None. Background graphics ON. Each slide prints
   as one page at 1080 by 1350.
3. On LinkedIn, choose Add a document, upload the PDF, give it a short title.
4. Paste the matching post text from final-posts.md as the caption, and put the sources link in
   your first comment.

Keep the caption tight, the carousel carries the argument, the caption is the hook plus a line or
two. Post 5 keeps the starter pack line as the series close.
