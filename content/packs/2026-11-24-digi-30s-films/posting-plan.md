# DiGi question films, the 30 second cut: twenty films

Built 24 November 2026. Drafts. Justin posts. These replace the 58 second versions in
`content/packs/2026-11-23-digi-question-films` and `-2` as the films to post and to advertise with;
the long ones stay as they are.

## Why a new cut

A frame by frame review against our own ad results (`content/ads/proven-rules.md`, `results-log.md`)
found the 58 second film a clear demo but a weak advert. This cut applies what it found:

1. The phone is on screen from the first frame, with the question typing into the real app.
2. How DiGi works, in four seconds: reviewed research, what worked in your house, families in the same spot and safety rules first flow into DiGi, and a plan for the child comes out. Every one of the four is a real part of the product (`digi/00-how-digi-works.md`).
3. Never a yes or no, two plain steps.
4. The exact words, opening on the first sentence of the real script card.
5. A few days later it asks how it went. A week on, the parent's own rating.
6. End card: What stage is your child? Three questions. No sign up. Then try DiGi free for four days. No card. One button. The stage check result screen makes the same offer.

Each film is 33 seconds, 1080 x 1920. Example family names only. Replies are illustrative and labelled.

## Voice and music

- The voice is Justin's own cloned voice, the clearer of his two, chosen by him on 24 November. DiGi is said the way the app says it. The words are in `videos/2026-10-09-digi-shorts/assets/vo3/lines.json`.
- A quiet original guitar bed sits under the voice. For Instagram, a version of every film with the taps and voice but no music can be made in seconds (`tools/make-ad.sh`), so a track added in the app does not clash.
