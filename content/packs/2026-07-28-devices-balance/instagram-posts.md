Caption first, strong hook line, short lines with air, a small set of hashtags at

## THREAD 1 (Believe) — You are counting the wrong thing

**Hook (image text):** It was never the hours.

**Caption:**

We have all been counting screen time.

The best research this year says the number of hours barely predicts anything.

What predicts harm is the shape of the use.

Calm when she comes off it, or wound up.
Making things, or scrolling in silence.
Meeting friends, or filling a gap.

Two kids. The same two hours. Completely different evenings.

The clock cannot tell them apart.
Only a parent paying attention can.

So watch the pattern, not the minutes.

Save this for the next Sunday the number goes red.

.

## THREAD 2 (Build) — I built a calendar, not a counter

**Hook (image text):** Everyone builds the counter. I built a calendar.

**Caption:**

Every screen time app counts your child's hours and turns them red.

I built the opposite.

A calendar that reads the school year and taps you on the shoulder before the
moments that matter.

The tables check, six weeks out.
The secondary deadline, 31 October.
The first phone, at the move to Year 7.

No test. No score. It never tells you if your child is on track, because scoring
a child just teaches them to stop telling you what they found hard.

It gives you the one thing nobody else does.
The order. And the timing.

Rules agreed before the phone are just how it works.
The same rules after feel like a punishment.

A counter tells you what already happened.
I wanted to warn you before it did.

.

## THREAD 3 (Solve) — Five good reasons to reach for a screen

**Hook (image text):** The reasons to pick it UP.

**Caption:**

We teach kids why to put the phone down.
We almost never teach them why to pick it up.

But a child who only hears no ignores you the first time a screen is good to
them. And one day it will be.

So we teach five good reasons, each with its catch.

Connection. And you can never quite log off.
Belonging. And the place you belong can turn.
Voice. And being seen is being a target.
Creativity. And the pressure to perform.
Support. And a warm stranger is still a stranger.

Both halves. Always together.
Teach only the catch and you teach fear.
Teach the good with the catch and you teach judgement.

And the offline life is not the enemy of the phone.
It is the ballast that keeps it one good thing among many.
