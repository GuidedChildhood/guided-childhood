# Image ideas for the LinkedIn posts

Built on the engagement rule: a real photo of you beats an infographic every time,
and the strongest posts weave the photo into the story rather than just attaching
it (the moon post is the template). Never a stock image or an AI diagram. Build a
bank of these and reuse them. No dashes.

## The bank to build (shoot once, reuse for months)

You at your desk mid build. You on your phone (screen visible). You outdoors with
the kids, a ball, a walk. You on the football tour with mates. You speaking or
presenting. You in the kitchen at the family table. You reading. Candid beats
posed every time.

---

## Devices and balance set

**Thread 1, It was never the hours.**
- Best: a real photo of your own weekly screen time report on your phone, held up
  to camera. The post opens by challenging that number, so showing yours is the
  hook made literal. Weave a line in: this is my number this week, and it tells you
  almost nothing.
- Alt: you and one of the kids doing something fully offline, the point of the post.

**Thread 2, I built a calendar not a counter.**
- Best: you at a wall calendar or a whiteboard with the school year mapped out,
  pointing at a date. The image is the product idea in one frame.
- Alt: you at the desk building, screen showing the calendar view.

**Thread 3, the five good reasons.**
- Best: you outdoors with the kids, a ball, the park, mid real world life. This is
  the offline ballast the post argues for, so the photo carries the balance point.
- Alt: a warm shot of a child making something (art, a video) on a screen, the good
  side held honestly.

## ACEs and social media set (handle with a calmer register)

The subject is heavier, so keep images human and plain, never a chart.

**Post 1, the wound not the screen.** A simple, honest portrait, you thoughtful in
a real setting, a cafe, a street, walking. Plain and human suits the subject.

**Post 2, loneliness is the bridge.** A genuine connection photo, you with a friend
or the football tour group. The post argues connection is the protection, so show it.

**Post 3, not how much but who.** A candid street or crowd shot, people passing, you
among them. It carries the point that harm clusters in specific people, not everyone.

**Post 4, what you do with a finding like this.** You building or working, head down.
It pairs the study with the person quietly doing something about it.

## Founder set

**Post 1, the rule DiGi can never say yes or no.** The Budapest moon photo. Done,
and it is the template for weaving image into story.

**Post 2, building the brain.** Your research behind DiGi brain image, already in use.
Keep it, it is a strong non stock visual that carries the whole three line idea.

**Post 3, the night it failed its own safety test.** You at the laptop, the eval or
a red score on screen behind you. The vulnerability of the post made visual.

**Post 4, I deleted my own social proof.** You at the desk looking at the homepage,
or a genuine before screenshot of the fake numbers you removed. Honesty on show.

**Post 5, why I am building this.** The most personal photo you are comfortable with,
you with family, or in a setting that means something. The post is the belief, so
the image should feel like a person, not a founder.

## Notes for the other channels

- **Facebook.** Same real photos, warmer and more casual is fine. The Year 6 post
  wants you looking at a calendar or the school gates.
- **Instagram.** Each caption already carries a hook image line and a carousel plan.
  Lead slide is the hook text over a real photo, then the five paired cards.
- **Mumsnet and Reddit.** No brand imagery. If a photo is used at all it is incidental,
  these rooms are text first and a marketing shot reads as selling.
- **Substack.** A single strong header photo, the moon or a real desk shot, not a
  banner graphic.
