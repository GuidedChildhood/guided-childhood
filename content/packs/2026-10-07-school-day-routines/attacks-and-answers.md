# The Wrong Villain, the school day: attacks and answers

A hostile panel (the Haidt camp, the UK ban campaigners, a methods critic, a clinician) attacked the flagship post on 8 October 2026 with sources. Thirteen attacks, ranked by damage. Each one has what is right about it, the concession that disarms it without conceding the thesis, and where the answer now lives (the body, the first comment, or a ready reply). Every panel sourced figure was then checked by a citation verifier against its primary source on 8 October 2026 (13 items: 9 confirmed, 4 corrected, 0 demoted); the corrections are applied below. The standing rules from the 0.4 percent debate (philosophy-education-is-key.md) apply: concede facts, never the thesis; represent studies whole; never soft on the platforms; do not die on a side quest; never post the number naked.

## Two corrections to our own files, from the panel

- The 2020 Nature Human Behaviour critique is Twenge, Haidt, Joiner and Campbell, not Blake.
- Rausch and Haidt's SSRN 5224958 critiques Ferguson's meta analysis of social media reduction experiments (Psychology of Popular Media, d 0.088), not the 2025 Professional Psychology correlational meta analysis that gave beta 0.061. Confirmed by the verifier on 8 October 2026 and fixed in viral post Part 9 the same day; the 2025 paper has four authors, Ferguson, Kaye, Branley Bell and Markey.
