The finding

Orben and Przybylski, Nature Human Behaviour 2019. Three of the largest adolescent datasets in the world, one analysis.

0.4%

The most of adolescent wellbeing that technology use explained. Sleep in the same children: β +0.07 to +0.25. Breakfast: β +0.12 to +0.17.

What it means

The two hardest moments of the school day are paid for the night before and at the last meal.

Same datasets, same method, same year. The regular bedtime and something to eat move more than any rule about the television.

3

To be fair

None of this makes the screen innocent.

The screen is often what moves the bedtime. Among 6,616 London pupils aged 11 and 12, the ones on a phone in the hour before sleep had 1.82 times the odds of too little sleep on a school night. In the dark, 2.13 times.

Cross sectional, so it describes the pattern rather than proves it. The pattern is enough to move the charger.

The 3.30 collapse

The meltdown is real. The label is not.

The internet calls it after school restraint collapse, and the idea under the name is self control running out like a tank. 23 labs, 2,141 adults, 2016: 0.04 against the 0.62 claimed. Feed them first, ask later.
