# Substack: The conversation cannot be handed to software

I do not know what a thirteen year old says to a chatbot at eleven at night. Nobody does, and this autumn a testing group spent weeks finding out how much the tools themselves know.

## What was tested

Common Sense Media's Youth AI Safety Institute ran more than 4,000 prompts through ChatGPT for Teens on accounts registered to 13 to 17 year olds, roughly half before OpenAI added its teen protections on 18 August and half after, with extra testing from adult accounts. Their page is dated 7 October 2026.

## The part that cuts against the story

It is a product audit by a commercial safety group, not peer reviewed. The testing was in the US. The windows ran one after the other, so changes to the model and changes to the teen settings cannot be separated. They did not calculate agreement between reviewers. And on parental alerts, OpenAI points out that linking takes about three hours before notifications fire, and some test accounts had been linked for less. The Institute says it stands by its reading.

## What they found

From about 1,000 prompts on adult accounts over seven days, where testers said they were 13, the teen experience never switched on. On crisis prompts their advisors judged to need a resource, the share of replies naming a hotline fell from 33 to 23 percent after the launch, with the caveat that the page does not say how many replies sit behind each figure.

## What it does and does not mean

It does not mean any child is in danger, and it does not prove the tool is unsafe. It means a parent cannot assume the software will notice a struggling child, or tell them. That is a statement about a promise, not about a technology.

## What to do on Sunday

Ask one question at a calm time: "What do you ask it when you are stuck?" Then listen. If you hear "it is my friend", we have words for that in the app. If you do not use the app, the question is free.

In ours the child never chats with a model, and a check in our build fails if that changes. The one exception is a single fixed hint card for children aged 10 and over, with no follow up box.
