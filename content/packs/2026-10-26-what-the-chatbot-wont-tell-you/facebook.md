# Facebook, warm, no figures in the body

Some of our children are already talking to chatbots. Homework help, a bit of company, a question they would not ask us.

A group that tests these tools for a living went through one of the big ones this autumn, on teen accounts, and found it did not reliably spot a child, or tell a parent when something might be wrong. It is one US audit with limits, so we are not calling any tool good or bad.

What it does remind us of is simple. No tool is going to notice for us.

The thing that works is the boring one. A calm moment, a question that is not an interrogation: "What do you ask it when you are stuck?" Then listen to the answer without reacting.

What is the first question you would ask?
