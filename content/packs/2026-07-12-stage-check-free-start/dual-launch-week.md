## Wednesday — the number nobody puts in the headline

Across more than 350,000 young people, screen use explained less than half of one percent of the difference in their wellbeing.

That is Orben and Przybylski, Nature Human Behaviour, 2019, Cambridge and Oxford. Candice Odgers at UC Irvine puts the wider picture at roughly 99.7 percent of the variation in adolescent depression coming from other things.

That number is not zero, and I will not pretend it is. But the harm concentrates in the children already carrying more, it does not spread evenly. Two children, same three hours, walk away completely differently.

Which is exactly why one rule for every child was never enough, and why both of the things I launched this week work by child and by age, not by blanket. Where your child actually is, and what they actually watch.
