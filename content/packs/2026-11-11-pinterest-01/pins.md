# Pinterest, first ten pins (11 November 2026)

Justin asked for pins once the site was claimed. Ten pins, 1000 by 1500
(Pinterest's 2:3), rendered by `tools/social-cards` from
`decks/pinterest-01.json`. The images are in `images/`.

Five are real free printables, three are everyday rows from the usual rows
bank (`lib/content/family-flashpoints.ts`, sourced), one is the first phone
guide and one is the stage check. Every link is a public page that loads
today, with `utm_source=pinterest` so each pin earns its own number in the
founder page's By post table.

**How to post.** In Pinterest, Create, then Pin. Upload the image, paste the
title, description, link and alt text, pick the board, then pick "Publish at
a later date". Two or three a day reads better than ten at once. The order
below is a sensible running order. No dashes anywhere, `npm run ai-tells`
checks this file.

**Boards to make first:**
- Screen time printables
- Parenting without the fight
- Getting ready for a first phone
