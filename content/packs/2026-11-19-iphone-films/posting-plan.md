## Captions, Instagram

**DiGi.** The questions that keep you up. Everyone in her class has a phone. The class group chat at midnight. Something upsetting they will not talk about. DiGi never says yes or no. It gives you a plan for their age and the words to say, then puts it on your check in and asks if it worked. What stage is your child? Three questions, no sign up. Link in bio.

**Quests.** Screen time stopped being a fight when it got a price. Family jobs pay nothing, because helping is belonging. Everything else pays stars, they ask for time on their own app, and you say yes on yours. What stage is your child? Three questions, no sign up. Link in bio.

**Scripts.** You know what you mean. Then the moment comes and the words go. Scripts are the words for the hard ones, picked for your child, one card at a time, with one thing to try tonight. What stage is your child? Three questions, no sign up. Link in bio.

**School and home.** Snap the school letter or the homework sheet, or forward the school emails. The dates land on the right day with a reminder the night before. Stuck on homework? A hint, not the answer. And the week prints for the fridge. What stage is your child? Three questions, no sign up. Link in bio.

Facebook: the same text, with guidedchildhood.com in place of "Link in bio".
