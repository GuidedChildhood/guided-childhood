## From the series plan, not made yet

These are not question films, but they belong in the same run.

- **Bedtime is protected.** No stars can buy it, and only a grown up can say yes. This is the timer film's sister, and the strongest proof of protected time.

- **Founder short: why I built it.** Needs Justin to choose one GREEN scene from `content/brand-story/founder-context.md`.
- **Proof short: where every answer comes from.** Covers the research base, the safeguarding route first when it is serious, and the approval gate.

## Rules every one of these keeps

- The question is the parent's own words, and the child's age fits every app on screen.
- DiGi's reply is illustrative and labelled. It stays under 120 words: two bold lead ins, the check in line, one real script, Tonight last. Never yes or no.
- The film ends on the stage check card. Example family only (Ava, Sam). No child's face, no real name.
