# Ad studio examples

Made 19 October 2026 to show what the ad studio skill
(`.claude/skills/ad-studio/SKILL.md`) produces. Each card takes the structure
of a competitor ad we logged (`briefings/notes/competitor-sightings.md`) and
keeps our own message. Rendered from
`tools/social-cards/decks/ad-studio-examples.json`.

Nothing here has been posted, boosted or scheduled.

| Card | Structure borrowed from | What we kept of theirs | What is ours |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| `01-one-moment.png` | Kairoo, "time's up every night" | One named moment, not a category | The words for tonight, ending at the stage check. The quote is labelled as illustrative. |
| `02-real-screen.png` | Sylva, FamilyWall, Nuet | A real screen, told as problem, move, help | The child's real page: jobs first, stars become minutes at a rate the parent sets |
| `03-the-quiz-our-way.png` | Balance Phone, "Is your child ready for a phone?" | The readiness question as the hook | We never give a verdict: a stage and a next step |

These are examples, not round two. A real round starts from the Ad Library
(Phase 1 of the skill), so the structures come from ads with days live behind
them rather than ads seen once in a feed.
