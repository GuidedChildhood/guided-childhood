# Taught beats banned, the schools pack

Five research led LinkedIn posts for Justin's personal profile, built so he can start talking about the schools platform without losing the researchers who follow him: three pure evidence posts that never name the product (a randomised trial where lessons raised knowledge but not talking to parents, the Molly Rose Foundation survey on how little the feed is taught, and the government's own timetable putting the ban eighteen months before the new curriculum), one school to home post framed as a hypothesis for teachers and researchers to argue with, and one post that names Guided Childhood for schools plainly, with every product line traced to the code. `findings.md` holds the eight findings that survived and the seven that were killed, `linkedin-posts.md` the posts with hooks, first comments and proof paths, and `chatgpt-pack.md` the Stage 2 card brief. Suggested order, kept clear of the existing 23 November week pack (the weeks of 23 and 30 November are full, 062 sits on Monday 7 December and 067 at the first slot from Wednesday 9 December) and at three a week at most: the week of 14 December, Post 1 on Monday, Post 3 on Wednesday, Post 2 on Friday (two weeks after 063's feed post); then the first week of January, Post 4 on Tuesday and Post 5 on Thursday, so the product post lands after four posts have earned it. Nothing is numbered, approved, scheduled or posted.
