# How to use this pack — Not One Ban (In Proportion series)

Built from the UC Irvine panel on children and technology, July 2026 (Prof Gillian Hayes moderating, with Katie Salen, Michael Levine, Tammy Balik of Roblox, Michael Preston and Mimi Ito). This is a single rich primary source, a recorded expert panel held in full, so it was not run through the six-lens verification pipeline. Instead every claim is attributed to the named speaker, and the two contested outside figures (the Australia ban result and the one-in-five broadband number) were checked against reporting before use and are flagged as attributed, not settled.

## The one line
A room full of people who have spent their careers on children and technology, and not one reached for a ban. It is the activity, not the device. Guide the child, hold the platform to account, and stop pretending a false birthday is a wall.

## The line that carries the pack
Not one ban between six experts. That absence is the hook for the whole series.

## Sequencing note
The newsletter is the source of truth and lands the full thesis in week one. The five LinkedIn posts each carry one thread, so spread them across the week in order. Post 5 must come last, because the ban-is-not-working landing and the platform-accountability turn only read correctly after the four calmer posts have built the case. Keep the plain thesis landing to roughly one in ten across the wider calendar so it stays a slow reveal.

## The one CTA rule
One direct ask only, to guidedchildhood.com/starter-pack, appearing in exactly two places: LinkedIn Post 4 and the newsletter close. Every other LinkedIn post uses the soft comment keyword only (ACTIVITY, EQUITY, PLAY, PROPORTION).

## Guardrails baked in
- No dashes anywhere.
- It is the activity, not the device, held throughout. The panellists are the evidence, quoted and attributed by name.
- Proportionality both ways. Calm on the ban panic across Posts 1 to 4. Firm in Post 5 on the industry's real duties, the platform accountability and the AI products courting very young children. Never blend the registers.
- Not a tech apology. The pack states plainly, in the panel's own words, that the companies have a lot to answer for. If a critic like the recent commenter tries to paint this as tech-apologist, Post 5 and the newsletter already pre-empt it.
- Connection over control. Model the conversation, never the confiscation.
- Never claim developmental outcomes. The pack claims only that a connected child brings you the problem rather than hiding it.
- DiGi is a calibrated pathway, never a treatment, never allow or deny.
- Attribute contested figures to the speaker or named research. The Australia result is early (one year, circumvention not long run wellbeing). The one in five broadband figure is an order of magnitude homework gap estimate. Say so if pushed.
- Names spelled correctly: Gillian Hayes, Katie Salen, Michael Levine, Michael Preston, Tammy Balik, Mimi Ito. Check any graphic before it goes out.
