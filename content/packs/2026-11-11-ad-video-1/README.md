# Ad video 1: mid match, Justin to camera

Written 11 November 2026. The first advert of the video round, which
`content/ads/proven-rules.md` puts ahead of more cards: every competitor
advert that has run more than 180 days is video, and the plain ones last.

Nothing here has been filmed, cut, posted or uploaded.

## The script, twenty seconds

Sixty words, about twenty one seconds at an easy pace. One clip per line.

1. They can't stop in the middle of a match.
2. You say it's time to come off. They go from fine to furious in about four
   seconds.
3. So agree it before they start. How many matches. They stop at the end of
   the last one.
4. That's one script from Guided Childhood. What stage is your child? Three
   questions, no sign up.

## The second opening, for the test

Filmed in the same sitting. These two lines replace lines 1 and 2; lines 3
and 4 are reused. The opening is the only thing that changes, so the two
adverts test the hook and nothing else.

1. It's a game. Turn it off now. Most of us have said it.
2. And they go from fine to furious, because they can't stop in the middle of
   a match.

## Where every line comes from

- Lines 1 to 3 are the product's own free script, "They cannot stop in the
  middle of a match", live at `/say/c34dec15-0836-4e59-8a60-beb42a1236f4`.
  "Fine to furious in about four seconds" is its situation line; agreeing the
  number of matches and stopping at the end of the last one is its Say this.
- "It's a game. Turn it off now" is the same script's Not this.
- The last two sentences are the hook, word for word.
- No scene from Justin's own life is used, so there is nothing to clear
  against `content/brand-story/founder-context.md`. "Most of us have said it"
  is the only line that speaks for him; cut it if it is not true of you.

## Filming, ten minutes

The read cards are `content/read-cards/2026-11-11-ad-video-1.pdf` (build:
`node tools/read-cards/build.mjs content/read-cards/2026-11-11-ad-video-1.json content/read-cards/2026-11-11-ad-video-1.pdf`).

- Phone upright, eye level, window light in front of you.
- Sit where the gaming happens. A controller on the sofa arm if there is one.
- Your own phone and no polish. The adverts that last look like this.
- One clip per card. Say card 1 twice.
- Send the clips uncut, by AirDrop or to Drive 05 New Footage Inbox.

## The cut, once the clips arrive

By `.claude/skills/talking-head-video`, with `silent-ugc` rules winning on
vertical:

- Captions throughout on a dark plate, natural skin, no grade.
- Line 3 cuts to the real script page, the same capture as card 2 of Round 1,
  with Justin's voice running under it.
- The end card is the hook, word for word, on cream with the espresso button.
- No backing track.
- Two exports, one per opening, each with its own link code
  (`v1-mid-match-a`, `v1-mid-match-b`), made on the day they are cut.

## The avatar test

Justin asked why not an avatar. The same four cards read by his own digital
twin make a third export with its own code, and the numbers decide between
the twin and the phone. Never anybody else's face.

## Gates

No person who does not exist. No number that is not ours. No outcome promised:
the script says what to agree, never that it works. No words a competitor
owns. Ends on the hook. No dashes.
