## The reply guard (read before answering any comment)

1. **Never repeat a child's details.** No names, ages with a school, places,
   or anything else a parent shared. Answer the moment, not the child.
2. **Anything that reads as a safeguarding worry gets no public answer.** That
   means a stranger contacting a child, images, self harm, an adult asking
   for secrecy, or a child at risk. Reply publicly only with: "Thank you for
   telling us. We have sent you a message." Then, in the private message,
   signpost: CEOP (ceop.police.uk) for contact or images, Childline 0800 1111
   for the child, Samaritans 116 123, and 999 in an emergency. Do not give
   advice on the situation itself.
3. **No promises about outcomes.** DiGi gives the words and the reason, never
   "this will fix it".
4. **One question can become a video** only if it is a common moment, it can be
   written without any detail from the comment, and a script exists for it.
5. Log each comment that became a video in `content/brand-story/posted-log.md`,
   so the bank of questions grows with what parents really ask.
