## The pattern

- **Fear gets the reach.** Roblox and predator stories: about 35 Roblox videos and 25M plays, about 47 predator videos and 23M plays.
- **Calm fixes get saved.** YouTube Kids setting (52.7K saves), toddler channels (68.2K saves), DNS filter.
- **The first phone and the conversation get small numbers** (3K to 28K). He barely covers "what do I say to my child". His one example ("all my friends play Roblox") answers with a different game, not a script.
- **He shows an access ladder** (watch, basic phone, locked phone, restricted iPhone) and says add more "as they show they are ready", without saying how to judge ready. **That is the stage check.**
