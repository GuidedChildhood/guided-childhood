## The five recurring questions

1. Is this app or game OK for my child? (Roblox, Minecraft, Fortnite, Snapchat, Discord, YouTube Kids.) His biggest earners after the 764 story.
2. Can a stranger reach my child through it? The fear thread.
3. How do I set the phone up, and which phone? The paid product sits here.
4. Should my child use AI? His answer: never alone.
5. How do I hold the line? Everyone else has one, meltdowns, bedrooms. His reframe content, and his lowest views.

## Where he gives a flat no or a block (our pathway answer differs)

- Texting a friend at 11: no.
- Roblox: no settings make it safe; his kids cannot have it.
- AI: never alone.
- Social media, porn, anonymous chat: no safe version at any age.
- Under 10: no internet, chat, AI or voice assistants. 10 to 14 a locked iPhone; 15 plus restrictions "reduced as responsibility is demonstrated". He never says what demonstrated responsibility looks like. **That gap is exactly the stage check.**
- Bedrooms: no internet in bedrooms. Close to our own position, not a gap.

**Overlap to know about.** He is not pure blocking: "start with nothing, add back slowly", the Gate System (paywalled), a free pass so a child can ask for help, a book called Skills, Not Rules. The difference: his gates are device settings the parent decides, on US ages. He never publishes the readiness criteria, the words to say, or the one thing to do instead.
