# READ ME, the cybertrauma founder update series

A founder voice series built on Catherine Knibbs's four 2026 reports and her doctoral research on
children aged 7 to 10, where each post opens on a fresh 2026 news story, links it to a Knibbs
finding, and shows the thing we actually built for it, with the mental health frame throughout. No
dashes. Held to the Odgers test. Source of truth: briefings/2026-08-18-cybertrauma-knibbs-series.md.

## The one thing not to get wrong

This must not read as a flip into panic after Louder Than The Evidence. The reconciliation, stated
in the briefing and carried in the posts: the average population effect is small, and the
concentrated tail is real, and both point at preparation and an askable adult, not a ban. Knibbs is
clinical evidence for the tail, and her fix is our pathway. Different question, same thesis, same
answer.

## Evidence discipline

- Attribute every Knibbs figure to her. Mechanism, not prevalence. Never a population percentage.
- Cybertrauma is a framework she coined, not a diagnosis.
- Do not stake a claim on her PhD title, lean on the twenty years of clinical practice, which is
  unimpeachable. Mirror her self styling (Dr) only if used, do not assert it.
- Verify the news numbers and any Knibbs quotes at source before print (egress blocked this session).
