# Founding Beta kit, the founding beta post, the 3 email warm list sequence, and the step by step

This is the Friday soft launch. It opens the 50 founder places to the warm list, before the public
launch on 12 September, and turns those founders into the beta and the testimonials. Justin's voice,
no dashes. Every link goes to guidedchildhood.com/starter-pack (the free three question stage check,
with the founder door behind it).

Verify /starter-pack and the founder counter work on mobile and desktop before you post anything.

---

## PART 1, THE STEP BY STEP (what to do, in order)

### Friday, soft launch day

1. **Check it works.** Open guidedchildhood.com/starter-pack on your own phone. Do the three
   question stage check as if you were a parent. Confirm the founder sign up and the £7.99 price
   show correctly, and that the 50 counter is live. If anything is broken, fix before you post.
2. **Post the Founding Beta post** on LinkedIn in your golden hour (mid morning). Put the link in the
   FIRST COMMENT, not the body (LinkedIn throttles posts with body links).
3. **Send Email 1** (the invite) to your warm email list the same morning.
4. **Be present for the first hour.** Reply to every comment and message fast. When someone says
   yes, send them straight to the link and offer to onboard them personally.
5. Optional, drop the same invite as a short Substack note so that audience sees it too.

### The weekend, onboard and listen

6. **Onboard every founder personally.** Record one 2 minute Loom or voice note showing them what
   they are looking at and the first thing to try. This is the single biggest thing that stops early
   users drifting away.
7. **Ask each founder three questions:** what did you understand immediately, what confused you,
   would you pay and what feels fair. Write down the exact phrases they use, they become your launch
   copy.
8. **Sunday, send Email 2** (why I built it, and what founders get).

### Mid next week

9. **Wednesday, send Email 3** (last call on the founding places), with the real number of how many
   of the 50 are gone.
10. **Screenshot every positive reaction,** with permission. These are your testimonials for the 12
    September public launch. Aim for 5 to 10 and one short parent story.

### The three weeks to 12 September

11. **Keep posting the research packs** (Louder, mechanism not mandate, cybertrauma, Australia ban),
    one every day or two, as your authority layer. They keep pulling fresh stage checks while the
    beta runs.
12. **Start school and Smartphone Free Childhood outreach.** Give a couple of sympathetic parents, a
    PTA, or a safeguarding lead the free stage check link and a one line blurb. Position it as the
    what next companion to the wait, never a competitor to it.
13. **Turn 2 to 3 founder reactions into posts,** here is what the first families found. Proof
    converts better than any research post.
14. **Watch the number that matters, stage checks,** not likes.

### About 12 September, the public launch

15. Run the Real World Tech Guide validation week, then the launch conversion posts (content/packs/
    2026-08-18-launch-runway/launch-viral-posts.md), now carrying the founders' testimonials.
16. Launch day is three emails, 6am it is live, 2pm a social proof update, 8pm last call.
