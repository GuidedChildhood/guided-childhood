# The daily routine, what to do each day

A dated calendar from now to the 12 September launch, plus the short habit you do EVERY day. Built on
the full playbook (03-full-playbook.md). No dashes. Soft launch this Friday 21 August, public launch
Thursday 10 September (confirmed, a weekday for LinkedIn reach). If your date moves, the shape holds,
just slide it.

Two accounts, two voices. LinkedIn is you, the founder. Instagram and Facebook is Natalia, the
family. Do not mix them.

---

## THE DAILY HABIT, every day, about 20 minutes

Do these four things every single day of the run, no matter what else is on:

1. Open guidedchildhood.co.uk/starter-pack. Glance at the stage check count and the founder counter,
   is it moving.
2. Reply to every comment and message from the day before, on LinkedIn and on the family account.
   Fast replies in the first hour of a post matter most.
3. Onboard any new founder personally, a 2 minute voice note or Loom. Do not let one sit overnight.
4. Screenshot anything kind a parent said, with a note to ask permission. These become the launch
   testimonials.

That is the engine. The posts below are the fuel.

## AFTER LAUNCH, the steady weekly rhythm

You do not keep launch pace. Settle into this:

- LinkedIn, Mon a research or story post, Wed a tool or insight post, Fri a soft ask or founder
  update. Three a week, not five. Keep the hidden thread to one post in ten. Reuse the stage check
  hook post monthly.
- Family Instagram, the four anchor days, Founder Monday (one founding story chapter a week),
  Research Wednesday, Service Friday, Happy News Saturday.
- The daily 20 minute habit continues, especially replying and onboarding.
- Once a week, look at one number, stage checks. Not likes. That is the whole game.
