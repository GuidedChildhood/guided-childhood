# The Launch Runway, one master waterfall for everything built this session

Every content pack made this session, sequenced into a single run to launch, plus the new launch
viral posts engineered for sign ups. Grounded in THE-STORY.md (the one line story, the funnel, the
launch economics). No dashes. The goal of the whole runway is one thing, stage checks at
guidedchildhood.com/starter-pack, because roughly 8,000 of them makes about 325 paying families at
our 4% benchmark (THE-STORY section 9).

## The idea of the runway

Trust first, then convert. The research packs earn the right to sell by being honest brokers, the
launch posts turn that trust into sign ups. So authority runs early and wide, conversion rises
toward launch day, and the founder cap post lands last. The hidden thread (real drivers are poverty,
ACEs, parental mental health) still lands only about one post in ten, so the run never becomes a
sales feed.

## CTA discipline across the whole run

- Research posts, soft CTA, link in the first comment, one open question.
- Real World Tech Guide, only day 7 carries the link in the body, the rest soft.
- Launch posts, product named, stage check is the ask, link in the first comment, said in the body.
- The founder cap post is the only hard scarcity push, once, closest to launch.
- The email funnel (Real World Tech Guide) captures throughout, so every stage check that leaves an
  email enters the five stage drip and is nurtured to /starter-pack without another post.

## The runway, in five phases

Cadence assumes roughly one LinkedIn post a day, Instagram mirroring in parallel, newsletters on the
weeks noted. Slide the calendar to your real launch date, the ORDER is the point, not the dates.

### Phase 1, Authority (about 2 weeks out). Earn the right to sell.
1. Louder Than The Evidence, posts 1 to 5 (the small effect, the method critique, Australia
   corrected to the eSafety source in post 4, the thing that works in post 5).
2. Louder newsletter to Substack and as a LinkedIn article mid phase.
Purpose, establish the honest broker. No product yet, only the soft CTA. This is the reputation the
conversion posts spend later.

### Phase 2, The turn (about 10 days out). The sharpest thinking.
3. Mechanism not mandate, posts 1 to 3 (the self audit, the insight, the platform stance).
4. Mechanism not mandate newsletter.
Purpose, this is the most shareable, most credible thing we have said, and it quietly makes the case
for education over prohibition, which is the case for the product. Still soft CTA.

### Phase 3, Building in public (about 1 week out). Founder plus proof.
5. Cybertrauma founder updates, posts 4 to 8 (each a finding and the thing we built for it, the
   thesis lands in post 6, the CTA post is 8).
6. Australia ban primary source post, slotted where the news is freshest.
Purpose, the reader now sees a founder who reads the evidence and builds for it. Post 8 carries the
first real /starter-pack push in the body. The camera is turning to the product.

### Phase 4, Validation (launch week minus a few days). The NYT agrees with us.
7. Real World Tech Guide, LinkedIn posts 1 to 7, one a day, Instagram mirroring in parallel.
8. Real World Tech Guide newsletter to Substack and LinkedIn article.
9. The lead magnet goes live (The Real World Tech Rulebook), capturing emails into the five email
   drip.
Purpose, third party validation right before launch. Day 7 (62% use controls, 75% rate them 3 out
of 5 or worse) is the natural pivot into the launch posts, it ends on the product.

### Phase 5, Launch conversion (launch week). Turn trust into sign ups.
10. Launch viral post 7 (62/75, so we built the opposite), the bridge straight out of the NYT week.
11. Launch viral post 1 (the Duolingo reveal, the one line story).
12. Launch viral post 3 (9 in 10 argue, policing without a plan, the pain).
13. Launch viral post 2 (the star quest, my son started earning it).
14. Launch viral post 6 (the words for 9pm, DiGi never says yes or no).
15. Launch viral post 5 (the pure stage check hook, lowest friction, workhorse).
16. Launch viral post 4 (the 50 founder places, closest to launch, the one hard scarcity push).
Purpose, product named, stage check the ask, cadence at its highest. Post 4 lands last because
scarcity only works once and only when it is real.

## After launch

- Reuse launch post 5 (the stage check hook) monthly, it is the identical funnel hook and the
  lowest friction ask we have.
- Keep one research post in the mix each week so the feed stays an honest broker, not a sales
  channel, and keep the hidden thread to one post in ten.
- Watch the number that matters, stage checks, not likes. THE-STORY section 9, about 8,000 stage
  checks is 325 payers. The runway exists to feed that one number.

## Channel spread (from THE-STORY section 9, switch on order)

Justin's LinkedIn first (a proven 73,911 impression format exists), then parent groups and the share
loop, then paid parent creators capped at £1,000 total and only after free traffic converts. The
money rule, never pay to find out if something works, only to scale something proven. Instagram runs
the Real World Tech Guide mirror and the launch posts as founder captions in parallel. Substack
carries the three newsletters.
