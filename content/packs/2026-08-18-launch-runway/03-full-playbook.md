# The full launch playbook, one timeline, every channel

Everything on one clock, from this week to the 12 September public launch and after. Three tracks
run in parallel: Justin's LinkedIn (founder voice), the family Instagram and Facebook (Natalia's
voice, the rebrand), and email. Plus the offline channels, schools, Smartphone Free Childhood, PR.
No dashes. Every link goes to the free three question stage check.

Files this playbook draws on, all in the repo:
- Founding beta post and the 3 emails: content/packs/2026-08-18-launch-runway/02-founding-beta-kit.md
- Launch conversion posts (7): content/packs/2026-08-18-launch-runway/launch-viral-posts.md
- The runway and strategy: 00-master-waterfall.md and 01-launch-strategy.md
- The Real World Tech Guide week: content/packs/2026-08-18-real-world-tech-guide/
- The research packs: Louder, mechanism-not-mandate, cybertrauma, Australia-ban
- The family rebrand: content/brand-story/rebrand-warning-carousel.md and handover-sequence.md

## The one rule that keeps it from reading salesy

Value before ask, roughly nine to one. Across the whole run you have about twenty research and story
posts and a handful of conversion posts. The offer is always the FREE three question stage check,
never buy my app, and the link sits in the first comment with an open question in the post. That
ratio is what earns the occasional ask. The only thing that reads salesy is conversion posts
back to back with nothing useful between them, or the scarcity push run too early or too often. This
playbook spaces them on purpose.

## WEEK 0, this week. Prep, then soft launch Friday.

### Before Friday (any day this week)
- [ ] Open guidedchildhood.co.uk/starter-pack on your own phone. Do the three question stage check
      as if you were a parent. Confirm the founder sign up, the £7.99 price, and the 50 counter all
      work, on mobile and desktop. Fix anything broken. This is the one non negotiable, every post
      drives here.
- [ ] Pick your real photos: one of you for the founder posts, and the family and craft photos the
      rebrand carousel needs (first cake topper, shop, a wedding, the three children now).
- [ ] Line up the 3 warm list emails in your email tool as drafts (from 02-founding-beta-kit.md).

### Friday, soft launch day (LinkedIn + email)
- [ ] Mid morning, post the Founding Beta post on LinkedIn. Link in the FIRST COMMENT, not the body.
- [ ] Same morning, send Email 1 (the invite) to your warm email list.
- [ ] Clear your first hour to reply to every comment and message fast. When someone says yes, send
      them the link and offer to onboard them personally.
- [ ] Optional, drop the same invite as a short Substack note.

### Saturday, family Instagram (Natalia's voice)
- [ ] Post the rebrand warning carousel (content/brand-story/rebrand-warning-carousel.md). This warns
      the family audience the name is changing, and starts the two week handover on the family
      account. Answer every reply personally.

Note the two accounts do different jobs. LinkedIn opens the founding beta to Justin's professional
audience. Instagram warms the 6,000 family followers for the rename. They run in parallel, same
week, different voice.

## WEEKS 1 TO 3, the run to 12 September. Beta, proof, authority, rebrand, outreach.

### The beta (the point of these three weeks)
- [ ] Onboard every founder personally with a 2 minute Loom or voice note. Biggest thing that stops
      early users drifting.
- [ ] Ask each founder three questions: what did you understand immediately, what confused you, would
      you pay and what feels fair. Write down their exact words.
- [ ] Screenshot every positive reaction, with permission. Aim for 5 to 10 and one short parent
      story. These become the launch proof.

### Email
- [ ] Sunday of week 1, send Email 2 (why I built it, what founders get).
- [ ] Wednesday of week 1, send Email 3 (last call on the founding places) with the real number gone.

### LinkedIn, the authority layer (keeps pulling stage checks while the beta runs)
- [ ] Post the research packs, one every day or two, in this order: Louder Than The Evidence (5),
      then the mechanism not mandate trio (3), then the cybertrauma founder updates (5), with the
      Australia ban post slotted where the news feels fresh. Soft CTA, link in first comment.
- [ ] Turn 2 to 3 founder reactions into here is what the first families found posts. Proof converts
      better than any research post.

### Family Instagram and Facebook, the handover sequence (Natalia's voice)
Run the canon six post sequence (content/brand-story/handover-sequence.md) over the two weeks:
- [ ] Week 1 Mon, Post 1 the heads up (the carousel already did this, so this can be the single photo
      or skipped).
- [ ] Week 1 Wed, Post 2 where it started.
- [ ] Week 1 Fri, Post 3 the Covid post. The big one. Answer every reply that week.
- [ ] Week 1 Sat, Post 4 what we did about it.
- [ ] Week 2 Mon, Post 5 the new name. Flip the handle, name and bio to The Guided Digital Childhood
      the same day, before the post. Keep Formerly Inspired by Alma in the bio for 90 days.
- [ ] Week 2 Fri, Post 6 what it actually is. The first family side ask, drives to the stage check.
- [ ] Save the sequence to a Highlight called Our Story and pin it.

### Offline and community outreach (start now, slow burn)
- [ ] Give a couple of sympathetic parents, a PTA, or a school safeguarding lead the free stage check
      link and a one line blurb. Frame it as the what next companion to the wait, never a competitor
      to Smartphone Free Childhood.
- [ ] Contribute genuinely in one or two local parent groups, disclose who you are, lead with the
      free tool, never a cold link drop.
- [ ] Draft one short PR pitch (local or education press) on the founder story or a data hook from
      the stage check. Send to a handful of named journalists, not a mass blast.

## AFTER LAUNCH, the ongoing rhythm

- [ ] LinkedIn, keep one research post in the mix each week so the feed stays an honest broker, not a
      sales channel. Reuse launch post 5 (the stage check hook) monthly, it is the lowest friction
      ask you have. Keep the hidden thread to one post in ten.
- [ ] Family Instagram, back to the normal four anchor days, Founder Monday (the story, one chapter a
      week from the founding story), Research Wednesday, Service Friday, Happy News Saturday.
- [ ] Fix retention and day zero before scaling traffic. Most churn is month one, right after the
      first charge. The product converts, the posts only bring people.
- [ ] Watch the number that matters, stage checks, not likes. About 8,000 stage checks makes roughly
      325 paying families at the 4% benchmark. That one number is the whole game.

## The numbers to hold honestly

- Launch goal, the first 50 founders (a fast warm audience sprint) and the first 1,000 free stage
  checks. Realistic in weeks to a few months with your audience.
- 1,000 paying is a 12 to 24 month objective, about 50,000 sign ups at 2 to 3% freemium conversion.
  Not a launch goal, do not judge the launch against it.
