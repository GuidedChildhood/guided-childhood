# Launch strategy, is this the road to the first users, and the corrected runway

Written 18 August 2026, answering three questions from Justin. Is the runway a good way to sell and
a good road to the first 1,000, what is the best way for THIS type of service to grow, and is Friday
this week or 12 September the right launch. Backed by three research sweeps (cold start growth for
parenting apps, UK distribution, launch sequencing), sources at the foot. No dashes.

## The short answer

- The content runway is a strong awareness engine. It is not, by itself, the whole road to the first
  users. It earns attention and points it at the stage check, which is right. It is missing three
  things that the evidence says do the actual converting: proof (testimonials), a share loop, and
  schools.
- Best way for this service to grow, in one line, win ONE channel first (your warm LinkedIn and
  Substack audience), make the free stage check the thing people share, then add Smartphone Free
  Childhood and school newsletters, then PR, then micro creators. Paid last, only once the funnel
  converts.
- Timing, do both as two stages. Soft launch a founding beta this Friday to the warm list. Hard
  public launch 12 September with the proof the beta gives you. A cold hard launch Friday spends your
  best audience on an unproven product with no testimonials, which is the single largest cheap
  conversion lever thrown away.

## Define the number before you chase it

1,000 paying users is a 12 to 24 month objective, not a launch goal. Freemium converts at about 2 to
3 percent for consumer apps, so 1,000 payers implies roughly 40,000 to 50,000 free sign ups
(RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2025 and 2026). Bootstrapped founder led apps that reached one
to three thousand paying customers took around 18 months to 2 years even when they did well (Indie
Hackers case studies, 2025).

So the honest ladder is:
- Launch goal, the first 50 founders (a fast warm audience sprint) plus the first 1,000 FREE sign ups
  (stage checks). Realistic in weeks to a few months with your audience.
- Then, ~325 paying families is £4,000 MRR, which THE-STORY section 9 already sets as the target from
  about 8,000 stage checks.
- 1,000 paying is the long game, gated by sign up volume times conversion times retention, not by
  launch tactics.

State this plainly so the plan is not judged against the wrong number.

## What the runway is missing (the three gaps)

1. **Proof.** Launching cold with zero testimonials is the biggest conversion killer. Social proof at
   launch lifts conversion by a median of about 37 percent and up to 270 percent in optimised cases
   (Genesys Growth 2026). The runway has no social proof posts. Fix, run a small beta and turn it
   into proof before the public push.
2. **A share loop.** Parenting apps grow parent to parent. 92 percent of people trust a friend's
   recommendation over any ad (Nielsen via Viral Loops 2025). The free stage check RESULT is your
   natural share unit, more than a generic refer a friend. Trigger the share at the high satisfaction
   moment, right after a parent sees their child's result, with pre written editable copy and a one
   tap share. This needs building, it is not a post.
3. **Schools and the parent movement.** One primary school newsletter reaches roughly 268 families in
   a single send (DfE census 2024 to 25). Smartphone Free Childhood is 140,000 plus parents across
   about 13,500 schools, already clustered by school in WhatsApp groups (SFC, 2026). Neither is in
   the runway. This is your highest leverage UK channel and it is free.

## The growth playbook for this service, in priority order

Backed by the research. Win them roughly in this order, do not spread thin across all at once, that
is a top failure mode (Moburst 2026, Airbridge 2025).

1. **Your warm audience first.** LinkedIn and Substack. Warm audiences convert 3 to 5 times cold
   traffic. For a bootstrapped founder this is THE cold start channel, not a nice to have. The runway
   is exactly this, so keep it, it is doing the right job. (CreatorFlow 2025, MediaIntercept 2026.)
2. **Smartphone Free Childhood plus PTA and school newsletters.** On thesis and one to many. The
   sharp move, position the product as the WHAT NEXT companion to the pledge, not a competitor to it.
   The movement says delay, it does not tell a parent what to do when the phone finally arrives. That
   is our gap to fill. Do not argue against delay, extend it. Enter by contributing in local groups
   and offering the free stage check as a resource, and give PTAs and safeguarding leads a ready made
   newsletter blurb and a QR code so sharing is zero effort. (SFC 2026, Parentkind 2025.)
3. **Founder led PR.** Local and education press first (Schools Week, TES, regional papers), then a
   national parenting desk on a data hook from the stage check ("what X thousand UK parents' phone
   readiness scores reveal"). Your Substack and LinkedIn traction is the proof of audience
   journalists want to see. (Carlos Alba Media, Startupmag UK 2026.)
4. **Nano and micro parenting creators on gifting or affiliate, not flat fees.** UK parenting micro
   creators run roughly £150 to £800 a post, but at your stage use free access plus an affiliate cut,
   track every creator with a unique code, and kill the non converters fast. The stage check is the
   call to action, never buy my app. (Influencer Marketing Hub 2026.)
5. **Mumsnet Influencer Network and value first forum participation.** The free to join influencer
   network is the realistic entry, keep the £5,000 Mumsnet ad buy for later. In all forums,
   participate as a person, disclose, lead with the free tool. Undisclosed link drops get you banned.
   (Mumsnet 2025.)
6. **Paid, last.** Only once the free to paid funnel converts on warm traffic. Paid before that burns
   money on a leaky funnel (Airbridge 2025, Moburst 2026).

Cross cutting rule for every channel, the shareable unit is always the free three question stage
check, never the paywall. It is genuinely helpful, it discloses nothing salesy, and it survives the
anti spam rules that would ban a direct product pitch.

## The conversion levers that matter more than any post

The product decides conversion, the posts only bring traffic. From RevenueCat 2025 and 2026:
- **A free trial is the single biggest lever**, a 7 day trial lifts paid conversion by 38 to 52
  percent, more than every paywall tweak combined. THE-STORY already has a four day trial, consider
  testing seven.
- **The first session decides it.** About 80 percent of trials start on day one, and month one is
  where subscription apps churn. Onboarding and day zero value matter more than acquisition. Do not
  pour traffic into a funnel that leaks in week one.
- **Pre select the annual plan** with monthly as the visible alternative, most people pick annual and
  12 month value rises.
- Show the trial on the paywall screen, trial inclusive paywalls win about 64 percent of A B tests.

The blunt version, fix retention before scaling traffic. If day three retention is weak, no launch
push helps, that is a product problem not a marketing one (Airbridge 2025).

## Timing, the two stage launch

**Friday this week, soft launch a founding beta.**
- Open the 50 founder seats to the warm list as a Founding Beta, price locked for ever, explicitly
  pre testimonial, with personal onboarding. Your engaged list fills a 50 person cohort in days.
- This is not the public launch. Its job is proof and feedback, not hype. The founders become your
  beta and your testimonials.
- Post launch post 1 (the Duolingo reveal) and a personal founding beta is open post. Begin the warm
  list email sequence.

**Weeks 1 to 3, beta plus authority plus outreach (the run to 12 September).**
- Run the founders as a beta. Collect 5 to 10 testimonials and one parent story (worried about X, the
  tool showed Y, did Z differently). Fix any day zero friction they hit.
- Run the research packs as the authority layer that keeps the audience warm and pulls fresh stage
  checks, Louder, mechanism not mandate, cybertrauma, Australia ban.
- Start school and SFC outreach with the free stage check as a resource. Start PR, local and
  education press first.

**About 12 September, hard public launch, with proof.**
- Run the Real World Tech Guide validation week (the NYT lands on our thesis), then the launch
  conversion posts.
- Launch day is three emails, 6am it is live with the link, 2pm a social proof update, 8pm last call
  (KickoffLabs 2025). Nurtured subscribers convert 2 to 3 times a cold announcement.
- Micro creators activate with tracked codes, school newsletters land, PR hits.

**Why not cold Friday.** The evidence is one directional. Your audience does not argue for launching
sooner, it makes the beta cheap and fast, so waiting three weeks costs little and buys the proof that
drives the biggest conversion gain (Teamout 2025, Genesys Growth 2026, CreatorFlow 2025).

## Corrected running order (the fix to the runway)

The content order inside the runway is sound. The STRUCTURE was wrong, it stacked all authority then
all conversion, with no beta, no proof, and no soft launch. Corrected structure:

1. Soft launch the founding beta Friday, open the 50 seats now, do not hold scarcity for the end.
2. Authority packs run DURING the beta window, not as a two week wall before any door opens.
3. Add a proof phase, beta reactions become 2 to 3 testimonial posts, the biggest current gap.
4. Add schools and SFC as a parallel outreach action, an email and a QR code, not a post.
5. Hard launch 12 September with the Real World Tech Guide week into the conversion posts.
6. The founder cap post is not last by default. Seats open Friday, and the "X of 50 gone" push fires
   when they are genuinely near, which for 50 seats could be week one. Honest scarcity means the real
   number and a real deadline, never extended (Automateed 2025). Code enforcement is what makes it
   honest, which we already have (THE-STORY section 7).

## The one page version for the fridge

- Goal for launch, first 50 founders and 1,000 free stage checks. Not 1,000 paying, that is the long
  game.
- Friday, open the founding beta to the warm list. 12 September, public launch with their
  testimonials.
- Grow by winning the warm audience first, then schools and Smartphone Free Childhood, then PR, then
  micro creators, paid last.
- Build the share loop, the stage check result is the thing parents pass to other parents.
- Fix retention and day zero before scaling traffic. The product converts, the posts only bring
  people.
