# Phone ads, round one

Made 19 October 2026 after Justin asked for ads at the quality of the
competitor ads we logged. What theirs share, and these copy: a real phone
with the real app in it, depth, and one line of the screen lifted out large.
Rendered with the ad template (`tools/social-cards/ad-template.html`) from
`tools/social-cards/decks/ad-round-phone.json`, at 2160 by 2700.

Every screen is a real capture: the ref fixtures (made up children, Teo and
Alfie) and the live stage check, in `content/ads/screens/`. Each callout is a
crop of the same capture, never redrawn.

Nothing here has been posted, boosted or scheduled.

| Card | Hook | Proof on screen |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| `01-words-for-tonight.png` | Know what to say before the argument. | A script, Say this and Not this |
| `02-jobs-first.png` | Jobs first. Then their time. | The child's five for today and My time, 40 min ready |
| `03-first-phone.png` | One day you'll hand them a phone. | The stage check itself, the page the ad sends them to |
| `04-tell-digi.png` | Tell it what went wrong today. Get one thing to do. | DiGi, Tell DiGi what is hard right now |

All four end at the hook: What stage is your child? Three questions, no sign
up. Give each its own link on `/dashboard/admin/videos` before posting, and log
it in `content/ads/results-log.md`.
