# Day 1, ready to send with clean links

Written 8 October 2026. The five drafts in hello@ carry the right words, To and
Subject. But the Gmail connector that writes drafts turns every web address it
saves into a google.com redirect, even a bare domain with no https (tested 8
October on a throwaway draft, then deleted). Those redirects stop working about
a day after the draft is saved and look like phishing to school filters, so do
not send a draft's links as they are.

For each school:

1. Open its draft in hello@ (the To and Subject are already right).
2. Select all of the body and delete it.
3. Copy the body below and paste it in. Gmail makes clean links from pasted text.
4. Send, and type SENT and the date in the sheet.

Send the trust first. Churchill is not a trust school, so it can go the same
day. Winscombe Primary, the Kings of Wessex and Fairlands go from day three,
because the trust email says the heads will hear in a few days (rule 3).
