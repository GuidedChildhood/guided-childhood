Justin, 24 November 2026: no sign ups yet, and real doubt about whether the app
is useful. The test: ten parents who are not friends or family go through the
check, and five of them talk to Justin for ten minutes. These messages find
them. Nothing here is posted; Justin posts.

Every link is tagged so PostHog shows which message brought each parent.

## The ten minute chat, five questions

1. What made you try it, and what were you hoping it would do?
2. Where did you stop, and what were you thinking at that moment?
3. Was the advice something you would actually say to your child tonight?
4. What would it need to do for you to pay for it?
5. If it vanished tomorrow, would you miss it?

Write each answer down in their words. After five chats the pattern is usually
obvious.
