## What the read found across the backlog

The same moves were in nearly every post. On its own each one is fine;
repeated, they are what makes a feed read as produced.

- An invented reflection near the top: "The number that stayed with me"
  (062), "The part I keep coming back to" (061), "The part I keep turning
  over" and "I am not sure which group I would have been in" (066).
- The same turn to the product: "I build a parent tool" (059, 061, 062) and
  "That's the part I work on" or "It's the bit I spend my days on" (060, 067).
- The same ending: a repost ask with a principle, then a closing question
  (059, 060, 061, 067).
- The same first comment: "The strongest objection is" (all six radar style posts).
- Signposting and staccato: "The headline version would be... The study does
  not say that" (059), "Same worry. Two very different answers", "I should say
  what the proposal doesn't show", "What I can tell you is" (060), "Good news.
  Half of it." (061), "The limits first." (067).

QA also forced the shared ending: it failed any post without a closing
question. From today a closing question is optional and a repost ask is never
on two posts running (`config/content-engine.json`, `linkedin`).
