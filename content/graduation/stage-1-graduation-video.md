# Stage 1 graduation video: The First Stamp (Foundation, ages 4 to 7)

Cast: DiGi and Pebble (the Stage 1 Friend). Register: simplest words, slower
pace, wonder over ceremony. Runtime 75 to 85 seconds. System rules in
graduation-films-system.md apply; montage echoes only Stage 1 lessons.

## Beats

### Beat 1. The gate (0:00 to 0:10)
The border gate in Foundation colours, morning light, the desk tiny beside
it. Pebble bounces on the spot; DiGi floats down with the stamp.
VO: "Pebble! Look who finished their very first book of learning."

### Beat 2. The journey montage (0:10 to 0:30)
Three gentle echoes:
1. A child's hand pressing a big friendly stop button, then telling a grown
   up. VO: "You learned to stop, and tell a grown up. Every single time."
2. A screen tucked under a blanket, waving goodnight. VO: "You learned how to
   say goodnight to a screen without the goodbye feeling sad."
3. A friendly cartoon face on a screen, with a question mark floating behind
   it. VO: "And you learned that behind every screen there is a someone, and
   not every someone is who they seem."

### Beat 3. The stamp (0:30 to 0:48)
The little passport slides across. DiGi lifts the stamp; Pebble covers its
eyes, peeking. One soft thunk, gold ink blooms.
VO: "Ready? Here it comes. Your very first stamp." (thunk) "There. You earned
that one, all by yourself, with your grown up beside you."

### Beat 4. What you can do now (0:48 to 1:05)
DiGi and Pebble either side of the open page, gate glowing behind.
VO: "Here is what is different about you now. Screens do not decide for you
any more. You know when to stop, who to tell, and how to say goodnight. The
next stage has bigger adventures, and Bloop is already waiting there."

### Beat 5. Hand to the end card (1:05 to 1:20)
The open passport page fills the frame and holds clean for the app overlay.
VO: "This page is yours forever. And look who came to clap."

## End card (app rendered)
Name, passport number, date, the child's earned Friends. Never in the video.
