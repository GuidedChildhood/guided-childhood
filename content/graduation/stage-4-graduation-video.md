# Stage 4 graduation video: The Fourth Stamp (Shaper, ages 13 to 15)

Cast: DiGi and Nova (the Stage 4 Friend). Register: near equal, respectful,
the biggest stage honestly named. Runtime 80 to 90 seconds. System rules
apply; the montage picks four echoes from the twenty Shaper lessons.

## Beats

### Beat 1. The gate (0:00 to 0:10)
The gate in Shaper colours, dusk light for the first time. Nova hovers
beside the desk, calm; DiGi rests a hand on the biggest passport yet.
VO: "Twenty lessons. Nobody gets through this stage by accident."

### Beat 2. The journey montage (0:10 to 0:34)
Four echoes, weightier:
1. A post held up to a window marked two years from now. VO: "You learned
   the footprint test, and you run it without being asked."
2. A message asking for a photo, and a hand turning the phone face down,
   unhurried. VO: "You learned what pressure looks like, and that telling
   stays safe even after a mistake."
3. A wall of notifications rebuilt into three quiet tiers. VO: "You took
   your notifications back, so the phone waits for you."
4. A mirror beside a feed, the child looking at the mirror. VO: "And you
   learned the honest check on your own mood, which is the bravest lesson
   in the whole book."

### Beat 3. The stamp (0:34 to 0:52)
The passport slides across. Nova steadies it. A long beat. Thunk, gold ink.
VO: "Stage four." (thunk) "The one most people never finish. On the record,
forever."

### Beat 4. What you can do now (0:52 to 1:10)
DiGi walks round the desk; Nova opens the gate.
VO: "Here is what is different about you now. You read the machine and the
message behind it. You know where the dangerous rooms are and you know the
way out of every one. One stage left, and it ends with you taking the
wheel. Cosmo is waiting."

### Beat 5. Hand to the end card (1:10 to 1:25)
The open page fills the frame, holds clean for the overlay.
VO: "This page is yours. And look who came to see it."

## End card (app rendered)
Name, passport number, date, earned Friends. Never in the video.
