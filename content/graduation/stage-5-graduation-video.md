# Stage 5 graduation video: Taking the Wheel (Independent, 16 plus)

Cast: DiGi and Cosmo (the Stage 5 Friend), and for the final shot all five
Friends together, the one film where the whole cast may appear because every
child completing Stage 5 has walked every stage. Register: quiet, proud,
adult. No confetti. Runtime 80 to 90 seconds. Hands into the readiness
review; wording bound by the social_media_law flag rules: completed the
preparation, never safe, never a verdict.

## Beats

### Beat 1. The gate (0:00 to 0:10)
The gate at dawn, all five stage colours faint in the sky. Cosmo's flame
burns steady by the desk. The stamp waits.
VO: "Twelve years of road. Most people your age got a phone. You got ready."

### Beat 2. The journey montage (0:10 to 0:32)
Four echoes:
1. Three locks turning on one door. VO: "Your accounts, your locks. Adult
   grade, chosen by you."
2. A settings screen being rebuilt by hand, defaults crossed out. VO: "You
   design your own defaults now. Nobody upstream decides for you."
3. A feed with its coin mechanism exposed. VO: "You know the money machine
   behind every feed, so it cannot pretend to be a friend."
4. Two identical faces, one real, one made, and a steady eye telling them
   apart. VO: "And you can tell the real from the made, which is the skill
   this century runs on."

### Beat 3. The stamp (0:32 to 0:50)
The final page. Every earlier stamp visible. DiGi offers Cosmo the stamp;
Cosmo offers it back; they press it together. Thunk, gold ink.
VO: "The last stamp." (thunk) "Every stage, every lesson, every
conversation. Completed."

### Beat 4. The wheel (0:50 to 1:10)
DiGi steps back from the desk entirely. The gate is fully open; the road
beyond is wide.
VO: "Here is the truth we built this whole road for. Nobody can promise you
safe, and we never will. What you have is better. Preparation, on the
record, earned. The wheel is yours now. I am not going anywhere, but I ride
in the passenger seat from here."

### Beat 5. Hand to the end card (1:10 to 1:25)
The completed passport, all five stamps, fills the frame and holds. In the
final held seconds the five Friends gather quietly at the page's edge.
VO: "This book is yours. And everyone came."

## End card (app rendered)
Name, passport number, date, the full earned set, and the line that leads
into the readiness review. Never in the video.
