# Stage 2 graduation video: The Second Stamp (Builder, ages 8 to 10)

Cast: DiGi and Bloop (the Stage 2 Friend). Register: capable, a little
grown, proud. Runtime 75 to 85 seconds. System rules apply; montage echoes
only Stage 2 lessons.

## Beats

### Beat 1. The gate (0:00 to 0:10)
The gate redressed in Builder green, the desk with the stamp. Bloop sits on
the desk swinging its feet; DiGi checks a list, pretending to be stern.
VO: "Bloop, check the list. Lessons, done. Conversations, done. Well then."

### Beat 2. The journey montage (0:10 to 0:32)
Four quick echoes:
1. A jigsaw of small clues assembling into a house with an address. VO: "You
   learned what stays private, and how small clues add up."
2. A too good to be true message with three tells lighting up on it. VO:
   "You learned to spot the trick before the trick spotted you."
3. A phone on a night stand, far from a sleeping child, stars above both.
   VO: "You learned what screens do to sleep, and made a rule that sticks."
4. A glowing feed drawn as a fairground, with the machinery visible behind
   the lights. VO: "And you saw what social media really is, behind the
   lights, before anyone asked you to join it."

### Beat 3. The stamp (0:32 to 0:50)
The passport slides across. Bloop drums the desk. Thunk, gold ink.
VO: "Stage two. Every lesson, every conversation." (thunk) "That is not a
sticker someone gave you. That is work you did, stamped."

### Beat 4. What you can do now (0:50 to 1:05)
DiGi to camera, Bloop saluting, gate opening.
VO: "Here is what is different about you now. You keep your own secrets
safe. You read messages the way a builder reads plans. The next stage is
where the group chats begin, and you are walking in with your eyes open.
Orbit is waiting."

### Beat 5. Hand to the end card (1:05 to 1:20)
The open page fills the frame and holds for the overlay.
VO: "This page is yours. And look who came to see it."

## End card (app rendered)
Name, passport number, date, earned Friends. Never in the video.
