## The caption (paste ready)

In a few weeks this account is going to change its name.

I wanted to tell you before it happened rather than after, because a lot of you have been here since the beginning and you deserve better than logging on one morning to a stranger.

Here is the short version, and I will tell it properly over the next two weeks.

Alma was born in 2015 and I could not sit still, so I started making cake toppers on a laptop in our bedroom while she slept. It became party supplies, then events, then weddings and the big birthdays, and then a shop of our own in Bristol. I loved every bit of it.

Nothing went wrong. We just had two more children, and something started happening in our house that I had no tool for. So my husband and I built one, first for our own family, at the kitchen table at nine at night.

Nothing is being deleted. Ten years of cake toppers and weddings are staying exactly where they are. The making has not stopped either. It only changed material. For ten years we made things out of card and sugar. Some of them still are. Some of them now run on a phone.

In a fortnight this becomes The Guided Digital Childhood. Same us, same kitchen. If you came here for cake toppers I hope you stay, because I think it might turn out to be more useful to you than a cake topper.

What made you follow this account in the first place. I would genuinely like to know.
