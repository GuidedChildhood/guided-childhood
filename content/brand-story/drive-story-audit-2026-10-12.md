# The Drive founder story documents, audited 12 October 2026

Justin asked for the founder and origin story documents in Drive to be reviewed
and reworded where necessary, taking account of everything learned since they
were written. Four documents, read in full against the live product,
`founder-context.md`, THE-STORY.md, the migrations that record decisions already
taken, and the research handled over the last fortnight.

`founder-story-canon.md` is now the single true version. This file records what
each document got wrong, what changed, and what is still Justin's to decide.

**The headline.** One document is actively unsafe to use and should not be filmed
from. One is close to right and needs a date and a mechanic changed. Two are
posting queues carrying a mechanic that can cost account level reach, and one
factual error that the product fixed in September.

## 1. "Your Authority, Origin Story and On-Camera Scripts", 3 June 2026

`1wImeBRcmLjXvABUUiWwEl2QUhd5K-vPSgWOyq2JtG1I`

The oldest document and the one that needs the most work. It predates the staged
pathway as the product, the voice rules, the privacy levels, and roughly four
months of evidence. Seven findings, worst first.

**The children's ages, stated as a credential in eleven places.** The document
gives three exact ages, builds three of the four scripts around saying them on
camera, puts them in all three Instagram bio options, and instructs Justin to
repeat a version "in every third video". `founder-context.md`, which Justin
supplied on 1 October, puts specific stories about the children at AMBER and
anything that could embarrass them now or later at RED, and says plainly not to
turn the children into content. Three exact ages said on camera is permanent, it
is their information rather than his, and "dad of three" carries identical
authority. **Changed: every age removed.**

**A false claim about the product.** Script 27 has him say "I see it in the data.
What children are actually searching for. What the algorithm is quietly pushing
at them." We hold no browsing data, no search history and no assessment data on
any child. THE-STORY.md records that the learning surface says so out loud and
that the code refuses to guess. This line would be read as a description of a
surveillance product we do not have and would not build. **Changed: removed, and
replaced with what Natalia and Justin actually each see.**

**A detection claim, twice.** "You built the instrument that spots it before
either of them ever sees the child" and "the pattern, shown to you, before it
becomes a problem". Both promise early detection of harm. We show a parent where
their child sits on a pathway and the next thing to do, which is smaller and
true. **Changed: the claim narrowed to what the product does.**

**Two named clinicians used as a foil.** The document opens by placing Justin
above Dr Becky Kennedy and Dr Catherine Knibbs, arguing they see the damage
afterwards while he spots it first. Both appear in the product's own source bank.
The comparison is wrong about us and unkind about them, and it would read badly
to either of them. **Changed: the authority now stands on its own.**

**Haidt grouped with Odgers, Orben and Przybylski as though they agree.** The
document lists all four as "the research I read" and draws one conclusion from
them. They are on opposite sides of this argument. `founder-context.md` says
exactly that: Haidt was the most prominent voice Justin encountered, and the
certainty of his argument is what did not match Justin's own experience. A hostile
expert catches this in one line, and it is the single easiest way to lose the
evidence literate audience the whole position depends on. **Changed: Haidt named
honestly as the voice Justin does not agree with.**

**Every CTA points at the Digital Health Report.** All three bio options and two
of the four scripts drive to the wellbeing product rather than the staged
pathway. That is a separate product on a separate domain, footer level in the
parent app, and which venture owns the page has been an open question since
August. The founder story is about the pathway. **Changed: the CTA points at
`/starter-pack`. Still Justin's to settle.**

**Smaller things that would have been caught on camera.** "Three years" of
research, a number nothing supports, against "two years ago" in the starter story
document. Script 28 has a four year old daughter watching YouTube while hook 9
has a four year old son. "Device Pathway OS" and "the OS for modern parenting",
which is not our naming and not Justin's voice. **All changed or removed.**

**Status: reworded in place in Drive, with the original content replaced rather
than annotated.** It was written to be filmed from, so leaving the wrong version
beside the right one is how the wrong one gets used.

## 2. "The starter story", 8 July 2026

`1bkaBgdyEhFAHQ2G_8-VXXeMvkbwvI-f8agSJpSBaE1g`

The strongest of the four and the base the canon was built from. Its spine holds:
the two bad answers, reading the papers instead of the headlines, capability
rather than protection, building in public, conceding the counter argument. Four
things needed changing.

- **"The pathway is nearly ready", twice.** It has been live with pricing for
  months. A story that says nearly ready in October reads as either stale or
  unfinished, and both cost trust. **Changed.**
- **"Two years ago".** Unsupported, and it contradicts the other document's three
  years. The honest anchor is the change of mind, five years ago, which
  `founder-context.md` states. **Changed.**
- **"PATHWAY in the comments to the lead magnet".** The comment for a keyword
  mechanic. `viral-post` already records that LinkedIn's 2026 authenticity update
  detects and suppresses these, with account level reach at risk on repeat use.
  **Changed to the link in the first comment.**
- **The research paragraph names only the 2023 era position.** Still correct, and
  now supported by considerably more, including a year long study that recorded
  what 197 adolescents' phones actually did and found no credible association
  either way for roughly nine in ten of them. The story is stronger with the
  newer finding in it, and stronger again with the social app result that costs
  us said first. **Changed.**

**Status: superseded by `founder-story-canon.md`.** The canon carries the long
and short versions forward. The Drive document can stay as the record of the July
version.

## 3 and 4. The two posting queues, 27 July 2026

`1LHJhIbJuBxx8NjWCV1OnvWoldRjS8E-PtT840TDcCqA` ("FINAL")
`14mm1QSYcN6BLJvXAczz0443oIsDwuhyTNjIOe8MYm8s` ("v2")

Ten alternating posts, five In Proportion on the Cunningham-Rowe and Foulkes
self diagnosis study and five founder story. Post 1 is already posted. The copy
is good and the evidence discipline in it is genuinely strong, particularly the
fifth In Proportion post, which gives the harm its full weight after four posts
arguing for calm.

The two documents are near identical. **v2 is the better one** and the only
difference that matters is post 4, "Building the brain", which v2 rewrites around
three lines, researchers in, principles on, no verdicts out, and which fixes the
researcher's name. Use v2. "FINAL" is the older file despite the name.

Three findings.

**Every In Proportion post carries a comment for a keyword mechanic.** LABEL,
REACH, HEARD, PATHWAY, PROPORTION. Four of the five are unposted. Our own
`viral-post` skill records that this is now the single biggest live mistake to
avoid, because the suppression can reach account level and lasts longer on repeat
use. Five of these in a series is repeat use. **Swap each one for the sourced
breakdown in the first comment, with one line in the body pointing at it.** The
mechanic is doing a real job, so it needs replacing rather than deleting.

**"Building the brain" reads the roll call.** The post names ten living
researchers as the findings DiGi answers from. Migration 123 deliberately took
living clinicians' names off the advice a parent reads, in Justin's own words,
for the reason that a name beside guidance inside a paid product reads as
endorsement whether it is meant that way or not. A public post at scale does the
same thing. The post works without the list: a bank of published findings, each
with its source, and the sources visible to the parent. **Recommended change, not
yet applied, because the post is Justin's and the call is his.**

**"Candace Odgers" in the FINAL version.** It is Candice. Migration 145 fixed
that spelling in the product in September. v2 already has it right, which is the
second reason to use v2.

**Also worth knowing before post 3 of the founder story runs.** It says DiGi
scored two out of seven on its own safety test and that a test now runs every
Monday. That is a true and strong post, and it is the kind of honesty the whole
brand rests on. It should say what the score is now, because the first thing an
interested reader will ask is whether it got better.

**Every post in both documents stacks a follow ask on top of the repost ask.**
`viral-post`'s 2026 correction says the in post follow ask is low value now and
can read as bait, and that the profile converts the follow. Keep it at a series
close, drop it from the rest.

**Status: not rewritten.** These are ten finished posts in Justin's voice and the
copy does not need my hand on it. What they need is the keyword mechanic swapped
and the spelling fixed, which is four small edits he can make in ten minutes, or
I can make on his word.

## What the audit changed about how we work

**Two documents said different things about the same fact, and neither was
checked.** Two years, three years, and ages that were never in the canon. That is
what a single source of truth is for, so `founder-story-canon.md` exists and
CLAUDE.md routes to `content/brand-story/` already.

**The product moved and the story did not.** The June document sells a reports
tool, names an OS, and claims data we do not hold. None of that was wrong when it
was written. It went wrong by sitting still. The Monday research radar has a
ledger for exactly this reason, and the founder story should be read against the
product once a term, on the same cadence as the curriculum review, rather than
continuously.

**A decision taken in one surface did not reach the others.** Migration 123
unnamed the clinicians inside the product in a well argued, deliberate change,
and three months later a posting queue still read the roll call in public. Worth
a line in the review standard: when a decision is about a claim rather than about
code, check the content surfaces too.
