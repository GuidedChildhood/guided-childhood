# 3 · No plan B

**Privacy: GREEN.** Names Guided Childhood: no. Proposed: Sunday 22 November.

**The scene:** `founder-context.md`, "Education and finding his ability later".
Moved to London for college to study biology with an interest in genetics.
Struggled to complete the work and dropped out. Did not immediately tell his
parents. No dramatic alternative plan. Drifted, spent a lot of time at the gym,
without knowing what direction his life should take. The school,
qualifications, university, career route seemed to work for other people but
not for him.

**Format:** text card, or a photo of him from then if one exists.

---

## Caption

> I dropped out of college in London and didn't tell my parents straight away.
>
> I'd moved there to study biology. I was interested in genetics.
>
> Then the same thing happened as at school. I struggled to get the work
> finished, and in the end I left.
>
> There was no big plan B. No moment where I suddenly knew what I wanted
> instead.
>
> I drifted for a while. I spent a lot of time at the gym, and not much time
> knowing where my life was going.
>
> School, qualifications, university, career. That route seemed to work for
> everyone around me.
>
> It didn't work for me.
>
> What changed it in the end wasn't a course. It was a job, and a boss.
>
> Did you take the straight route, or the long way round?
>
> Part four on Wednesday: the boss who backed me before I'd proved anything.
