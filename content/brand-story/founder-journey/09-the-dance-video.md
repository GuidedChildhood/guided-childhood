**The scene:** `founder-context.md`, "The origin of DiGi". His youngest was
watching a dance video. When it was time to stop, very upset. Justin reacted on
instinct and threatened to take away Saturday football. Almost immediately he
thought that was not the right response, and then: what would have been? He had
spent years researching and still did not know what to say in an ordinary
moment. He read practical parenting work, Dr Becky and others, and that helped
produce the idea behind DiGi.

**Format:** Reel with a real DiGi screen recording.
