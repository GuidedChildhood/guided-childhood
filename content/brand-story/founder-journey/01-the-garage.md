# 1 · The garage

**Privacy: GREEN.** Names Guided Childhood: no. Proposed: Sunday 15 November.

**The scene:** `founder-context.md`, "The Porsche in the garage". His dad bought
a damaged Porsche with a partner because the family could not afford one
outright, and rebuilt it at home. Justin, about seven, passed tools and looked
forward to seeing what had changed each day. The first drive felt enormously
exciting. His dad also stripped down, rebuilt and sprayed a guitar.

**The job of this post:** open the series and say what it is. The scene is not
turned into a metaphor for screens. It is just where it starts.

**Format:** single image. A carousel if he has two or three old photos.

---

## Caption

> When I was seven, my dad rebuilt a damaged Porsche in our garage.
>
> We could never have bought one outright. So he bought a damaged one with a
> partner, and rebuilt it at home.
>
> My job was passing tools.
>
> I used to look forward to going in and seeing what had changed since the day
> before.
>
> He could take something that looked impossibly complicated, take it apart,
> lay the pieces out and put it all back together. I thought he was close to a
> magician. He did the same with a guitar once. Stripped it, rebuilt it,
> sprayed it.
>
> Then the first drive in the finished car. I can still remember how exciting
> that was.
>
> What stayed with me was never really the car. It was watching him do it.
>
> Over the next few weeks I'm going to tell the story of how I went from a dad
> who thought screens were simply bad for kids to building something for
> families about exactly that. Ten posts. It starts here.
>
> What did you watch a parent do when you were small that you still think about?
>
> Part two on Wednesday: school, and why it never quite fitted.
