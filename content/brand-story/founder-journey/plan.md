**For Justin's personal Instagram, not the Guided Childhood account.** Ten posts,
one true scene each, in order: from a dad who thought screens were simply bad
for kids to the man building Guided Childhood. Drafts only. Nothing here is
posted, scheduled or sent anywhere.

Drafted 7 November 2026. Every scene comes from
`content/brand-story/founder-context.md` and nothing else. Nothing from the
private context is in any draft.

told the shop, the platforms copying ideas, the move to the countryside, the
screens battle at home in general, and ban or free for all. The handover
sequence told Covid and YouTube as a babysitter in Natalia's voice. So:

- **Left out:** the shop story, the Covid babysitter scene, the kids learning
  languages and crafts from screens. All told already.

  dropping out, the boss, the accounting build. Then the parts the walk and talk
  skipped: the YouTube moment and The Social Billboard, the research, the DiGi
  origin, building it with AI.
- **Touched lightly:** the morning walks appear in post 10 only as where the AI
  idea arrived, not retold as the move.

## The checks every draft went through

- One scene per post, from `founder-context.md`, nothing added. Where the source
  is silent (what the car looked like, what anyone said) the draft is silent too.
- No dialogue attributed to anyone. No emotion he did not record.
- No children's names, ages or faces. Teo is never named.
- Product claims in posts 8 to 10 each carry a proof path in the file.
- Hidden thread: post 8 is the brick (evidence more complicated than the
  headlines, waiting and preparing as different jobs). No post states the full
  thesis, none casts a platform as the villain, and the Haidt test passes:
  every post leaves the reader more capable, not more frightened.
- `npm run ai-tells -- content/brand-story/founder-journey` clean.
- Voice: the research/01 voice notes CLAUDE.md points to live in the old
  `guided-childhood-build` folder, which is not in this repo, so the voice
  keeper rules in `content-engine/SKILL.md` and `founder-story-canon.md` stood in.
