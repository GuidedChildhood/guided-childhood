# 5 · The first build

**Privacy: GREEN.** Names Guided Childhood: no. Proposed: Sunday 29 November.

**The scene:** `founder-context.md`, "Learning by building". Around 2010 he
helped build an online accounting, SaaS type service while much of the
accounting industry stayed traditional. The idea was to make company formation,
accounting and admin much easier online. He was good at understanding the
product deeply, explaining it to clients and agencies, solving problems and
generating referrals, and learned he was strongest at understanding what a
customer was struggling with and making their life easier. He also saw the
limits of growth.

**Kept GREEN:** the hard part is one line about growth, not the partnership
detail. Business failures and significant financial pressure are AMBER, so the
partnership difficulties are left out.

**Format:** text card, or a three slide carousel.

## Caption

> Around 2010 I helped build an accounting service that lived online, when most
> of that industry was still very traditional.
>
> The idea was simple. Make setting up a company, the accounts and all the admin
> much easier, without the paper chase.
>
> My part was knowing the product inside out. Explaining it to clients and
> agencies. Sorting out problems. Winning referrals.
>
> That's where I found the thing I'm best at. Working out what someone is
> actually struggling with, then building or explaining something that makes
> their life easier.
>
> I also learned that a good product doesn't grow into a big business just
> because it's good.
>
> I didn't know it then, but it was practice. Years later the struggle I wanted
> to make easier was my own, at home.
>
> What's a job you did that turned out to be training for something you didn't
> see coming?
>
> Part six on Wednesday: the YouTube recommendation that started it all.
