# 4 · The boss

**Privacy: GREEN.** Names Guided Childhood: no. Proposed: Wednesday 25 November.

**The scene:** `founder-context.md`, "Education and finding his ability later".
A manager recognised he was conscientious and capable and encouraged him
towards leadership. A female boss in particular backed him and later gave him
an excellent recommendation. Being valued made him want to become better. He
found he was good at organising people, getting work completed, understanding
customers, explaining products and seeing what clients needed. At about 32 he
became a company director, which did not seem quite real.

**Format:** single image.

---

## Caption

> I found out what I was good at from a boss, not a teacher.
>
> When I started working, a manager noticed I was conscientious and could get
> things done, and pushed me towards leading people.
>
> One boss in particular backed me. Years later she gave me a brilliant
> reference.
>
> What I remember most is what being valued did to me. It made me want to be
> better.
>
> It turned out I was good at things after all. Organising people. Getting work
> over the line. Understanding customers, and explaining a product so it made
> sense to them.
>
> At about 32 I became a company director. For someone who struggled at school
> and dropped out of college, that didn't feel quite real.
>
> Someone believing in you before you've proved anything changes what you're
> willing to try.
>
> Who backed you before you'd proved anything?
>
> Part five on Sunday: the first thing I helped build.
