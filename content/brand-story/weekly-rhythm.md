# The weekly rhythm on Instagram and Facebook

**Four anchor days, in the family voice. No dashes in any copy.**

This is the Instagram and Facebook rhythm for the account formerly called
Inspired by Alma. It is **not** the LinkedIn calendar. That already exists at
`content/packs/2026-07-08-posting-calendar/` and it is Justin's, in Justin's
voice, aimed at parents on LinkedIn and at heads and DSLs.

---

## How this sits next to the LinkedIn calendar

| | LinkedIn and Substack | Instagram and Facebook |
|---|---|---|
| Voice | Justin, first person, founder | `Natalia` and Justin, "we", the family |
| Register | Evidence led, long, dense, authority mode | Warm, short, recognition mode |
| Existing cadence | Mon research, Wed mechanism, Fri school or evidence, Sun founder, Thu Substack | Mon founder, Wed research, Fri service, Sat happy news |
| Hook style | Cold open on a precise number | Cold open on a moment a parent has lived |
| Links | Never in the body. First comment only | Instagram: bio only. **Facebook: links allowed in the body** |
| Carousel | Native PDF carousel | Instagram only. **Facebook has no organic carousel**, it takes one tall image plus long text |

**The useful overlap.** LinkedIn does founder story on Sunday, Instagram does it
on Monday. That is deliberate and it is a saving, not a clash. The same chapter
gets written once and told twice: Sunday it is Justin's angle, what he learned
building it. Monday it is the family's angle, what it was actually like. Write
them in the same sitting.

Same for research. LinkedIn Monday and Instagram Wednesday can run the same
finding two days apart, one in authority mode with the effect size, one in
recognition mode with the tonight line.

## The four days

### Monday · Founder Monday

**Job.** Why we care. One chapter of the founding story.

**Source.** `founding-story.md`, the twelve chapter table. One per Monday, in
order. Chapter 6, the Covid babysitter admission, is the big one and it does not
run until the five before it have.

**Structure.**
1. Open on the moment, not the lesson. A specific afternoon, a specific room.
2. What actually happened, told plainly, three to five short paragraphs.
3. The turn. One line that costs something to say.
4. No moral. Do not tell the reader what to take from it.
5. Close on a real question a stranger can answer from their own life.

**Image.** A real photo, every time. The first cake topper. The empty shop. A
child on a tablet with their back to the camera. Ten years of this account is a
photo archive and it is the single biggest asset in the whole plan.

**Never.** No CTA. Not one. Monday earns the right that Friday spends.

---

### Wednesday · Research Wednesday

**Job.** What is actually true. One finding, plain English, and what it means at
your house tonight.

**Register.** This is the one day that borrows from LinkedIn, but it must come
down two levels. A parent scrolling Instagram on the bus does not want an effect
size. They want to know whether to worry about the thing they read yesterday.

**Structure.**
1. The claim they have already heard, stated fairly.
2. What the research actually found.
3. The honest pivot. **Always.** Concede the strongest opposing point before
   anyone raises it. "That number is not zero."
4. **The tonight line.** One thing to do or say this evening. This is the whole
   reason the post exists and it is the thing that gets it saved.
5. Close on a question.

**Sourcing.** Every claim traces to `briefings/notes/positive-canon.md` or a
named primary source. The canon: Odgers, Orben, Przybylski, Vuorre, Ferguson,
Livingstone. Ferguson never as sole source. Haidt is not an ally. Never claim
outcomes for children, claim calmer conversations.

**The hidden thread lives here.** Wednesday is where the 1 in 10 rule is
applied. Nine Wednesdays out of ten carry one brick of the thesis: an effect
size, a proportionality point, a CAMHS number, a note on the vulnerable tail.
Roughly one in ten states it plainly, that poverty, adverse childhood
experiences and parental mental health outrank platform time in the evidence.
Read `.claude/skills/content-engine/hidden-thread.md` before drafting any
Wednesday. It is internal and it never appears as a public statement of strategy.

**Never.** No panic. No screenshot of a scary headline. The differentiation is
being the calm one.

---

### Friday · Service Friday

**Job.** What we built. One service, one real problem, one hinge.

**The rule that makes or breaks this day: open on the parent's problem, never on
the feature.** Nobody has ever wanted a digital passport. They have wanted their
eleven year old to stop asking for Snapchat every day for a fortnight.

**Structure.**
1. The problem, in the words a parent would use. Ideally our own house.
2. What we made for it. Plainly. No feature list.
3. **The hinge.** One sentence naming how this connects to one other part. This
   is the sentence that turns a feature into a system.
4. The ask, soft. Bio link on Instagram, real link in the body on Facebook.

**Format.** Carousel or a screen recording. A real screen, real data, a real
child's name blurred. Never a mockup.

**Never ship a claim we cannot point at.** Every Friday post names a service
that exists at a route today. The map at `service-post-map.md` carries the file
path for each one so it can be checked in ten seconds. A service in a plan file
is not a service.

---

### Saturday · Happy News Saturday

**Job.** Why this is not doom. Devices used well, and the ordinary good.

**Format.** Carousel, five to seven cards. The most shareable thing we make.

**What goes in it.**
- A real thing a child made, learned or did with a screen this week.
- The genuine positives, honestly: connection, identity, learning, making.
- Something from the outside world worth being cheerful about.
- A printable or a chart, the craft strand, roughly one Saturday in three.
- The AI road, framed as a road, not a threat.

**Why this day matters more than it looks.** The whole thesis is that screens
are not the villain and competence is the protection. Saturday is where that
gets proved rather than argued. Any account can say it is not panicking.
Saturday is where we show what we are for.

**Never.** Never a screenshot of a child's face who did not consent. Never a
before and after that shames a parent or a child. From the growth plan's own
never transfers list: our contrast is confusion before, calm plan after, and the
transformation is the parent's, not the child's face.

## Tuesday, Thursday and Sunday

**Empty on purpose.** They are the buffer that keeps the four alive. Four posts
a week is what survives three children and a business, and a rhythm that
survives beats a rhythm that impresses for a fortnight and dies.

If there is energy spare it goes to Stories, not grid posts, because Stories are
where the link lives and where the replies come from. Tuesday is a good reply
day, which is also what the LinkedIn daily routine does.

---

## The ask ratio

Roughly **one post in five carries a direct ask**. Friday carries it. Saturday
carries a soft one when the carousel is a printable. Monday and Wednesday never
do.

That is deliberately more generous than the LinkedIn mix of 20% direct ask,
because Instagram and Facebook are further from the buying decision and closer
to the school gate. Trust first, for longer.

---

## Standing rules for every post on this account

1. **No dashes.** Not in captions, not in carousel cards, not in Stories. Ages
   as "4 to 16", never "4-16". Restructure the sentence.
2. **UK English, UK examples.**
3. **No AI isms.** Not unlock, dive in, game changer, landscape, delve,
   revolutionary, genuinely, the truth is.
4. **Instagram: no links in captions.** Bio link only. **Facebook: links are
   fine in the body**, and Facebook posts run longer and warmer than Instagram.
5. **Recognition over data.** The Instagram register is "that is my child", not
   "here is the effect size".
6. **No emoji in the evidence posts.** Occasional and light elsewhere. Hashtags
   occasional on Instagram, never on Facebook.
7. **Never allow or deny.** Always a calibrated pathway. This is
   non negotiable 1 and it applies to a caption exactly as it applies to DiGi.
8. **No clinician's name attached to our advice.** Settled 29 July 2026. Cite
   published academic research freely, never imply a person endorses us.
9. **Every post ends on a question a stranger could answer from their own life.**
10. **Real photos beat graphics.** Ten years of archive. Use it.
