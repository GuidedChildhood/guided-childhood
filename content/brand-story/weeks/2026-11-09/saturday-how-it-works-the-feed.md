## Instagram (Reel)

**On screen, frame one:** WHY THE FEED NEVER ENDS

**Caption:**

> A feed is a recipe, not magic. You watch, it learns, it serves more.
>
> 1. Every video finished, skipped, paused or watched again becomes a clue.
> 2. The feed scores what to show next from those clues.
> 3. It serves the best guess, and watches what happens.
> 4. Then it goes round again, with more clues than last time.
> 5. That loop is why it never runs out: there is always another guess.
>
> Children can learn this, and they like learning it. In our lesson they play
> the feed on paper, scoring videos the way it does, and work out for
> themselves why it never stops.
>
> Five minutes of any feed, side by side, counting how many videos arrived
> without anyone choosing them, teaches more than a talk about screen time.
>
> What do you think your own feed has learned about you?
>
