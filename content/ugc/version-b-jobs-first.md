# Version B: jobs first

**Length** 45 seconds, three clips of fifteen.
**Format** 9:16, 1080p, captions on, hook plate top and subtitles bottom.
**On camera** The same mum as Version A, same kitchen, same top. Continuity
matters more than production value. If Version A used a real person, this must
use the same person.
**Purpose** Conversion. This is the one we put money behind. It shows the loop
working end to end: she asks, the app holds, it lands on the parent phone, one
line goes back, the job gets done, the timer runs.

**Hook plate (burned top, first two seconds)**
> Same morning. She asked the phone first.

The child in this one is about thirteen and it is her own iPhone. That is the
point of the version. It is not a lock on a young child's tablet, it is a
teenager's own device asking before it opens.

## Notes for the render

- The screens are the hero. Cards go large, about three quarters of the width,
  centred slightly high, roughly one and a half seconds each, with a clean face
  gap of half a second between them. Nothing over the hook, nothing over the
  final line.
- Six cards across three clips is the right density. More than that and it stops
  reading as a person talking and starts reading as a product tour.
- Do not animate the screenshots and do not fake a scroll. Still cards only.
- British accent, same as Version A.
