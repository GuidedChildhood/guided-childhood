# Version A: the morning battle

**Length** 30 seconds, two clips of fifteen.
**Format** 9:16, 1080p, captions on, hook plate top and subtitles bottom.
**On camera** One mum, talking to camera the whole way through, phone held in
one hand, kitchen behind her. Real person if we have one.
**Purpose** Reach. This is the video that gets sent between parents. It sells
nothing. It ends on the line that makes them want part two.

**Hook plate (burned top, first two seconds)**
> She is not awake. She is online.

---

## Clip 1 (0 to 15 seconds)

**Spoken, 38 words**

> Ten to seven in the morning. She is not awake, she is online. Breakfast is
> going cold on the table. I have said her name four times. Four. And the school
> run has not even started yet.

**Delivery** Flat, tired, faintly amused at herself. Not performing outrage.
This is a woman describing a Tuesday.

**Visual** Continuous single shot. Mum to camera, head centred, no headroom,
kitchen soft behind her. She is holding a mug she never drinks from.

**Cutaways over the face**

| At the words | What appears | Source |
|---|---|---|
| "she is online" | `[REAL]` Close on a phone screen glow on a young face, shot from the side, eyes down. Face out of focus or out of frame. | Phone, ten seconds, your kitchen |
| "breakfast is going cold" | `[REAL]` Static shot of a full bowl of cereal going soft next to an empty chair. | Phone, five seconds |

---

## Clip 2 (15 to 30 seconds)

**Spoken, 39 words**

> And I am the bad guy. Every single time. Because the phone never tells her to
> stop, so I have to. I take it off her, she hates me for ten minutes, and we do
> the whole thing again tomorrow.

**Delivery** Slows on "every single time". The last sentence is almost a shrug.

**Visual** Same continuous shot, same mum, no cut. She sets the mug down at
"I take it off her".

**Cutaways over the face**

| At the words | What appears | Source |
|---|---|---|
| "I take it off her" | `[REAL]` A hand reaching in and lifting a phone out of another hand. Shot from behind, no faces. | Phone, five seconds |
| "the whole thing again tomorrow" | `[REAL]` Same bowl, same chair, morning light a shade different. | Phone, five seconds |

---

## Close

**Final spoken line, in the last two seconds, over black or over the mum
holding still**

> That was every morning in this house. Until the phone started doing the
> asking.

> Part two: the same morning, with Guided Childhood.

## Notes for the render

- Never name the product inside a clip prompt. The model will invent a fake one
  and bake it into the frame. The product is named in the caption and the end
  card only.
- No app screens in Version A. This one is deliberately screen free apart from
  the glow. The screens are what makes part two feel like relief.
- American accent is the workflow default. Override it. This is a British
  household and the accent has to match the audience we sell to.
