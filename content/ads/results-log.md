(`.claude/skills/ad-studio/SKILL.md`) reads this before each round, so a
loser is never remade without a reason and a winner is built on.

(`/dashboard/admin/videos`), never likes or views. Give each post seven days.
A verdict needs at least 300 reached; below that it is too early.
