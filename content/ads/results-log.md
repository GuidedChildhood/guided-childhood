(`.claude/skills/ad-studio/SKILL.md`) reads this before each round, so a
loser is never remade without a reason and a winner is built on.

Judge on stage checks begun and signups, from the founder links page
(`/dashboard/admin/videos`), never likes or views. Give each post seven days.
A verdict needs at least 300 reached; below that it is too early.

## What we learned

- **Round 1, read 14 November (2.3 days, £10.64):** 9,210 reached, 216 clicks, 136 page loads, **0 stage checks begun**. Left out won the clicks by far (117 clicks, 89 page loads, from 2,679 reached); earned time was weakest (5 clicks). The adverts worked and the page lost everyone at question one, which asked for the child's name and birth date first. Campaign paused; question one made one tap (`plans/2026-11-14-stage-check-one-tap.md`). Our own "opened" count (about 1,370) is inflated by Facebook's link checkers: judge page loads on Meta's landing page views.
- **Round 1, read 16 November (live 12 to 16 November with a pause on 14, £19.96):** 16,542 reached, 455 clicks. Left out is the winner: 8 stage checks begun from 6,438 reached, 1.2 per thousand at £1.78 a check, and all 8 came after the one tap question one went live on 14 November, so the fix worked. The other five are retired with none: their clicks were a fifth of left out's or less. Most of their reach came before the fix, so the verdict rests on that click gap as much as on the zero. The 8 checks gave no signups yet, so the next leak is after the check, not before it. Round 2 is left out three times with only the school year changed (proven rule 10), against left out itself as the control.
