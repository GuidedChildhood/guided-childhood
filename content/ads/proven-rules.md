# What the proven adverts share

Written 11 November 2026 from `content/ads/swipe/ads.json`: 21 adverts read by
hand in the Meta Ad Library, from six companies that sell to our parent.
Eight had run 90 days or more, which is the best public sign an advert pays.

This is the theory we work to, and every rule below points at adverts that
somebody has kept paying for. A rule with nothing behind it is not in here,
however often it is repeated in marketing books. Days live is a clue, never
proof, and none of these numbers is ours. Our own results
(`content/ads/results-log.md`) overrule this file the day we have them.

## The goal

The best performing adverts we can make, judged on one number: **stage checks
begun per thousand people reached**, and once money is spent, **the cost of
one stage check begun**. Never likes, never views. Each round borrows the
structures below, says our own thing in them, passes the gate, is tested, and
the winner is what gets the budget.

## The rules

1. **Video outlasts everything.** All four adverts live more than 180 days
   are video (Qustodio 244, 244 and 240 days; GoHenry 187). Of the eight past
   90 days, six are video. Cards support a video; they do not replace one.
2. **Open on one named moment, in the family's own words.** "Nobody's gonna
   know!" and a line about texting when they get there (Qustodio, 244 days),
   a forgotten bus fare (GoHenry, 63). Never a category such as screen time.
3. **Show the moment in a real home before you show the product.** A door, a
   hallway, a kitchen worktop. The product arrives second. Ours: the
   controller on the sofa, then the script.
4. **A real person says it to the camera.** A creator (GoHenry, 187 days), the
   founder (Good Inside, 50), a dad filming himself (FamilyWall, 48). Shot on
   a phone, not lit. Ours is Justin.
5. **Tell it as something already on their phone.** A message thread, a
   permission box with two buttons (GoHenry, 63 days, two adverts). Ours are
   real screens, which theirs are not: the script, the child's ask waiting for
   Mum or Dad.
6. **The real app in a real hand** (Qustodio, 97 days). Never a mocked screen.
7. **One frame, many lines.** Almost every long runner is in several
   versions: the frame holds and the hook changes. That is what Round 1 does.
8. **A small ask on the button.** "Tap to try it free", "Take the quiz". Ours
   is smaller than any of them: three questions, no sign up.
9. **The quiz hook is being paid for.** "Is your child ready for a phone?" has
   run 75 days for Balance Phone. Ours stands, and differs where it matters:
   we name the stage and the next step, never ready or not ready.

## Not proven

- **Generated families** (Kairoo's whole campaign, 5 to 11 days old). It may
  work. Nobody can say yet. Look again on 2 December.
- **Polish.** Nothing past 180 days is polished.

## What we take and what we leave

We take the structure: where the moment sits, what the picture is of, how
small the ask is. We leave their pictures, their films and their sentences,
which are theirs, and the things that work against us even when they pay:
fear on the button, a child's face, "safe", "protect", tracking, and a person
who does not exist.
