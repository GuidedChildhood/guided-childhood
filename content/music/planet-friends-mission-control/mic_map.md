# Mic map: who raps which line

The real cast, from `digi-squad/README.md`. Five Planet Friends, so the four
placeholder parts are redistributed: every friend gets four or five solo lines
plus a share of the mid line passes, and each line goes to the friend whose
stage and register it fits. DiGi, the golden star, is the guide and not a
Planet Friend, so DiGi has no part.

| Character | Stage, ages | Register | Voice direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pebble | 1, 4 to 7 | Bouncy | Brightest and highest voice, cheerful, bouncy rhythm. Leads the chorus calls. Never scary. |
| Bloop | 2, 8 to 10 | Playful | Fast, energetic, playful. Takes the double time line and the punchy one word endings. |
| Orbit | 3, 11 to 13 | Level | Crisp, clear, measured confidence. A one beat pause before the punchline, the way Orbit tilts the head before answering. |
| Nova | 4, 13 to 15 | Still | Deep, laid back, low register, unhurried and steady. The restraint is the style. Leads the bridge. |
| Cosmo | 5, 16+ | Still | Warm, grounded, oldest sounding voice, calm authority. Finishes each line cleanly. |
| ALL | | | Group unison chant |

These are delivery directions only. The bible is firm that each friend's voice
is generated once and recorded in its voice table, and that a voice is never
inferred from a colour. Phase 6 maps each part to the real Higgsfield voice.

Line counts: Pebble 4 plus a pass plus the chorus calls, Bloop 5 plus a pass,
Orbit 5 plus a pass, Nova 5 plus the bridge, Cosmo 4.

## Why each friend has the lines they have

- **Pebble** (explore, feelings, 4 to 7) leads the chorus, spells out TRUST, and opens the quest verse with kick a ball, climb a tree.
- **Bloop** (gaming, routines, who made it) gets the hooked like a fish double time, the screen time line and the snap it, track it punch.
- **Orbit** (checking, scams, the magnifier) gets the truth, the who designed it question, transparency, and spotting the letter in the bag.
- **Nova** (pressure, notifications, still) opens the song, takes Relationships and Sleep (the friend who moves the notification cards aside puts the phone to bed), and leads the bridge.
- **Cosmo** (readiness, 16+) gets ready or rushed, the kid with no compass, and the Passport to Sixteen couplet, because Cosmo is the friend at sixteen.
