## Description

The tablet fight on a long drive is rarely about whether. It is about when.
Twenty minutes in, someone asks, someone says not yet, and the next hour is a
negotiation on the motorway.

Are We Nearly There moves that conversation to the kitchen table, before
anyone is buckled in. You fill in the journey deal card together: the tablet
comes after the next town, or at the services, or at the halfway point. A bit
of tablet time is your child's anyway. Stars from the games add minutes, at
one star for five minutes or a rate you agree. The first half hour and the
arriving stay screen free. Then the car games fill the miles in between, and
when the agreed stop comes, the tablet comes with it.

Pebble rides along for the younger ones and Bloop for the older games. Print
it, clip it to a board, pop the pencils in a bag.

What you get:

- The journey deal card: when the tablet comes in, the minutes that are
  yours anyway, the star rate, the protected bits, and a line for everyone
  to sign
- Car bingo with pictures for ages 4 to 7: sixteen hand drawn things to spot,
  from a tractor to a dog in a car
- Car bingo, the hard card, for ages 8 to 11: twenty four things to spot on
  UK roads, with a free star in the middle
- The number plate game, with plates to fill in
- Alphabet spotting from A to Z, with a harder version for older children
- Twenty questions, with ideas to start and circles to count the questions
- Twelve kind and silly would you rather questions
- A journey map to colour: a big winding road from home to there, with a
  star and a drawing box at every stop
- Six doodle prompts for the road
- The star tally: stars from each game, the minutes they add, and when the
  tablet came in
- A how to use it page for the grown up
- Your Passport stickers for the Pebble and Bloop pages of the Guided
  Childhood Passport
- A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink friendly version. Every page
  works in black and white too

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print a fresh set for every
journey, and one for the way home. For personal use.

I am Justin, a dad who got tired of the screen time fight. If anything will
not print or open, message me and I will sort it fast.
