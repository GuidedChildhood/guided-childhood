How to use this pack

On our long drives the tablet fight was never really about whether. It was about when. Twenty minutes in, someone asks, someone says not yet, and the next hour is a negotiation at seventy miles an hour. This pack moves that conversation to the kitchen table, before anyone is buckled in. The tablet comes at a point you all agreed. Until then there are games, and every game can add minutes to the tablet time.

The one rule

The tablet comes when the deal card says it does. Nobody has to ask, and nobody has to argue.

Get it ready

Print the pages and clip them to a clipboard or a big hardback book. Put pencils, a couple of crayons and a sharpener in a bag. Print one bingo card for each child, and a fresh set for the way home.

Agree the deal first

Fill in the deal card together before you leave. Let your child write or draw the stop where the tablet comes in. Read it back, everyone signs, and it rides in the front with the snacks.

On the road

1. Start with the looking games: car bingo, the number plates, the alphabet. They keep eyes out of the window.
2. At the end of each game, colour the stars on the tally page. Stars earned stay earned, even if the journey gets grumpy later.
3. When the agreed point arrives, the tablet comes out with the minutes on the tally. No new conditions get added on the motorway.
4. Give a five minute warning before tablet time ends, then it goes back in the bag.

The star rate

One star is five minutes of screen time, the same rate as the Guided Childhood app. Want your own rate? Agree it and write it on the deal card.

The driver drives

Spotting and writing are for passengers. The driver can still guess in twenty questions. If reading in the car makes someone feel queasy, stick to the window games.

Twenty questions

One person thinks of something. Everyone else asks questions that can only be answered yes or no. Tick a circle for each one. Guess it inside twenty to win. A round played earns a star.

Our journey map

Colour a star each time you stop or pass a big place, and draw what you saw in the box. When the tablet comes in, draw a little tablet on the road at that spot. Every stop drawn earns a star.
