## Description

One wheel, 26 letters, 78 clues. Read the clue, race the alphabet, colour the letter green.

A to Z Showdown is a print and play quiz game for ages 7 to 11, and for the grown ups brave enough to join in. Every answer starts with the letter on the card. A right answer turns that letter green on the wheel, a miss turns it coral, and a pass comes back round at the end of the lap.

What you get, 9 pages:

- The letter wheel, to laminate or to print fresh for every game
- 78 clue cards in three rounds: Starter for ages 7 to 9, Challenger for ages 9 to 11 and the Family Showdown, where the grown ups play too
- Every answer printed upside down at the foot of its card, for the host only
- How to play in five steps, solo against the timer or versus a sibling
- A score sheet with a solo record and a tie breaker
- 26 blank cards to write a round of your own
- A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink friendly version
- A phone version: the rules and the wheel, so the game travels
- A thank you page with a free gift for your family

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your own family needs. For personal use only.
