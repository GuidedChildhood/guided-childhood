## Description

The Guided Times is a real newspaper for children, twelve pages long, with
something to do on every page. This is Issue 1, the Christmas edition, and
it is all about new screens under the tree.

If a tablet, a games console or a first phone is arriving this Christmas,
this is the calm way in. Your child reads the Christmas morning plan with the
Planet Friends, fills in their new screen's ID card, and you agree the house
rules together before anything is switched on.

Print it at the kitchen table, or open the page turning version on a phone,
tablet or computer and turn the pages like a real paper. No app, no account,
and it works offline.

What is inside:

- The front page, with the Planet Friends and DiGi
- A letter from the editor, news in brief, two Christmas facts and a comic strip
- The Christmas morning plan: six things to do together before you press play
- My new screen's ID card: its name, its bed, its bedtime, its first three apps
- The big question: should screens be switched off on Christmas Day? Two sides and a family vote
- The app desk: how to read an age label, five questions before you download, and Dear DiGi
- The homework helper: the help ladder, a homework spot, AI and homework, and how much homework is normal by school year
- The good sites guide: free websites for children and for grown ups
- Orbit's puzzles: real or pretend, a word search, a code to crack and a maze, with answers
- Make and do: Boxing Day bingo and a sign to make for the screens' bed
- The shop page and the family's Christmas screen promise to sign

What you get:

- A4 and US Letter in full colour
- An ink friendly version, A4 and US Letter, that doubles as a colouring paper
- A read on screen PDF, made to fit a tablet or phone
- The page turning version, with spreads on a computer and one page at a time on a phone

For ages 6 to 11, with a grown up nearby for the bits marked with a star.

This is Issue 1 only, an instant download. It is not a subscription. A new
issue arrives in the shop each month, so tap the heart on our shop to see
the next one.

Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your own family needs. For
personal use only.

How to open it: on a computer, open any file. On a phone, the PDFs open in
your Files app; on an iPhone, open the read on screen PDF in Books to turn
the pages. The zip has a short guide inside.

I am Justin, a dad who got tired of the screen time fight. If anything will
not print or open, message me and I will sort it fast.
