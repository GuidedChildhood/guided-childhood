THE GUIDED TIMES: HOW TO READ IT ON A SCREEN

Inside this zip is {{FILE}}. It is the whole newspaper in one file. Open it and turn the pages like a real paper. It works with no internet and no account.

ON A COMPUTER
Double click the file. It opens in your web browser. Turn the pages with the arrow keys, the buttons, or by clicking the edge of a page.

ON AN ANDROID PHONE OR TABLET
Open your Files app, go to Downloads, tap the zip to unpack it, then tap the file. Choose Chrome if it asks. Swipe to turn the page, double tap to zoom in.

ON AN IPHONE OR IPAD
Open the Files app, go to Downloads, tap the zip to unpack it, then tap the file. The iPhone preview shows the pages in a strip you swipe left and right. For page turning on an iPhone, open the Read On Screen PDF in the Books app instead: tap the PDF, tap Share, then Books.

TO PRINT
Use the A4 or US Letter PDF, or the ink friendly zip if your cartridge is low.
