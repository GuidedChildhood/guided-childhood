## Description

Most first phones arrive in a rush. A birthday, a new school, a box opened on
the sofa and handed straight over, and the rules turn up later, one argument
at a time. This kit turns that round. The phone arrives with a plan, and the
plan is made together.

The First Phone Kit is a print at home set for families with a child aged 9
to 13. Orbit, the Planet Friend for the first phone years, walks you through
four steps in order: an honest readiness check, a phone deal your child writes
in their own words, one setup night side by side, and a first month of small
weekly practice.

What you get:

- How to use the kit: the four steps in order, and the one promise to say out
  loud before the box is opened
- The readiness check, your child's side: eight honest lines, each answered
  ready, nearly or not yet, each with the thing to practise next
- The readiness check, your side: the same three answers for the grown up,
  and how to read both pages together. No pass mark and no date, only ever the
  next step
- My phone deal: your child writes it with big writing space. What the phone
  is for, where it sleeps at night, what they do if something worries them,
  who they can call, what they do when they want more time, and one rule of
  their own
- Our promises to you: the grown up's half, signatures, and three review dates
- The setup night checklist: the codes, the bedtime lock, time limits, app
  approvals, age ratings, contacts, location agreed out loud, the charger's
  new home, the weekly report. Written for iPhone or Android without relying
  on menus that move with every update
- The first 30 days: one small thing to practise each week, a tick box for
  every day, and three weekend check in questions
- Day 30, the first review: keep, loosen, practise
- The words for the first wobble: what to say when it is five more minutes,
  when a group chat goes wrong, when a new app is asked for, and when your
  child asks whether telling you will cost them the phone
- Your Passport sticker for the Orbit page of the Guided Childhood Passport
- A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink friendly version
- A phone version: the setup night checklist and the week by week plan, so you
  have them on your own phone

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your
own family needs. For personal use.

It works whether the first phone is a smartphone or one that only does calls
and texts. It is a parenting tool for planning and practising together. It
does not promise any particular outcome for any child.
