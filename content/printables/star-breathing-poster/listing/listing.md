## Description

A finger on the star. Breathe in up one side, hold at the point, breathe out down the other.

The Star Breathing Poster is a print at home poster for ages 4 to 9, with a sign for the calm corner it lives in. Stick it at your child's finger height and practise it once on a calm day, like a game, so it is familiar when a feeling is big. It works at home and in a classroom.

What you get, 3 pages:

- The star breathing poster: a star to trace, with breathe in, hold and breathe out marked on it, and five stars to touch for five rounds
- The calm corner sign: My Calm Corner, all feelings welcome here
- A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink friendly version
- A thank you page with a free gift for your family

These pages come from The Calm Down Corner Kit, which is in this shop too. If you would like the whole set, choose that listing and you will not need this one.

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your own family or your own class needs. For personal and single classroom use only.
