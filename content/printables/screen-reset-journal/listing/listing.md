## Description

Thirty days, one small change a day, ten minutes every evening, together.

The 30 Day Screen Reset Journal is a print at home journal for the whole
family. Children 8 and up write alongside a grown up, and younger brothers and
sisters draw along. Every day has one small, doable change: phones sleep
outside the bedrooms for a night, a meal with no screens, a walk with phones in
pockets, notifications off for one app, a screen free first hour, a board game
after tea, the grown ups putting their phones in a drawer for an hour. The
grown ups do every change too.

Each evening you tick the change, everyone circles one of five faces for how
the day felt, and you write one line about what you noticed. On day thirty you
sit down together, look back through the faces, and draw the line: what stays,
what goes, and what you try next month. It is a reset, not a ban, and the
family is the one choosing.

What you get:

- A cover and a how it works page: ten minutes every evening, your start
  date, your reset team, and a five point family deal
- Where we start: a no judgement baseline page for your screen days now, your
  flash points, what you would like more of, and day zero faces
- The five faces key: really tough, hard going, up and down, getting there,
  going great, the same five faces the Guided Childhood app uses for its
  daily check in
- Thirty days on ten pages, three days to a page: the day's change, a done
  tick, five faces to circle for up to four people, and a line for what you
  noticed
- Thirty distinct changes in a sensible order, from small starts in week one
  to the bigger ones in week four
- The line we draw: what stays, what goes, what we try next month, day thirty
  faces and a place for everyone to sign
- Your Passport stickers for the Bloop and Nova pages of the Guided Childhood
  Passport
- A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink friendly version

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your
own family needs, and print it again next January. Printed double sided and
stapled, it makes a little booklet for the kitchen table. For personal use.

It is a family activity for trying small changes together and noticing how
the days feel. It is not a treatment for anything.
