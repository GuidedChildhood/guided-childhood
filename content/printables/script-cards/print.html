Say This
The words for every screen moment

Fifty cards, one for each of the hard screen and tech moments of a childhood, from the first tablet to the last rule. The moment is on the front, in your child's words or as it happens. The words to say are on the back, with one line on why they work. None of them is a yes or a no. Every card is a next step you can take tonight.

Print

Pages 2 to 31 are the cards. Print them double sided and choose flip on long edge. Card or thick paper, 200 to 300gsm, feels like a real deck. Try pages 2 and 3 first to check the backs line up.

No duplex?

Print every page single sided. Cut out each front and its back, then glue them together back to back. Page 3 is the back of page 2, page 5 the back of page 4, and so on.

Cut

Cut along the dashed lines. The colour runs a little past each line, so a small wobble never shows. Round the corners with scissors if you like.

Five levels, one for each stage

Start at your child's age. Read the level before it too: the moments do not wait for birthdays.

60

cards in all: a cover, how to use, five level cards, the fifty moments, a card about the app and two blank cards for a moment of your own.
