## Description

Screen time rules are the easy part. The moment is the hard part: the
tablet goes off and the tears start, or your eleven year old asks if telling
you will cost them their phone, and you have about two seconds to find the
right words.

Say This is a deck of 50 cards, one for each of those moments, from age 4 to
16. The moment is on the front, often in your child's own words. What to say
is on the back, with one line on why it works. None of the cards is a yes or
a no. Each one is the next step, in words you can say tonight.

The cards come in five colour coded levels, one for each stage of
childhood: Pebble (4 to 7), Bloop (8 to 10), Orbit (11 to 12), Nova (13 to
15) and Cosmo (16 and up). They cover the timer going off, the first phone
ask, "everyone else has it", bedtime and the bedroom, group chats, strangers
in games, money in games, AI, and the moments when something goes wrong
online.

What you get:

- 60 cards: a cover card, how to use, five level cards, the 50 moments, a
  card about the app and two blank cards for a moment of your own
- Fronts and backs that line up when you print double sided, with dashed
  cut lines and colour that runs past the line so a small wobble never shows
- A start here page: how to print, cut and use the deck
- Five Passport stickers, one for each stage page of the Guided Childhood
  Passport
- A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink friendly version
- A phone version: the first card of each level as a phone screen

Every script comes from the Guided Childhood app, where there are over 300
of them, along with the lessons and the stage check.

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your
own family needs. For personal use only.

I am Justin, a dad who got tired of the screen time fight. If anything will
not print or open, message me and I will sort it fast.
