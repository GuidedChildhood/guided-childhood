## Description

Homework written down the moment it is set, ticked when it is done, then screens with no nagging.

My School Year Planner is a print at home planner for the UK school year, September 2026 to August 2027. The months are dated and start on a Monday. It is made for a child to fill in themselves, with a grown up nearby for the first week.

What you get, 22 pages:

- 12 dated monthly calendars, September 2026 to August 2027, each with a box for the month's big things
- My timetable, to copy once and photograph for the fridge
- Term dates 2026 to 2027, to fill in from your school's website
- My homework this week: subject, task, due day and a tick. Print one for every week
- A reading log with a star rating for every book
- An exam and revision planner, and a page for your subjects
- Homework first, then screens: the deal in three steps
- A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink friendly version
- A phone version: three screens for the homework week and the reading log
- A thank you page with a free gift for your family

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your own family needs. For personal use only.

I am Justin, a dad who got tired of the screen time fight. If anything will not print or open, message me and I will sort it fast.
