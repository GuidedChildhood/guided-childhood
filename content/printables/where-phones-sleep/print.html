Two minutes before you print

One shelf in the kitchen. Every device sleeps there. Yours goes first.

A bedtime phone battle is often really about where the phone goes. So take the question away. Pick one spot outside the bedrooms, put the chargers there, and make it the place every device in the house sleeps, the grown ups' phones included. This pack gives that spot its signs and puts the habit on the fridge, where everyone can see it.

Set it up tonight
Ages 4 to 7 · Pebble

Start before there is a phone. The tablet and the games controller can sleep on the shelf too. Make it part of bedtime: carry them down together and say goodnight to them.

Ages 8 to 10 · Bloop

Decide it together. Ask, "Where should our devices sleep?" Let your child choose the spot and help set the docking times, then write them on the charging line.

Why out of the bedroom?

On sleep, the UK Chief Medical Officers' advice for parents and carers (February 2019) says: "Leave phones outside the bedroom when it is bedtime." We have kept to that advice and we claim nothing stronger. This pack does not promise better sleep or behaviour. It gives one family habit a home in your kitchen, where everyone can see it.

UK Chief Medical Officers' advice for parents and carers on children and young people's screen and social media use, published with their commentary on a systematic map of reviews, Department of Health and Social Care, 7 February 2019, gov.uk. This pack is not endorsed by the UK Chief Medical Officers or the UK Government.

Print the signs and labels on card if you have it. Laminate the charging line and tonight's screen plan, or slip them into a plastic sleeve, and write on them with a dry wipe pen, so they last all year.
