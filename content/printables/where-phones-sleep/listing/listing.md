## Description

Every device in the house sleeps in the kitchen. Grown ups' phones too.

Where Phones Sleep is a print at home pack that turns one shelf near a
socket into your family's charging station, and puts the habit on the
fridge where everyone can see it. Pick the spot, set a docking time for
each person, put your own phone there first, and let the signs do the
reminding.

Phones out of the bedroom at bedtime is part of the UK Chief Medical
Officers' 2019 advice for parents and carers. This pack gives that habit a
home in your kitchen, where everyone can see it. Not endorsed by the UK
Chief Medical Officers or the UK Government.

What you get:

- A two minute parent guide: setting it up tonight, ages 4 to 7 and 8 to
  10, and what to say when a night slips
- Devices Sleep Here, the station poster, with a "Everyone docks by" line to
  fill in
- A stand up sign for the chargers: "Phones charge in the kitchen, not the
  bedroom"
- The bedtime charging line for the fridge: a docking time and a wake up time
  for each person, grown ups at the top, and a tick for every night
- 12 charging spot labels with the Planet Friends and DiGi, plus 12 matching
  cable flags so nobody takes the wrong charger
- Tonight's screen plan, wipe clean: who, what, stars (one star is five
  minutes), when it ends, and what you do next
- Phones in the basket: a screen free meal times sign, a label for the
  basket and two table tents
- Our family tech rules on one page: six rules to keep or cross out, room
  for your own, and a line for everyone's name
- Your Passport stickers, for the Pebble page and the Bloop page of the
  Guided Childhood Passport
- A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink friendly version
- A phone version: the charging line and tonight's plan, to send to the
  family chat

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your
own family needs. For personal use only.

I am Justin, a dad who got tired of the screen time fight. In our house my
phone docks first, so the rule is for everyone. If anything will not print or open, message me and I will sort it
fast.
