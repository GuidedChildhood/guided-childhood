## Description

Twenty four doors, one a day from 1 December, and behind each one something
small to do together away from a screen.

The Christmas Screen Free Countdown is a print at home advent for ages 4 to
11. Pin the poster up, cut out the cards, and each day your child finds the
door, you do the card together, and the day's sticker goes on. Most days take
ten to twenty minutes and use what is already in your house: a paper chain,
a blanket den, a kitchen disco, a card for a neighbour who lives on their own,
torch stories, a sock snowball fight. Nothing to buy.

Nothing gets switched off either. December evenings are long and screens fill
a lot of the gaps; the countdown just puts one small thing in front of them.

The five Planet Friends, Pebble, Bloop, Orbit, Nova and Cosmo, take a day
each in turn, and DiGi the golden star sits on top of the tree.

What you get:

- The countdown poster: a tree of 24 numbered doors with DiGi on top, each
  door with a circle for its sticker
- 24 activity cards, six to a page, each with its date, whose day it is, what
  to do and what you need
- A sticker sheet: 24 day stickers with the day's Planet Friend, plus 6 spares
- The 24 days at a glance, a planner for the grown up, so biscuit day does not
  catch you short
- A how it works page: one door a day, ten to twenty minutes, swap any day
  that does not fit
- Our favourite days, to fill in together on Christmas Eve: the three best
  days and the one you will keep doing in January
- Your Passport stickers for the Pebble and Bloop pages of the Guided
  Childhood Passport
- A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink friendly version

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your
own family needs: a sticker sheet each if you have more than one child. For
personal use.

I am Justin, a dad who got tired of the screen time fight. If anything will
not print or open, message me and I will sort it fast.
