How the countdown works

Twenty four doors, one a day from 1 December. Behind each door is one small thing to do together away from a screen. Most take ten to twenty minutes and use what is already in the house. There is nothing to buy.

1 · Pin up the poster

At your child's eye height: the fridge, a door, the bottom of the stairs. Write your family name on it.

2 · Cut out the cards

Keep them in an envelope or a mug by the poster. Glance at the planner on page 3 each Sunday so nothing catches you out.

3 · Open a door a day

Your child finds today's number on the tree. Read the card out and do it together. Ten to twenty minutes is plenty.

4 · Stick the sticker

The matching sticker covers the door. Watching the tree fill up is half the fun.

Swap any day that does not fit

Late home, someone poorly, a party? Swap the card with a later one, do two tomorrow, or skip it. A missed door just gets its sticker late.

What it is for

December evenings are long and everyone is tired, so screens fill a lot of the gaps. Nothing here gets switched off. The countdown adds one small thing a day that you do together, so there is always something else to reach for.

Ages 4 to 11

Little ones need you for the reading and the scissors. Older ones can run the day themselves, and some will want to. More than one child? Take turns finding the door and sticking the sticker.

The five Planet Friends take a day each, in turn, and DiGi the golden star sits on top of the tree. Ask your child whose day it is before you read the card.

You will need: a printer, scissors, and sticker paper or a glue stick. In the Guided Childhood app, a child's five a day works the same way: a small mix of things to do each day, ticked off together. This countdown is that idea, wrapped up for December.

Your countdown stickers

One sticker for each door. Cut round the dashed circle and stick it on the door with the same number. Little hands can cut roughly. Nobody checks the edges.

Six spares, with DiGi Junior

For a sticker that goes missing, a swapped day, or a day so good it deserves two.

Draw your own

Swapped in an idea of your own? Draw its sticker here: a snowman, a jigsaw piece, your dog in a hat.

Printing tip: choose actual size, not fit to page, so each sticker matches its circle on the poster. More than one child? Print a sheet each and take turns on the doors.
