## Description

One shelf in the kitchen. Every device sleeps there. Yours goes first.

The Devices Sleep Here Sign is a print at home poster and a stand up sign for the place the phones and tablets charge overnight. Pick a shelf or a tray by a socket, write the docking time on the line and put your own phone on it first. It takes the bedtime question away, because the answer is on the wall.

What you get, 3 pages:

- The charging station poster: Devices sleep here, with a line for the time everyone docks by, grown ups too
- The stand up sign to cut and fold: Goodnight, phones. The bedroom is for sleep on one side, Phones charge in the kitchen, not the bedroom on the other
- A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink friendly version
- A thank you page with a free gift for your family

These pages come from Where Phones Sleep, which is in this shop too. If you would like the whole set, choose that listing and you will not need this one.

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your own family needs. For personal use only.

I am Justin, a dad who got tired of the screen time fight. If anything will not print or open, message me and I will sort it fast.
