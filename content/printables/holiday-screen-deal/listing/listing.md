## Description

Two weeks off school, and by the third morning you are bargaining over
breakfast about screens. This planner is the deal you make once, on day one,
together at the kitchen table, so the argument is already settled before
anybody is cross.

The Holiday Screen Deal is a print at home planner for ages 5 to 12, with
Bloop for company. It uses three kinds of screen time. A small core that is
your child's every day, no stars needed. Extra time earned through jobs, one
star for five minutes, up to a weekly cap. And protected times, like meals,
the hour before bed and family days, that no number of stars can buy. If your
child wants a screen in a protected time, they ask, and you answer with a yes
or a not now and what comes next.

What you get:

- How it works: the ten minute setup for day one, the three kinds of time,
  and where to start the numbers for each age
- Our holiday deal: the core, your star rate, the weekly cap, your protected
  times, how an ask works, and a place for both of you to sign
- My daily core: five things in a good holiday day (something outdoors,
  something made, something for someone else, reading, screen time), big
  tick boxes, print one a day or laminate it
- The jobs board: eight jobs with star values and four blank rows for your
  own, plus a family jobs box for the things everyone does and nobody is paid
  for
- Week one and week two: a grid with a box a day for stars earned, a running
  total, minutes used and a face to draw, with the weekly cap at the top
- The look back: what worked, what was hard, and what you keep for term time
- Your Passport stickers for the Pebble and Bloop pages of the Guided
  Childhood Passport
- A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink friendly version
- A phone version: the deal and the jobs board, so the plan travels with you
  to Granny's

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your
own family needs. For personal use.

It is a planner for a family to agree screen time together. It makes no
promise about your child's behaviour, only that the deal is written down
where everyone can see it.

I am Justin, a dad who got tired of the screen time fight. If anything will
not print or open, message me and I will sort it fast.
