## Description

A routine chart tells your child what comes next, so you are not the one
saying it ten times before school. This one also answers the question that
starts most screen arguments: is it screen time now?

The Planet Day Flip Chart is a morning, after school and bedtime routine flip
chart with 120 picture cards. Four sheets stack and staple at the top, each
cut a little shorter, so the labels show like stairs and your child flips to
the part of the day they are in. The picture cards go into the slots, and a
tick goes on each one when it is done.

The screen time cards come in three kinds, and each looks different, so even
a four year old can read the answer off the chart:

- Gold, my time: a small amount every day that is theirs, no stars needed
- Star, star time: extra time earned with the job cards, at a rate you choose
- Stripes, screens rest: breakfast, lunch, tea, school time and the hour
  before bed. No star space on them, because no star buys them

What you get, 25 pages:

- Two flip charts: Pebble's for ages 4 to 7 and Bloop's for 8 to 10. Each has
  a name sheet, a morning board, an after school board and a bedtime board,
  nine slots on each
- 120 picture cards at 2 inches: 20 morning, 20 after school, 16 bedtime, 12
  jobs that earn stars, the same 12 as family jobs with no stars, 12 screens
  rest cards, 4 my time cards, 4 star time cards and 20 blank cards to write
  your own
- Two ready made week charts, one per age, with the routine printed in and a
  tick box for every day. No cutting
- The star tally strips and the star shop: what stars can buy and what they
  never buy
- A parent page on the three kinds of time and a cut and staple guide
- Your Passport stickers for the Pebble and Bloop pages of the Guided
  Childhood Passport
- A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink friendly version

What you can type on: the PDFs are fillable. Open them in a free PDF reader
and type your child's name (once, and it fills every name line), the blank
cards, the star rate, the bedtime and the my time minutes, then print. The
printed pictures and words are fixed.

The week starts on Monday. UK spelling throughout. Laminate it, or use clear
sticky back plastic, and the ticks wipe clean with a dry wipe pen.

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your
own family needs. For personal use.

It is a routine chart for planning the day together. It does not promise any
particular outcome for any child.
