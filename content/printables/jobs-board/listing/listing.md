## Description

At eleven to fifteen the screen argument stops being about bedtime and starts
being about fairness. This board makes the rules visible, so the deal does
the arguing and you get to be on their side of it.

The Jobs Board is a weekly chore chart for tweens and teens with two columns
that never mix. Family jobs are the ones everyone in the house does, grown
ups included, and they earn nothing, because helping at home is part of
living there. Earning jobs are the bigger or extra ones, and they pay in
stars for screen time at a rate you agree.

What you get, 8 pages:

- A parent page: the two columns, the star rate, a weekly most, protected
  time and how pitching works
- A filled example board, so they see how it works before they start
- The weekly board, blank: six family jobs and eight earning jobs, Monday to
  Sunday, with a stars total
- Stars to minutes: a converter at five and ten minutes a star, their own
  rate, their daily my time, and the protected windows (the hour before bed,
  meals, school hours) that no star buys
- 6 pitch a job slips: they propose a new earning job, or a swap, and you set
  the stars when you say yes
- Our jobs deal: six terms, who runs the board (you, together, or them), two
  signatures and a review date
- Your Passport stickers for the Orbit and Nova pages of the Guided Childhood
  Passport
- A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink friendly version
- A phone version: how the board works and how to pitch a job, for their own
  phone

What you can type on: the PDFs are fillable. Open them in a free PDF reader
and type the name, the jobs, the stars, the rate and the deal, then print.
Or print and write by hand. The rate, the name and the weekly most fill in
once and appear on every page.

The week starts on Monday. UK spelling. There is no pocket money page: this
board pays in screen time, not cash.

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your
own family needs. For personal use.

It is a planning tool for agreeing jobs and screen time together. It does
not promise any particular outcome for any child.

I am Justin, a dad who got tired of the screen time fight. If anything will
not print or open, message me and I will sort it fast.
