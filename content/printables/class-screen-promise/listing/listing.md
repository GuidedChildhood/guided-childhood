## Description

A class promise sticks when the class makes it. This set gives you the words
to start from, the lesson to make it in, and everything to send it home.

Our Class Screen Promise is a print at home poster set for KS1 and KS2, ages 5
to 11. Six short promises a class can say aloud: being kind on screens,
telling a grown up when something feels bad or scary, real or pretend, keeping
private things private, noticing who is on the other side of the screen, and
picking our own stop. You read them together, talk each one through, vote,
and every child signs a slip of their own. DiGi the golden star and all five
Planet Friends keep the poster company.

What you get:

- The class poster, Our Class Screen Promise, with six promises, a space for
  your class name and the date you made it
- The same poster with six blank lines, for classes who write their own words
- 30 pupil promise slips: five sheets of six, one Planet Friend per sheet, with
  "I promise to", a name line and a box to draw in, in big type for KS1
- Teacher notes: how to make the promise in one lesson (talk, vote, sign), one
  question for each promise, where to display it, and a half term tracker for
  coming back to it
- A letter home, ready to fill in and copy, with one conversation for families
  and a cut off family promise slip
- A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink friendly version
- A3 ready: the PDF is vector, so the poster scales cleanly to A3 on any
  printer

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your
own class needs. For personal and single classroom use.

It is a classroom display and discussion resource. It sits alongside your
school's own online safety teaching and safeguarding policy and does not
replace either.
