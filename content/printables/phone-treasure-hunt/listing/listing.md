## Description

Past the chocolate calendar, with a first phone under the tree this year?
Here is a way to hand it over that is fun, and lands on a plan.

The Phone Under The Tree Treasure Hunt is ten clue cards you hide around your
home. Each card has a rhyming riddle to the next hiding place, and one line
your child reads out loud: where the phone sleeps at night, who they tell if
something feels wrong, what they never spend without asking. By the time they
reach the box, they have said the whole plan themselves, in their own voice.

What you get, 13 pages:

- How to set it up, in twenty minutes the night before
- The hiding map: every card's hiding place in order, with a tick box
- 10 clue cards, two to an A4 page, each with a riddle, a read out loud line
  and space to write your own clue if your home is different
- Two box cards: "You found it" for a phone in the box, and "You found the
  plan" for families where the phone comes later, with a date to sit down
  together
- Our phone promises: the lines from the cards as a short deal you both sign
  before it is switched on
- 4 blank clue cards to write your own
- Your Passport sticker for the Orbit page of the Guided Childhood Passport
- A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink friendly version
- A phone version: the hiding map and the promises on your own phone

What you can type on: the PDFs are fillable. Open them in a free PDF reader
and type the name, your own clues, the hiding places, the blank cards and the
promise lines, then print. Or print and write by hand. The printed riddles and
lines are fixed.

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your
own family needs. For personal use.

Not a phone this Christmas? The hunt still works, and ends on the plan
instead: a date, the promises, and the first thing to practise. It is a fun
way to start the conversation. It does not promise any particular outcome for
any child.

I am Justin, a dad who got tired of the screen time fight. If anything will
not print or open, message me and I will sort it fast.
