## Description

Most screen time advice gives you a rule. This gives you the whole plan on
paper: time that is your child's, time they earn, time nobody can buy, the
words for the hard moments, and a calm corner for when it all gets big.

The Whole System Bundle is five Guided Childhood printables in one download,
for families with children aged 4 and up. Every pack is the full pack, the
same as when it is bought on its own.

What you get, 71 pages across five packs:

- The Star Chart (7 pages): agree the jobs together, earn the stars, one star
  is five minutes of screen time
- Earn Your Screen Time (7 pages): a daily chart for 5 to 7, a weekly one for
  8 to 11, screen tokens and the ending well cards
- The Calm Down Corner Kit (9 pages): feelings cards, the star breathing
  poster, calm choice cards and the words for the grown up
- Say This script cards (32 pages, 60 cards): fifty scripts for the hard
  screen moments from 4 to 16, in five levels
- The 30 Day Screen Reset Journal (16 pages): one small change a day, five
  faces each night, and the line your family draws at the end
- Passport stickers in every pack, for the stage pages of the Guided Childhood
  Passport
- Every pack in A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink friendly version
- Phone screens for the packs that have them

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your
own family needs. For personal use only.

These are parenting tools for agreeing a plan together. They are not a
treatment for anything.

I am Justin, a dad who got tired of the screen time fight. If anything will
not print or open, message me and I will sort it fast.
