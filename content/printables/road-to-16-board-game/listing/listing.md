## Description

A board game that the whole table can play, from a six year old to a twelve
year old, and that quietly teaches the family's screen rules while you play.

The Road to 16 is a 60 square board that runs from age 4 to 16, through five
zones, one for each Planet Friend, with DiGi as your host. Land on a job and
take stars. Land on a family job and help another player, with no stars,
because helping at home is part of being a family. Land on a stripy square
and screens rest: no stars can be spent there. Land on a question mark and
answer a choice card.

Every choice card is a real question from Guided Childhood's lessons: the
advert with a big button, the pop up with a timer, the group chat that turns
unkind, the feed that is all football. A wrong answer costs nothing. Read the
answer out loud and play on.

What you get, 19 pages:

- The board over four A4 sheets that tape into one big board, plus the whole
  board on a single A3 sheet
- One page of rules a seven year old can follow
- 48 choice cards in five piles, one per zone, six to a page, with a card
  back to print on the reverse
- 5 Planet Friend counters that fold into stand up tents
- 40 star tokens
- A grown up page on the five stages behind the board
- Your Passport stickers for the Pebble, Bloop and Orbit pages of the Guided
  Childhood Passport
- A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink friendly version

You need a dice, scissors, tape and two to five players. A game takes about
thirty minutes.

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your
own family needs. For personal use.

It is a family board game. It does not promise any particular outcome for any
child.

I am Justin, a dad who got tired of the screen time fight. If anything will
not print or open, message me and I will sort it fast.
