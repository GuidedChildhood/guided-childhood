## Description

One picture and two words on every card, so a child who is not reading yet can follow their own day.

These Routine Picture Cards are a print and cut set of 60 cards for the three parts of a child's day, with 12 blank cards for the things only your house does. Line up the cards for today, in order, and turn each one over as it is done. They work on a fridge, a strip of card, a lanyard or a classroom wall.

What you get, 7 pages:

- Morning cards, among them wake up, wash my face, brush my teeth, get dressed, breakfast, pack my bag and shoes on
- After school cards, among them unpack my bag, snack, homework, reading, play outside and tidy my toys
- Bedtime cards, among them bath, pyjamas on, brush my teeth, story, night light and sleep
- Four job cards: water the plants, empty a bin, sort the recycling and pair the socks
- 12 blank cards to draw and name your own
- A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink friendly version
- A thank you page with a free gift for your family

These pages come from The Planet Day Flip Chart, which is in this shop too. If you would like the whole set, choose that listing and you will not need this one.

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your own family or your own class needs. For personal and single classroom use only.

I am Justin, a dad who got tired of the screen time fight. If anything will not print or open, message me and I will sort it fast.
