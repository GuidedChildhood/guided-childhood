## Description

How do you feel right now? Point to a face, or pick up the card that matches.

These Feelings Cards are a print and cut set for ages 4 to 9: eight feelings to point to and twelve calm choices to try when a feeling is big. Your child picks the card, not you. If it does not help, they pick another. There is no wrong choice, only the next thing to try.

What you get, 3 pages:

- 8 feelings cards: happy, sad, angry, worried, frustrated, tired, excited and calm
- 12 calm choice cards, among them squeeze a cushion, have a drink of water, do a big stretch, ask for a hug and tell your adult
- A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink friendly version
- A thank you page with a free gift for your family

These pages come from The Calm Down Corner Kit, which is in this shop too. If you would like the whole set, choose that listing and you will not need this one.

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your own family or your own class needs. For personal and single classroom use only.

I am Justin, a dad who got tired of the screen time fight. If anything will not print or open, message me and I will sort it fast.
