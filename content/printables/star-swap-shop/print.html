A shop where screen time is one shelf, not the whole shop

Most reward charts pay out in one thing: screens. So the screen becomes the prize, and everything else becomes the work you do to get it. The Star Swap Shop changes the shelves. Your child still earns screen time, at the same rate as our app, one star for five minutes. But on swap day the shop also sells time with you: choosing what is for tea, a board game, a bake, a bike ride. You set the prices together. Put the park on the same shelf as the tablet and see what gets bought.

Ages 4 to 7 · Pebble

Swap little and often. Young children live in today. Count stars at teatime and swap a few that evening, or colour them into the star jar to save for swap day.

Ages 8 to 10 · Bloop

Save for swap day. Pick one day a week. Tally the stars, then go shopping. Saving up for something on the big save shelf is worth praising out loud.

The five shop rules

1. One star is five minutes of screen time, the same deal as the Guided Childhood app. Want a different rate? Agree it, write it on the menu, keep to it.
2. Earned stars are never taken back. Consequences live somewhere else. The shop only ever pays out.
3. Prices are set together and written on the menu. Change them on a Sunday, never at the till.
4. Time together means phones away, yours too. That is what makes it worth the stars.
5. Keep it a game. If the shop starts to feel like a chore, change the prices, not the child.

The habit cards

Twelve cards, each one a healthy tech habit, from screen off at the first ask to devices parked at bedtime. Hand one over when you spot the habit, without being asked for it. Cards are collected, never spent. A full set of twelve earns the Full Set Collector certificate.

Print the charts and the menu on A4 paper, and the tickets and cards on card if you have it. Laminate the menu and write prices with a dry wipe pen, so they can change as your child grows.
