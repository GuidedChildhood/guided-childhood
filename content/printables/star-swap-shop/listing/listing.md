## Description

A reward chart where screen time is one shelf, not the whole shop.

Your child earns stars all week for jobs and healthy tech habits. On swap
day the Star Swap Shop opens. One shelf sells screen time, at one star for
five minutes, the same deal as the Guided Childhood app. The next shelf
sells time with you: choosing what is for tea, a board game, a bake, a bike
ride. The last shelf is the big save, for film night or a day out. You set
the prices together and write them on the menu.

Pebble fronts the chart for ages 4 to 7, with stars to colour and a star jar
for saving. Bloop fronts the chart for ages 8 to 10, with habit rows and a
swap day tally of stars earned, spent and saved.

What you get:

- A two minute parent guide with the five shop rules
- The ages 4 to 7 chart: picture jobs and stars to colour, counted at teatime
- The ages 8 to 10 chart: jobs, three habit rows and a swap day tally
- The shop menu: three shelves with price tags, including blank ones for your
  own ideas
- 15 swap tickets to cut out: screen time, time together and the big save
- 12 habit cards to collect, from screen off at the first ask to devices
  parked at bedtime
- Two certificates: Star Swapper of the Week, and Full Set Collector for all
  twelve cards
- Your Passport stickers, for the Pebble page and the Bloop page of the
  Guided Childhood Passport
- A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink friendly version
- A phone version: both charts and the shop menu, three screens

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your
own family needs. For personal use only.

I am Justin, a dad who got tired of the screen time fight. Earned stars are
never taken back in our house, and time together means phones away, mine
too. If anything will not print or open, message me and I will sort it fast.
