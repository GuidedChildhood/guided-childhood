## Description

One star is five minutes of screen time. Real jobs earn the stars, and the
daily screen time question becomes a deal your family already made.

The Star Chart is the chart from the Starter Pack, on its own, for ages 4 and
up. You agree the jobs together first, write them on the chart, and stick it
on the fridge. Your child colours a star the moment a job is done, and four
stars in a day earns a Planet Friend for the board.

What you get, 7 pages:

- Let us agree the jobs: the page you fill in together first
- The star chart, with your own jobs, bonus rows and the spend row
- The jobs list, with star values and how to make your chart
- Stars to stick and Planet Friends to earn
- The Planet Friend board: four stars a day, five Friends a week
- Cut out star tokens and a weekly tracker
- Your Passport sticker for the Pebble page of the Guided Childhood Passport
- A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink friendly version

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your
own family needs. For personal use only.

I am Justin, a dad who got tired of the screen time fight. If anything will
not print or open, message me and I will sort it fast.
