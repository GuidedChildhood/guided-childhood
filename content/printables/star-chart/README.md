# Star Reward Chart (standalone Etsy listing)

The star reward chart sold on its own, separate from the full Starter Pack, so
Etsy has a cheap, single purpose chart listing (the review engine) as well as
the premium pack. Same design, same deal: one star is five minutes of screen
time.

## What it is (3 pages)

Extracted from the Starter Pack so the two never drift:
1. The star reward chart (name field, the deal, five daily jobs across the week,
   spend row, age guidance).
2. The jobs list plus how to make your chart.
3. Cut out star tokens and the parent tracker.

## Price rung and season

- Etsy: standalone single, 3 to 4 pounds. The review engine, evergreen.
- The full Starter Pack stays the premium 8 to 12 pound anchor. This listing
  feeds buyers up to it.

## Build

Rebuilt from the Starter Pack PDFs with the scratchpad `gh/build-star-chart.mjs`
(pages 2, 3 and 12). Ships A4, US Letter and ink friendly. No separate source,
so a change to the pack chart flows straight through on the next build.
