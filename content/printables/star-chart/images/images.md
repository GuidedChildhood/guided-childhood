# Image ledger: The Star Chart

The Star Chart has no source of its own: it is the Starter Pack's print.html
in its star chart edition, so its art is the Starter Pack's (see
`../../starter-pack/images/images.md`). This folder holds only what the
listing photos need: `friends/pebble.png` and `DiGi-star-3d.png`, copied
unaltered from `public/digi-squad/`, and `preview/`, the page renders.

## Rebuild

    node content/printables/build-pack.mjs star-chart
