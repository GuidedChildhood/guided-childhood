## Description

The whole day on one page: morning, after school and bedtime, ticked off from Monday to Sunday.

The Weekly Routine Chart is a print at home chart with the routine already filled in, so there is nothing to cut out. There is one chart for ages 4 to 7 and one for ages 8 to 10. Write your child's name on the top line, stick it on the fridge and let them tick each one as it is done. A missed tick is just a missed tick. Tomorrow is a new column.

What you get, 3 pages:

- The chart for ages 4 to 7: wake up, get dressed, breakfast, teeth, shoes and coat, then after school and bedtime
- The chart for ages 8 to 10: make my bed, pack my bag, homework, reading, bag and clothes ready, lights out
- A picture beside every line, for children who are not reading yet
- A line for their own screen time each day, and where screens rest: meals, school and the hour before bed
- A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink friendly version
- A thank you page with a free gift for your family

These pages come from The Planet Day Flip Chart, which is in this shop too. If you would like the whole set, choose that listing and you will not need this one.

The two real life photos show the chart filled in as an example. Yours arrives blank, ready to fill in yourself.

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your own family needs. For personal use only.

I am Justin, a dad who got tired of the screen time fight. If anything will not print or open, message me and I will sort it fast.
