## Description

The best talks with a tween rarely start with "so, how was school?". They
start with a good question at tea, when nobody is looking at a phone.

What Would You Do? is 100 conversation cards in three suits:

- 40 talk at tea: everyday questions that get everyone talking, grown ups
  included
- 34 online situations: a group chat that turns unkind, a player who asks
  what school you go to, a photo request, a pop up with a timer. Every one
  comes from a real choice question in Guided Childhood's lessons, and the
  strong move from that lesson is printed upside down at the foot, so you
  talk first and check after
- 26 what would DiGi say?: the things children ask (can I have a phone, five
  more minutes, can I post this). Everyone guesses what DiGi would say. DiGi
  never says yes or no: it says where you are, the next step and the words

What you get, 29 pages:

- 100 cards, four to an A4 page, 88 by 124mm, with a ring hole guide in the
  corner
- One card back for all of them, to print on the reverse if you want
- A parent page on keeping it to ten minutes, and the one rule at the table:
  nobody gets in trouble for an answer
- Your Passport stickers for the Bloop and Orbit pages of the Guided
  Childhood Passport
- A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink friendly version

Card stock works best. Cut on the dashed lines, punch the corner, and keep a
suit on a ring by the table or in the car.

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your
own family needs. For personal use.

These are conversation cards. They do not promise any particular outcome for
any child.
