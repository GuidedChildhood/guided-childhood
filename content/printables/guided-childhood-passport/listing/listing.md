## Description

A stamp in this passport is earned. Your child gets one when they have
learned something, never for a birthday.

The Guided Childhood Passport is a print at home passport for the journey
from four to sixteen. There are five pages, one for each stage, and each page
belongs to one of our Planet Friends: Pebble, Bloop, Orbit, Nova and Cosmo.
Every page asks for three things. First you have the conversation, and the
words to say are printed on the page. Then you do one small thing together.
Then comes the check: your child tells you the big idea back in their own
words. That is a chat, not a test. When all three are done, the page gets its
stamp.

Join at any age. Start on the page for your child's age today. The earlier
pages stay open, so you can come back and fill them in whenever you like.

What you get:

- The fold out passport: one sheet, one slit, three folds, and it is a little
  burgundy passport the size of a real one. Instructions included.
- Five full size stage pages, one per Planet Friend, with the stamp ring, the
  three things to do, and lines for the best thing they learned
- A stamp and sticker sheet: five big stage stamps, a Passport complete seal,
  small stamps and Friend stickers for the fold out book, and twenty DiGi stars
- A page for grown ups explaining what earns each stamp, at every stage
- A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink saver version
- A phone version: seven screens, so the Passport can live on a phone as well
  as the fridge

The stages:

- Pebble, ages 4 to 7: the first device conversation
- Bloop, ages 8 to 10: the bedroom rule
- Orbit, ages 11 to 12: the algorithm conversation
- Nova, ages 13 to 15: when things go wrong online
- Cosmo, 16 and up: building independence

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your
own family needs. For personal use only.

I am Justin, a dad who got tired of the screen time fight. The Passport is the
paper version of the one in the Guided Childhood app. If anything will not
print or open, message me and I will sort it fast.
