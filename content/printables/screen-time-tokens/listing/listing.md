## Description

A token handed over is a promise kept, so the timer matches the token.

Screen Time Tokens are a print and cut set for ages 5 to 11: tokens for 5, 15 and 30 minutes of screen time, and two cards for the hardest part, which is the ending. Hand over the token that matches the time, set the timer and bring out the card when there are five minutes to go.

What you get, 3 pages:

- 12 screen tokens: five for 5 minutes, five for 15 minutes and two for 30 minutes, each marked with the stars it costs at one star for five minutes
- 3 family swap tokens, to swap screen time for a treat you choose together
- Two ending well cards: Five minutes to go, and Time is up, with the words to say
- A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink friendly version
- A thank you page with a free gift for your family

These pages come from Earn Your Screen Time, which is in this shop too. If you would like the whole set, choose that listing and you will not need this one.

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your own family needs. For personal use only.

I am Justin, a dad who got tired of the screen time fight. If anything will not print or open, message me and I will sort it fast.
