The three kinds of time
Same three kinds of time, whatever house we are in.
Our time

A little every day, no strings

Yours. Not earned, never taken away. Today's is for today, and it goes first.

Earned time

Do a job, earn a star

One star is five minutes, on any screen. Earned stars are never taken back.

Protected time

Times no stars can buy

Meals, bedtime and the hour before. Want a screen then? Ask. You always get an answer.
