## Description

Eyes out of the window, not on a screen. Spot it, cross it off, shout bingo.

Car Bingo is a print at home set of road trip games for ages 4 to 11. The picture card is for ages 4 to 7 and the hard card is for ages 8 to 11, so a younger and an older child can play on the same journey. A page of number plate and alphabet games is there for when the bingo is won.

What you get, 4 pages:

- Car bingo, the picture card: 16 things to spot, among them a tractor, a bridge, a church spire and a wind turbine
- Car bingo, the hard card: 24 things to spot, among them a caravan, a tunnel, a car towing a boat and a place name with a Z in it
- The number plate game: turn the last three letters of a plate into a silly sentence
- Alphabet spotting: find something for every letter from A to Z
- A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink friendly version
- A thank you page with a free gift for your family

These pages come from Are We Nearly There?, which is in this shop too. If you would like the whole set, choose that listing and you will not need this one.

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your own family needs. For personal use only.

I am Justin, a dad who got tired of the screen time fight. If anything will not print or open, message me and I will sort it fast.
