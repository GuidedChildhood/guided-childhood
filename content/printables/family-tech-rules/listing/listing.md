## Description

Six house rules for screens, on one page for the fridge. Keep a rule, cross one out or write your own.

Family Tech Rules is a print at home page that a family fills in together, with a second page of signs for screen free meals. Everyone signs it, grown ups included, because the rules are for the whole house. Rules a child helped to write are rules a child keeps.

What you get, 3 pages:

- Our family tech rules: six rules to keep or cross out, two lines to write your own, a line for everyone to sign and a date to look at them again
- The six rules: devices sleep in the kitchen, meal times are screen free, when the timer goes the screen goes down, ask before a new app or game, tell a grown up if something online feels wrong, and grown ups keep these rules too
- Phones in the basket: a sign for the meals you eat together, a label for the basket and two tents to fold for the table
- A4 and US Letter in full colour, plus an ink friendly version
- A thank you page with a free gift for your family

These pages come from Where Phones Sleep, which is in this shop too. If you would like the whole set, choose that listing and you will not need this one.

This is an instant download. Nothing is posted. Print as many copies as your own family needs. For personal use only.

I am Justin, a dad who got tired of the screen time fight. If anything will not print or open, message me and I will sort it fast.
