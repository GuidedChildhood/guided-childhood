Play and outside jobs pay the most stars on purpose. Screens are brilliant, but bodies and brains grow strongest running about and making things.

How to make your chart

Five minutes, then it runs itself

Print the chart page and this jobs list. Card stock lasts longer.
Write their name at the top and swap in any jobs that fit your family.
Laminate it, or slide it in a wallet, so a dry wipe pen makes it last all year.
Stick both pages on the fridge at their eye height, chart on the left, jobs on the right.
Colour a star together the moment a job is done. Naming the good thing is half the reward.
Spend the stars: five minutes of screen each, or save them for a bigger treat.

DiGi says: never take a star back. The chart only ever pays out. Consequences live somewhere else, this is where the good gets seen.

How to

The jobs, shown step by step

No. 4

A star is easier to earn when a child knows exactly what the job looks like. Cut these out, pop them by the sink or the table, and let them follow along until it is second nature.

🍽️
Lay the table
Worth ★★
Count the people eating. One of everything each.
Mats down first, one in front of every chair.
Fork on the left, spoon and knife on the right.
Cup above the knife. Napkin folded on the plate.
Step back and check every seat matches. Star earned.
🧸
Tidy your room
Worth ★
Bed first. Pull the cover up flat, pillow on top. The room looks half done already.
One home each. Books to the shelf, toys to the box, teddies on the bed.
Clothes in the basket if dirty, folded on the chair if clean.
Floor sweep with your eyes. Anything left has a home. Put it there.
Doorway check. Stand at the door. Tidy from here? Star earned.
🫧
Load the dishwasher
Worth ★★
Scrape the big bits into the bin or the food caddy first.
Plates and bowls on the bottom, all facing the middle.
Cups and glasses on the top, upside down so they fill up with water.
Cutlery handles down in the basket. Ask a grown up for anything sharp.
Tablet in, door shut, press start with a grown up. Star earned.
🌟

DiGi says: do the job with them the first few times, not for them. Show, then watch, then let go. A child who knows the steps earns the star on their own, and that is the whole point.

Move

The five minute wake up workout

No. 5

Six moves, one after the other. Do the whole round twice and that is a star earned. No kit, no space needed, brilliant before school or to break up a screen.

1
Star jumps
Ten of them
Arms and legs out like a big star, then back in.
2
Bear walk
Across the room
Hands and feet on the floor, bottom in the air, walk like a bear.
3
Frog jumps
Eight big hops
Crouch down low, then spring up high with a ribbit.
4
Tall tree
Hold to ten
Stand on one leg, arms up like branches. Swap legs.
5
Speedy runs
On the spot
Run as fast as you can without moving, knees up high.
6
Slow breaths
Three big ones
Breathe in for a smell the flower, out for a blow the candle.
Star jump illustration
Why it pays big

Moving first thing wakes the brain up better than any screen. That is why a good run about is worth three stars on the chart.

Skills

Football to practise, dances to learn

No. 6
⚽
Four football skills
TEN GO IS ONE ROUND. SPACE OR GARDEN.
1
Toe taps
Ball still, tap the top with each foot, quick as you can. Count to twenty.
2
Dribble the line
Little touches, keep the ball close, walk it heel to wall and back.
3
Stop it dead
Roll the ball, then trap it still under your foot. Both feet.
4
Aim and pass
Pick a target, pass with the inside of your foot, hit it five times.
💃
Six dance moves
PUT A SONG ON. STRING THEM TOGETHER.
1The reach. Reach up high to the left, then the right, like picking stars.
2The stomp. Stomp left, stomp right, clap in the middle.
3The spin. One slow turn all the way round, arms out wide.
4The wiggle. Wiggle down low, then wiggle all the way back up.
5The robot. Stiff arms, little steps, beep like a robot.
6The freeze. Dance mad, then freeze like a statue when the music stops.

Fuel

Build a breakfast that lasts till lunch

No. 7

A good breakfast is three parts on one plate. Get all three and a child stays full, calm and focused for the whole morning, no mid morning meltdown, no snack begging.

Slow energy
oats, toast, cereal
Building blocks
egg, yoghurt, beans
Colour and water
fruit, veg, milk or water

FILL ALL THREE PARTS OF THE PLATE

The child science, plainly
1Slow energy burns steady, so there is no sugar crash and grumpy hour before lunch.
2Building blocks keep a child full for hours and help a growing body do its growing.
3Colour and water wake up the brain and top up the water they lost overnight.

Make it a job: let them build their own plate from the three parts. A breakfast they built is a breakfast they eat, and that is a star earned.

Tips

Put your pack together, make it last

No. 11
Assemble the booklet
Print the lot on plain A4, or card for the chart and jobs list so they live on the fridge all year.
Fridge pages: the star chart and the jobs list. Laminate them or use a wallet, then a dry wipe pen resets every week.
Cut out the how to cards and pop them where the job happens, by the sink, the table, the toy box.
Booklet the rest: stack the move, skills, breakfast, read and game pages, fold in half and staple the spine. A little newspaper of their own.
Start small. Pick three jobs for week one. Add more once the habit sticks. The chart only ever pays out.
