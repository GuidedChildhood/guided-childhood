## Top selling downloads to model (research, July 2026)

The proven winners in this exact niche, and what to copy from them:

1. **Editable chore and reward charts** are the review engine. Top listings sit
   at hundreds of five star reviews, priced 3 to 5 dollars. Copy: the editable
   name field, daily rewards for younger children and weekly for older ones,
   and clearly showing what a star is worth. Our Starter Pack and Earn Your
   Screen Time already do this.
2. **Screen time contracts and trackers** sell strongly on their own. Copy: the
   explicit deal, one star is five minutes, front and centre. This is our edge,
   lead with it in the title and the first photo.
3. **Rainbow sliding routine charts** (a reusable arrow you move) are a top
   format. Copy: sell a laminate and dry wipe, use it all year angle, which we
   already teach on the how to make your chart page.
4. **Editable chore chart bundles** that fold screen time, allowance and chores
   into one listing hit the highest sales. Copy: bundle the singles into a
   named outcome (The Family Screen Reset Kit) priced 20 to 30 percent under
   the sum, per the bundles recipe.
5. **Star Seller mechanics** matter as much as the product: five star reviews,
   ship (deliver) on time, and reply to every message quickly. For digital
   that means instant files that just work and fast replies. Build the reply
   habit from listing one.

## Sell the charts as their own listings, not only the pack

Etsy rewards focused, single purpose listings, so the charts go up on their own
as well as inside the premium pack. This widens the funnel: a parent searching
star chart finds the cheap chart, leaves a review, and gets fed up to the pack.

- **Star Reward Chart** (`content/printables/star-chart/`), 3 pages, 3 to 4
  pounds. Built and ready, extracted from the pack so they never drift.
- The full **Starter Pack** stays the premium 8 to 12 pound anchor.
- Earn Your Screen Time chart, already built, is a third chart listing.

Each chart listing carries the same free bonus line to /etsy-bonus.
