## Checks

- It is fair to YouTube Kids: it says the manual mode works. It names no harm and makes no claim about what YouTube Kids shows.
- None of the banned playlists words. "Suggested" is used in place of "algorithm".
- One mention of another product by name. If Justin would rather not name it, the card becomes "Is this just a kids' video app?" and the caption loses its second paragraph.
