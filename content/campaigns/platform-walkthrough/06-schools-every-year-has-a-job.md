## Post text, LinkedIn (Justin)

Online safety gets taught in a panic, the week after something happens.

I think that is backwards, so I drew the map the other way.

Every year group gets a job that fits its age.

Reception is screens shared with a grown up, and the first idea that some
things are real and some are made.

Years 1 to 6 are the habits years, before the first phone. How a game keeps
you playing. How a feed decides, taught with a paper algorithm before any
child meets a real one.

Years 7 to 9 are the detective years. Feeds, fakes, scams.

Years 10 and 11 take the serious cases, named plainly and taught calmly.

Years 12 and 13 are AI used well, data rights and the first CV.

It comes to 34 modules. Ten behaviours come back at every stage, deeper each
time, so nothing is taught once and forgotten.

The whole map is open. Any teacher or parent can read every card, and one
lesson is free to teach in full.

The link is in the first comment.

What do you wish your school had taught a year earlier than it did?
