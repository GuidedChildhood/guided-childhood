"Two more minutes" is never two more minutes, and by the time it is twenty
the row is not really about the tablet any more.

Here is the piece we built for that.

lands on your phone and you say yes or not yet. Then a countdown starts that
you are both looking at. When it runs out, the timer says so, and you are not
the one calling time.

a deal running out, not you being mean.
