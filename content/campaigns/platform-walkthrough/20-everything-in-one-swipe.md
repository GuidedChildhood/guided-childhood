## Post text, LinkedIn (Justin), if he wants the three platform version

I have built three things, for three different rooms.

For the living room: Guided Childhood. A plan for getting a child to sixteen
ready for a phone, with the parent and the child working on it together.

For the classroom: a scheme of 34 lessons from Reception to Year 13, with
the teacher's words under every slide.

For the sofa: The Social Billboard, where a parent chooses everything a
child watches before they press play.

They look different and they come from one idea. A child gets better at
this by being taught, a bit at a time, by an adult who is in the room.

Each one has a free way in, and none of them asks for a card.

Links in the first comment.

If you could only have one of the three, which room needs it most?

First comment: the stage check (`guidedchildhood.com/v/tour-20-li`), the free
lesson, and the first playlist.
