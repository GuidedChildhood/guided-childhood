## Caption, Facebook

It is a fair question: is this just parental controls?

It is not, and we would rather say so plainly. Parental controls make today's
decision for you. They are useful, and we have a whole section that walks you
through setting them up properly: the tablet, the console, the telly, the
laptop and the phone, then the apps one by one. If a first phone is coming,
there is a guide for letting it out a bit at a time.

But that is the easy half. One day the controls come off. The half that
lasts is the words you say, the deal you agree, and the practice your child
gets before that day. That is what the rest of the app is for.

If a new device is coming home this week, set it up once and set it up
properly.
