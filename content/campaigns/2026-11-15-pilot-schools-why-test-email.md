# Pilot email 1: on sign up

---

**Subject:** Your pilot: why it is worth one lesson this term, and five questions

Hello [first name],

Thank you for signing [school] up for the pilot. I wanted to tell you why I am
asking you to test it, what you get for doing so, and the five things I most
need to hear back.

**Why test it**

The revised RSHE guidance has been in force since September 2026, and it asks
a lot of the digital side: what pupils are taught, in which year, and how you
know. I have tried to build the whole job for you: the lesson, the words to
say on every slide, the sheets, and a record that fills itself in so you can
show what each class has been taught.

I do not want you to take that from me. I want you to teach one lesson and see
whether it is true in your classroom, with your pupils, on a normal Tuesday.

**What your code opens**

Two lessons matched to the phase you teach, for a term, with:

1. The classroom player, with a script on every slide, so a teacher who is not
   a specialist can teach it without preparing for an evening.
2. Every printable for both lessons: the pupil booklet, the two quizzes, the
   knowledge organiser and the learning record.
3. One tap at the end of the lesson to mark it taught. That builds a record
   for your school. No pupil is ever named.
4. The Hub: the statutory mapping, the policy text, the staff briefings, the
   data protection pack, and a new kit for the subject lead with deep dive
   questions, a pupil voice sheet and a staff confidence audit.

**The five questions**

After you have taught a lesson, reply to this email with a line on each. That
is all I need.

1. Could you teach it from the script, without extra preparation?
2. Did the pupils stay with it? Which part held them, and where did it sag?
3. Was anything wrong for your class: the level, the timing, a word, an example?
4. Was the record at the end worth the tap? Would your subject lead or head use
   what it shows?
5. What would stop your school using this next year?

The fifth one matters most. If the answer is the price, the timetable, or
another scheme you already pay for, I would rather know now.

**When the term ends**

You decide. Carrying on is one click, with your details already filled in.
Stopping is fine too, and your answers will still have made it better for the
next school.

If you would like me to walk your staff through it in fifteen minutes, or if
your code has not reached you, reply and I will sort it the same day.

Thank you for giving this your time.

Justin

Justin Phillips
Guided Childhood
