Six things we argue about most weeks in our house, and probably yours.

The TV before school. Getting out of the door with hair done. The bedroom floor. Who sits in the front. The reading record. And the school email nobody read until Friday.

So we put the week on the fridge. Two sheets, filled in together on Sunday night:

A star chart with the morning and school jobs already on it.
Our school week: a tick a night for reading and homework, the front seat rota, the morning order with the TV last, and ten minutes on Sunday for ClassDojo, the school app and the emails.
