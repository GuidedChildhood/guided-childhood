# Parent groups on Facebook: the admin message and the posts

16 November 2026. Door 3 of `plans/week-of-2026-11-02-first-20-signups-plan.md`,
written for Justin to post himself. Facebook does not let an app post into
groups for a person, and a group post from a bot would break the group's trust
and Facebook's terms, so these are copy and paste.

No family story is used, because none of these moments is on record as one of
Justin's own (`content/brand-story/founder-context.md`). If one is true for you,
add it in your own words. Every claim here is one the product makes about itself (`components/starter/ShareStage.tsx`):
three questions, the child's stage and what to say tonight, no sign up, free.

## How to use it

1. Pick up to five local parent groups you are already in or can join:
   Winscombe and Sandford, Cheddar, Weston super Mare, North Somerset, and one
   Bristol parents group.
2. Read each group's rules. Most allow a business post only on one day a
   week, and some not at all.
3. Send the admin message first. Post only where the admin says yes.
4. Give each group its own link so you can see which one works. Write down
   which is which:

| Link | Group |
| --- | --- |
| guidedchildhood.com/v/grp-1 | |
| guidedchildhood.com/v/grp-2 | |
| guidedchildhood.com/v/grp-3 | |
| guidedchildhood.com/v/grp-4 | |
| guidedchildhood.com/v/grp-5 | |

5. Stay and help. When someone asks a phone or screen question, answer it as a
   parent with no link (the reply below). Most groups welcome the person who
   helps, and remove the one who only promotes.

## Finding the groups (searched 16 November)

Most parent groups on Facebook are private, so a web search cannot see them;
the search inside Facebook can. What the web search did find:

| Group | Where | Fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Sandford Baby and Toddlers](https://www.facebook.com/sandfordparentandtoddlers/) | Sandford Parish Hall, Wednesday mornings in term time | A page, mostly under 4s. Worth a friendly message: many of those parents have an older child too |
| [Bristol Parents Club](https://www.instagram.com/bristolparentsclub/) | Bristol, South Glos and North Somerset | Small (about 210 followers), general parenting. Ask the owner |

Not to post in: the parent carer and autism support groups the search also
found (Bristol Autism Support, North Somerset Parent Carers, NeonDaisy). They
exist to support families of children with additional needs, and a product
post there would be unwelcome and off topic.

Type these into the Facebook search bar, then tap Groups:

- Winscombe, Sandford, Banwell, Churchill, Axbridge, Cheddar
- Weston super Mare mums, Weston parents, Worle mums
- North Somerset mums, North Somerset parents
- Bristol mums, Bristol parents, and your children's school name with PTA or Friends of

Pick groups with recent posts and at least a few hundred members, and join as
yourself before messaging the admin.

## 1. The message to the group admin

> Hi, I'm a dad near Winscombe. I've spent this year building something for
> parents who aren't sure when their child is ready for a phone: three quick
> questions that tell you your child's stage and what to say tonight. It's
> free and there's no sign up. Would you be happy for me to share it once, on
> whatever day suits your rules? Completely fine if not. Justin

## 2. The post, for the day the admin names

Lead with this one in November and December.

> Is a phone on your child's Christmas list this year?
>
> Every answer I found was either ban it or give in, and neither felt like a
> plan.
>
> So I built a short check for it. Three questions, and it tells you your
> child's stage and what to say to them tonight. It's free and there's no sign
> up.
>
> guidedchildhood.com/v/grp-1
>
> I'm a dad round here, so if you try it I'd really like to hear what it got
> wrong.

Change `grp-1` to that group's link before posting.

## 3. A second post, for a group that allows another one later

> Does anyone else have the same argument every night when the tablet has to
> go off?
>
> One thing that helps more than a new rule: agree the ending before the
> screen goes on, together, and let your child say when.
>
> If you want the words for your child's age, there's a free three question
> check here, no sign up: guidedchildhood.com/v/grp-1

## 4. The reply when someone asks for help (no link)

Use when a parent posts a phone or screen worry. Change it to fit their
question; never paste it word for word twice in one group.

> That one comes up in so many houses. Something that helps: decide the ending
> before the screen goes on, and let them say when, instead of pulling it away
> halfway through. It turns the fight into a plan. How old is yours?

Only if they ask what you use, reply with the group's link.

## Before you post

- Check the link opens the three questions (all five were checked on
  16 November and they do).
- Post from your own profile, as yourself.
- Don't post the same text in more than one group on the same day.
