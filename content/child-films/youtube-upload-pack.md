# YouTube upload pack: the five DiGi films

Channel: Guided Childhood (Justin, 16 November). Upload in this order, which is the order of the lessons.

Settings for every film:
- Audience: **Yes, it's made for kids.** (This turns off comments and end screens. Each film already ends on its own stage check card.)
- Language: English (United Kingdom). Upload the `.en-GB.srt` file under Subtitles.
- Thumbnail: the `-thumbnail-1280x720.png` file.
- Playlist: "Watch together: DiGi films for 4 to 7 year olds".
- Visibility: Public.

## 1. Me on a screen and me in real life. A film to watch with your 4 to 7 year old

- Video: `1.1-release/me-on-a-screen-and-me-in-real-life-youtube-16x9.mp4`
- Thumbnail: `1.1-release/me-on-a-screen-and-me-in-real-life-thumbnail-1280x720.png`
- Subtitles: `1.1-release/me-on-a-screen-and-me-in-real-life.en-GB.srt`

Title:
```
Me on a screen and me in real life. A film to watch with your 4 to 7 year old
```
Description:
```
A five minute film to watch together. A photo only shows a tiny slice of a person, and the real you is so much more. Your child learns one small rule: if anything on a screen makes you feel funny inside, say no and tell your grown up.

Afterwards, play the mirror game and ask: "If something on a screen made you feel funny inside, what would you do?" Then cheer the answer.

What stage is your child? Three questions, no sign up.
guidedchildhood.com/starter-pack
```
