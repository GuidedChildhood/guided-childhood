## Title

One piece is never just one piece | Watch together with your 8 to 10 year old

## Description

A six minute film to watch together with your 8 to 10 year old.

Bloop has lost a jigsaw piece again, and DiGi shows how private details get collected one small piece at a time. A name on a leaderboard. A birthday in a quiz. A school in a friendly chat. Each one feels like nothing. Joined up, they are enough to get into an account.

Your child learns:
• the piece test: could this help somebody find me, get into something of mine, or pretend to be me?
• the six things that always stay private
• the one move to know by name: "let's chat somewhere else". That is always a check with your grown up moment.

There are two pause points with a question for you to ask. Afterwards, play Five Pieces together.

Tonight's promise, from you: "If anyone asks you to move to another app, come and tell me. You won't lose the game for telling me."

If what comes out is bigger than a piece of information, stop and do not question them further. Report it to CEOP at ceop.police.uk. Childline is free on 0800 1111.

What stage is your child? Three questions, no sign up: guidedchildhood.com/starter-pack
