# Parent film: phones go to bed first

Subject group C (sleep and the phone at night). The parent half of the 1.6 pair.
16 November 2026. Recorded and built: see README.md. Three lines changed in recording (below); the words as recorded are in build/lines.json.

**Watch it, then act on it.** About 75 seconds. Portrait first (the family
account and the product), a landscape version from the same build.
Voiced by DiGi (Tamsin, slower) with full captions, so it works with the sound
off. No figures: the teenage sleep numbers do not apply to a child of 4 to 10
(withdrawn as a claim on 14 November), and the film does not need one.

Real product screens only, filmed from the `ref-*` preview pages with made up
data and swapped names. The menu path in beat 5 is checked by a person in the
app before filming, or the beat is cut.

## The one action

Pick the spot where every screen sleeps tonight, your own phone included, and
book the child film for a time you are both there.

## What it does not say

- No hours of sleep a child "needs", no study, no percentage.
- Nothing about blue light. The film is about the routine, not the science of light.
- Never that screens are bad, or that this child will sleep better. We claim a
  calmer bedtime routine, not an outcome.
