## Not swapped in, on purpose: 2.1

The app has no Stage 2 Watch together film yet. When a child finishes a film,
`/api/parent-lessons/complete` awards the stage passport once the child has
completed every active lesson of that stage. With 2.1 as the only active
Stage 2 lesson, watching that one film would award a Stage 2 child the whole
Stage 2 passport. That needs a decision (keep 2.1 out of the app until more
Stage 2 films exist, or change the passport rule) before the row goes in.
