# Parent film: is this real?

Subject group B (AI, fakes and chatbots). The parent half of the 1.5 pair.
Draft 1, 17 November 2026. NOT RECORDED. Nothing generated.

**Watch it, then act on it.** About 70 seconds. Portrait first, landscape from
the same build. DiGi's voice (Tamsin, slower) with full captions, so it works
with the sound off. No figures.

One real product screen: the Book a time panel under a Watch together film,
filmed from `app/ref-watch-booking` with made up data. Watch together booking
went live with migration 400 on 17 November, so the screen is real.

About 120 spoken words.

## The one action

Next time they ask "Is this real?", say "Good question. Let's look together",
and book the film for a time you are both there.

## What it does not say

- No percentage of people fooled, no study.
- Never that AI is dangerous or bad. Made up pictures can be fun.
- Nothing about spotting clues in a picture: the clues change, the question
  does not.

## Line to check before recording

Beat 2, "Sometimes nobody can tell just by looking". It is worded as
"sometimes" on purpose: it claims that it happens, not how often. If that is
too strong for Justin, the fallback is "It can be very hard to tell just by
looking."
