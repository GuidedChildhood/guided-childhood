# 1.5 remake: Real or pretend?

Child lesson film, Stage 1 (ages 4 to 7), watched with a grown up.
Subject group B in `../subjects-2026-11-16.md` ("is this real?": AI, fakes and
chatbots). Draft 1, 17 November 2026. NOT RECORDED. Nothing generated.

## What changes from the film live today

The live 1.5 is clean and is going out first in its old pictures (14 November
decision). This remake brings it to the series standard and adds the one thing
parents are reacting to now: a computer can make a picture of something that
never happened.

## The lesson

- **Knowing**: some things on screens are real, some are pretend, and a
  computer can make a picture of something that never happened. When I am not
  sure, I ask "Is this real?"
- **Feeling**: I do not have to know by myself. Asking is the clever thing,
  and I am never in trouble for asking.
- **Special word**: PRETEND
- **Catchphrase, three times or more**: "Is this real?"
- **Routine practised**: see something amazing, ask a grown up "Is this real?",
  look together.
- **Pebble's joke**: Pebble holds the picture book upside down, then turns it
  round. Never the lesson itself.

## What it deliberately does not do

- It does not teach a child to spot computer made pictures by their clues
  (fingers, shadows). Those clues change as the tools improve, and a child
  who thinks they can tell is easier to fool than one who asks.
- It never makes a made up picture frightening. Made up pictures can be fun;
  the skill is asking before believing (the school lesson's own line).
- No figures and no claims about how many people are fooled.

## SEGMENT A

**1. Hello** (r01)
PICTURE: A cosy living room. Pebble holds a big picture book upside down, puzzled. DiGi floats beside, glowing.
DIDGEE: Hello, it's Didgee. And here's Pebble.
PICTURE (r02): Pebble turns the book the right way round and beams.
DIDGEE: Oh, Pebble. Other way round. That's better.

**2. Calm me** (p02, reused)
DIDGEE: Let's breathe together. Breathe in, and blow out slowly. One more. Lovely.

**3. Remember when** [PRODUCT ONLY] (r03)
PICTURE: Pebble looks at a small framed photo of itself on a shelf.
DIDGEE: Last time, we learned that a photo only shows a tiny bit of you. Today, we're picture detectives.

**4. Today's one thing** (r04)
PICTURE: A magnifying glass resting on a cosy rug, with soft sparkles.
WORD ON SCREEN: PRETEND
DIDGEE: Today's special word is pretend.
DIDGEE: Pretend means made up. Not really happening.

**5. Teach: real** (r05)
PICTURE: Through a window, a real puppy splashing in a puddle in the rain. Pebble watches, delighted.
DIDGEE: Some things are real. A real puppy. Real rain. Real splashes.

**6. Teach: pretend** (r06)
PICTURE: A tablet showing a bright cartoon of a friendly dragon flying over a castle. Pebble giggles.
DIDGEE: Some things are pretend. Dragons are pretend. Someone drew this one, to make a story.

**7. Teach: made by a computer** (r07)
PICTURE: A tablet showing a little dog in a space helmet standing on the moon, looking very real. Pebble's eyes go wide.
DIDGEE: And here's a tricky one. A computer can make a picture of something that never happened.
DIDGEE: This dog never went to the moon. A computer made the picture. It looks real, but it isn't.

**8. Nobody knows by looking** (r08)
PICTURE: Pebble scratching its head, a little question mark floating above.
DIDGEE: Sometimes you can't tell, just by looking. And that's okay. You don't have to know by yourself.

**Pause and ask 1** (p07, reused)
CARD (live): Can you think of something on a screen that is PRETEND? And something that is REAL?
DIDGEE: Pause me here, and ask each other the question. I'll wait.

## SEGMENT B

**9. The magic question** (r09)
PICTURE: Pebble holding a tablet up, pointing at it, mouth open as if asking. DiGi beside, sparkling.
WORD ON SCREEN: Is this real?
DIDGEE: So when you're not sure, ask the magic question.
DIDGEE: Is this real?

**10. Ask a grown up** (r10)
PICTURE: Pebble hurrying along a hallway towards a warm open door, holding the tablet.
DIDGEE: Take it to your grown up and ask: is this real? Then look at it together.

**11. The game** (r05, r06, r07 again, each quickly)
DIDGEE: Let's play. Real, pretend, or made by a computer? Shout it out.
PICTURE: r05, the puppy in the puddle.
DIDGEE: The puppy in the puddle? Real!
PICTURE: r06, the dragon.
DIDGEE: The dragon? Pretend!
PICTURE: r07, the dog on the moon.
DIDGEE: The dog on the moon? Made by a computer. Ooh, tricky.

**12. The clever thing** (r11)
PICTURE: DiGi and Pebble give each other a high five, little sparkles.
DIDGEE: The clever thing isn't knowing. The clever thing is asking.

**Pause and say 2** (p07, reused)
CARD (live): Say together: "When we are not sure... we ASK." Then give each other a high five.
DIDGEE: Pause me here. Say it together, and do your high five.

## SEGMENT C

**13. Never in trouble** (r12)
PICTURE: Pebble and DiGi sitting together on a sofa, cosy and calm.
DIDGEE: You will never be in trouble for asking. Grown ups love that question.
