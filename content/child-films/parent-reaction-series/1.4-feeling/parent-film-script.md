# Parent film: the five minutes after they tell you

Subject group A (seen something upsetting online). The parent half of the 1.4
pair. Draft 1, 17 November 2026. NOT RECORDED. Nothing generated.

**Watch it, then act on it.** About 70 seconds. Portrait first, landscape from
the same build. DiGi's voice (Tamsin, slower) with full captions. No figures.
Our own words and our own position (the trust framework, `digi/07-trust-framework.md`:
connection is the protection, repair over punishment), not anyone else's advice.

One real product screen: the Book a time panel, the same capture as the 1.5
parent film (`../1.5-real/build/pics/screen.png`).

About 130 spoken words.

## The one action

Tonight, say "You will never be in trouble for telling me", and book the film
for a time you are both there.

## Checked before writing

- 0808 800 5000 is the NSPCC helpline for adults worried about a child, on the
  NSPCC's own page (nspcc.org.uk/helpline), checked 17 November 2026. Its
  opening hours differ between their own pages, so the film gives the number
  only, never the hours.

## What it does not say

- No figures about how many children see upsetting things.
- Never that the parent did something wrong, and never "take the device away".
- No promise that the child will be fine. We claim a calmer conversation and a
  route, not an outcome.

## Line to weigh before recording

Beat 2, "If telling you costs them the tablet, they may stop telling you". It
is worded as "may" on purpose: it is the reasoning behind the never in trouble
promise, not a measured rate. If Justin wants it softer, the fallback is "If
telling means losing the tablet, telling gets harder."
