lines, every word checked by transcript. No music, on Justin's rule.

Cost: 26.7 credits (8.75 a shot, the voice under half a credit).

Seen on the contact sheet: both characters stay on model, with real squash and
stretch. Two flaws. In shot 2 Pebble's colour darkens to brown for a moment,
and in shot 3 the star squashes out of shape in its twirl. A full film would
regenerate any shot like that.

What a whole film would cost at this price: a five minute film is about 50
shots, so roughly 440 credits fully animated. Key moments animated (about 10
shots) with the rest as gentle motion is about 90 credits a film.
