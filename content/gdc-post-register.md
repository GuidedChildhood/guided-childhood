# The GDC post register

**One place a number is claimed. Check it before you assign one, and add your
row before you write the post.**

This exists because the numbering broke twice in two days, both times for the
same reason: a number was assumed free instead of established.

- 13 October: GDC-022 was given to "Two children, same game" when GDC-022 had
  already been published in September. The real one had "done" on its first line
  inside the body, where a file listing cannot show it.
- 14 October: GDC-036, 037 and 038 were given to three rewrites when all three
  were already in use. They were missed because they are markdown files in
  Drive and the search that looked for them filtered to Google Docs.

The root cause is not carelessness about files. **Two systems assign numbers and
neither can see the other.** ChatGPT keeps a permanent register inside its daily
pack documents in Drive. Claude keeps numbers in this repository. Nothing
reconciles them, so the next collision is only a matter of time unless both
write here.

## The rules

1. **A rewrite keeps its number.** One idea, one number, whatever draft it is
   on. A rewritten GDC-037 is still GDC-037. Renumbering a rewrite is what
   caused most of the mess.
2. **A new post takes the next free number in this table**, and claims it here
   before a word is written.
3. **Status lives in the title of the Drive file**, never inside the body text.
   POSTED, READY, NEEDS REVIEW, PARKED.
4. **Never reuse a number that has been published**, even if the post was later
   superseded. A published number is spent for good.
5. **Check every file type.** The register has been held in Google Docs, in
   markdown files and in the repo. A search filtered to one of those will lie to
   you.

## What is still unverified

The **POSTED** rows are taken from a "done" marker inside each document. Only
Justin can confirm a post actually went out and on what date. A URL and a date
against each posted row would make this register trustworthy rather than merely
tidy, and would let us judge the posts on what they did.

A **planned** date is an intended running order, nothing more. Claude cannot
post and does not know whether a post went out, so a planned row stays planned
until Justin says otherwise. Only he moves a row to POSTED.

Note on 044: v2 reached Drive marked READY on 18 October carrying a claim that
did not hold, that the industry cannot agree whether the last ID spill was
seventy thousand documents or two million. The v2 document is in the Drive
trash and v4 replaces it. **A row saying ready means ready as of the newest
version named in it.** If a version number here is behind the pack, the pack
wins and the Drive document needs replacing before anything is posted.

## The Drive title rule (Justin, 23 October 2026)

**Every Drive document for a post that has not gone out carries NOT POSTED YET
in its title**, right after the number. He asked for it because READY does not
say what it sounds like: READY means nobody has checked it, and a title that
only says READY gives no clue whether a post is still waiting to go out.

The title shape is:

`<number> NOT POSTED YET GDC-<number> <short title>, CLAUDE REVIEWED <date>`

Only Justin changes NOT POSTED YET to POSTED, and only he moves the matching
register row. Claude never does either, because Claude cannot see LinkedIn and
cannot know whether a post went out.
