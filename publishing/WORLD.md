# The Guided Planet, the world

A small round green planet where every issue takes place. It is the positive,
discovery led side of the Guided Childhood philosophy: *delay where it helps,
protect where we can, prepare throughout.* The paper celebrates what children
make and discover with technology, and builds the judgement to go with it. It
is never anti screen, never an uncritical celebration of technology, never a
sales brochure.

## The six places

Every page belongs to one of them, and the map on page 2 shows all six.

| Place | What happens there | Its emblem |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Discovery Observatory** | Science, space and remarkable digital discoveries | A dome with a telescope |
| **The Maker District** | Coding, creativity, invention and young digital creators | Workshops with a gold gear |
| **The Thinking Forest** | AI, algorithms, misinformation and critical thinking | Two trees, one gold leaf |
| **The Connection Garden** | Friendship, kindness, communication and digital belonging | Two flowers whose stems join |
| **The Research Room** | What scientific evidence actually tells us about growing up with technology | A book and a magnifier |
| **The Family Explorer** | Practical activities families can try together | A compass |

The dotted gold pathway joins them, as it joins the stages in the app. It is the
one gold detail on any page.

## Bloop, the guide

Bloop is the paper's recurring guide: the friend who asks the interesting
question, introduces the activity, and goes off to investigate how something
works ("Bloop investigates" is a standing page). Bloop is canon from
`digi-squad/README.md` and is never redesigned here:

- Stage 2, ages 8 to 10, green, playful. Verb: Create.
- The running joke: always mid build, always one piece short. Pats pockets,
  finds it somewhere daft, carries on.
- When wrong: rebuilds rather than defends. "Right. Start again from the bit
  that worked."
- Never wins the game on screen, never makes a child feel slow.

The other friends appear one to a page where their stage fits: Pebble for
kindness and feelings (the Connection Garden), Orbit for questions that need
checking (the Observatory, the Thinking Forest, older readers), DiGi the golden
star for evidence (the Research Room). Nova and Cosmo are for teenagers and
stay out of a 7 to 12 paper unless a page is written for older readers. Every
friend is an approved file in `library/characters.json`.

## The standing pages

1. Cover: the night sky over the Guided Planet, a story to look up at.
2. Welcome: the editor's letter and the map.
3 to 9. Stories and activities, each in its place, at least one news story with
   an activity beside it, at least one research page, one Bloop investigation,
   one paper game that needs no account.
10. Five good things to explore together, all checked that month.
11. The Family Experiment: seven days, one small thing a day.
12. The Planet Passport, the next issue, every source, and one line for grown ups.

## Growing the world

New places are added by Justin, not by a session: a place needs an emblem in
`templates/art.ts`, a ground and tag in `design/newspaper.css`, an entry in
`DESTINATIONS` in `engine/schema.ts`, and a line here. The same places can
appear in the app and in lessons, because the art is code and the words are
data.
