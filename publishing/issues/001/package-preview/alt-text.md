# Alt text, Issue 001

- **Page 1, cover.** Night over the Guided Planet. The International Space Station glides across a sky full of stars, trailed by a dotted gold path, and Bloop stands on a green hill looking up at it.
- **Page 2, world-map.** A map of the Guided Planet floating in space, with six labelled places joined by a dotted gold path: the Observatory, the Maker District, the Thinking Forest, the Connection Garden, the Research Room and the Family Explorer.
- **Page 3, code-in-orbit.** The International Space Station on its dotted orbit above the curve of the Earth, beside a small computer whose grid of coloured lights shows a smiling pixel face.
- **Page 4, maker-skyline.** An illustrated street of workshops in the Maker District, with a big gold gear and lit windows in every building.
- **Page 7, friendship-valley.** Two homes on two green hills far apart, joined across the valley by a dotted gold line and a tin can telephone, with flowers growing in between.
- **Page 8, recommendation-machine.** A diagram of a recommendation machine. Four signals go in at the top, labelled watched, liked, skipped and searched. A guess comes out at the bottom, labelled next up.
- **Page 10, explorer-trail.** A dotted gold trail across a starry sky, stopping at five numbered places.
