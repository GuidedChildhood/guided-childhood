# The Guided Planet

## Issue 001: The Curiosity Edition, November 2026

A free illustrated newspaper for children aged 7 to 12 and the grown ups who read with them. Every month: the good side of screens, and the judgement to go with it.

Issue 001 is The Curiosity Edition. Children's code heading for the space station. Young inventors who started with a person who needed help. What scientists actually know about screen time. Friendships that travel further. And Become the Algorithm, a paper game about who really chose the next video.

Twelve A4 pages, ready to print in colour or in an ink saver version, with an accessible online edition and every source listed in full.
