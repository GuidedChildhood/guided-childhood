The Guided Planet

We made a newspaper. It is free, it is twelve pages, and it is about the good side of screens: children writing code that runs on the space station, young inventors fixing real problems for real people, and a paper game where your child gets to be the algorithm and works out how recommendations really work.

  • The children's code travelling above Earth (page 3)
  • Young inventors. Big ideas. A world of possibilities. (page 4)
  • Design your own invention (page 5)
  • Not all screen time tells the same story (page 6)
  • Sometimes a screen helps a friendship travel further (page 7)
  • Who chose the next video? You, or the algorithm? (page 8)
  • Become the Algorithm (page 9)
  • Five good things to explore together (page 10)
  • Seven days of curiosity (page 11)
  • Your Planet Passport (page 12)

Every fact has its source printed on the page, and none of the activities need a phone or an account. Print it, or read it together on a screen.
