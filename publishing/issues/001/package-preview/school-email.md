Subject: A free newspaper for your pupils: The Guided Planet, Issue 001

I am writing to share The Guided Planet, a free monthly newspaper for children aged 7 to 12 about the good side of screens.

Issue 001 covers ESA's Mission Zero (pupils' code running on the International Space Station, open until 22 March 2027), young inventors from Coolest Projects 2026, what the research says about different kinds of screen use, online friendship with safety qualifications, and a paper activity on recommendation algorithms that needs no devices or accounts.

Every page lists its sources. It is free to print, copy and share with your class. The algorithm activity touches the Education for a Connected World strand on managing online information for ages 7 to 11.

You are welcome to send it home with pupils or put it on your website.

Download: https://www.guidedchildhood.com/guided-planet/issue-001/guided-planet-001.pdf
Sources for every fact: https://www.guidedchildhood.com/guided-planet/issue-001/sources/

Justin Phillips, Editor in Chief, The Guided Planet
