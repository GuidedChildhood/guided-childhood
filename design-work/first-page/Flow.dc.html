Change step 4 as well

The six tiles are morning TV, controller fights, will not put it down, bedtime screens, mood after screens, and something else. Asking for a phone, online safety and starting the conversation were all folded into something else at some point.

So the biggest questions parents have cannot be answered back, because we stopped asking them. I would take it to nine tiles and add the phone question, social media and AI chatbots.

One number to fix today

The walkthrough tells a brand new parent there are sixty scripts. There are 335, and 90 of them are free. It is the worst place in the product to undersell, because it is the page directly before we ask for money.

The homepage has the same problem in a milder form: 160 scripts and 100 lessons against a real 335 and 147.

Why this page decides the sale

The two doors come straight after it. Whatever a parent believes when they reach that screen was built here, in the thirty seconds before it.

A tour of the features tells them what the app has. Their own three worries answered tells them what their life looks like on Friday. Only one of those is worth £7.99.
