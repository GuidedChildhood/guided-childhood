# Ad campaign council, 19 October 2026

Justin asked for three agents to research whether the ad campaign will work,
whether it looks good enough and not AI made, the best colours (not too much
gold), the best performing competitor ads, the most shareable part of our
service and the top problems on parent forums, and to keep talking until they
agreed. Run as one workflow: an ad performance researcher, a parent problems
researcher and a creative director researched in parallel, then a chair drafted
one brief and all three reviewed it, three rounds.

The full brief, every finding and 64 sources are in
`briefings/data/2026-10-19-ad-campaign-council.json`. This page is the short
version.

## Did they agree

On the direction, yes, from the first round. On every detail, not quite: after
round three each agent still raised small fixes (wording, crop sizes, one
measurement step). None of them contradicts another, so they are applied
below as part of the brief rather than run for a fourth round.

## The verdict

Not yet, and the fixes are cheap. The ads get the hardest thing right: real
app screens. They will not beat the competitors yet because:

1. **Wrong moments.** The script ad shows a 13 to 15 script to parents of 8 to
   13s. Nothing covers the most intense problems for this age.
2. **The hook is cut in half.** "Three questions, no sign up." is on 2 of 9
   cards, and tiny where it is.
3. **Too much gold, counted in touches.** Five or six butter touches per card
   against our own one gold detail rule. Ad 02 is about two thirds butter.
4. **Things that read as templated or AI.** The 3D Orbit in the same spot on
   four cards (the renders were made with an image generator), the sheen and
   glow, the 9:41 clock, callouts that repeat the screen.
5. **Copy that breaks our gates.** The stage check page says "a safe, capable
   16" and "the screen battle". "Five more minutes?" is now Kairoo's and
   Ohana's phrase.

## The top parent problems (forums, in their words, sourced in the data file)

1. Switching off mid game, gaming rage, and the parent who caves.
2. The first phone decision and the fear of being left out (Year 6 to 7).
3. Snapchat and TikTok pressure because plans are made there.
4. Unkind class group chats in Years 6 and 7.
5. The phone in the bedroom at night.
6. Parental controls that fail or get got round.
7. Finding porn or disturbing content on a 10 to 12 year old's device.
8. Feeling like the bad guy and a failing parent.
9. Another adult undoes the rules.
10. "Addicted", where nothing else interests them.

## What wins (patterns, with the evidence level in the data file)

- Exact words for one named moment. This is how Good Inside grew without paid
  media.
- Plain, native looking creative over polish. Looking AI made costs attention.
- Pain point statements open most long running ads.
- A quiz funnel whose ad says plainly what you get.
- Spread through parent WhatsApp groups (Smartphone Free Childhood).
- Do not copy: borrowed proof (user counts, stars, press logos).

## The most shareable part of the service

**The script**: one named moment, "Say this" against "Not this", free on a
public `/say/<id>` page a parent can forward to the school WhatsApp group. The
star quest is the reason to buy, so it is the conversion ad, not the share
hook. This is inference from the product and similar cases, not our own data,
and today it cannot be measured: `/say/` sends strangers to `/starter-pack`
with no source code.

## Colour and look

- **Cream is the ground** (about 55%), ink headlines, **espresso button**
  (`.btn-ink`, white text), ink soft for the sub and hook line.
- **Butter is one mark, never a surface**: the underline under the moment words
  on cream, or the button on an ink card, never both, plus the logo. Total
  butter on a finished card, including inside the screen, at or under 10%.
  Never a background, never text, never under another brand's name.
- **No 3D characters on paid ads to parents.** They stay for organic and child
  facing work.
- **Round 1 uses flat screen crops, no phone frame**, one fixed slot at or
  under 30% of the card, 2.5px ink edge. No sheen, glow, tilted callouts or
  9:41 clock.
- **Headline 88 to 96px**, at most 3 lines, a statement naming one moment. The
  only question on the card is the CTA. Every card ends with "What stage is
  your child?" and, directly under it, "Three questions, no sign up."
- A real person only: Justin's own hand, Justin to camera, or a consenting real
  family member.

## Fixes from the final review round, applied

- Boost only posts that won organically; never add an unproven creative to a
  paid set to make up a number.
- Script crops run from SAY THIS to the end of its quote, one continuous run,
  so the words are readable in the feed and the title does not repeat the
  headline.
- The jobs first crop runs from the waiting job row down to the My time card,
  so the headline's "jobs" is on screen.
- No age corner on Round 1 cards; the screen's own label is the only one.
- The screen slot is sized to the 30% cap (about 760 by 575).
- The mid match script still says "I will give you the warning at the end".
  That contradicts the 12 October position ("the ending is agreed, not
  warned"), so it needs Justin's copy edit before it leads an ad.
