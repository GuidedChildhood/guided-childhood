# Where Phones Sleep: the kitchen charging station and fridge pack, plan for Justin's yes

Written 6 November 2026. Justin: "build some good fridge printouts also with
signs for where to charge phone devices in kitchen".

## Why this pack, and why it is not a copy

Nothing in `content/printables/` covers the charging station, and the
Passport already tells families to build one. The Pebble page's doing is "Set
up a charging station in the kitchen or hallway tonight", and the Bloop page's
doing is "Decide together where devices sleep". This pack is the kit for
those two lines: the signs, the labels and the fridge pages that keep the
habit going after the first night.

How it differs from what we sell:

- **The Family Phone Agreement** is a one-off ceremony for one child of 10 to
  12 getting a first phone. This pack covers the whole house, every night,
  grown ups included, and has no ceremony and no promises to sign.
- **Earn Your Screen Time and the Star Chart** are about earning minutes. This
  pack only shows the stars on tonight's plan (one star is five minutes) and
  is about where screens go, not how they are earned.
- **The Calm Down Corner Kit** is about feelings. There is no overlap.
- **The Jobs Board and Table Talk cards** (claimed in PR 1425) are a jobs
  chart and conversation cards. The dinner sign here is only the phones in
  the basket rule, with no question cards.

## Contents (one listing, 10 pages)

DiGi fronts the pack, since it is for the whole family. The friends appear
where a page is for the child.

1. **Cover**
2. **Two minute parent guide**: set the station up tonight, pick the docking
   time together, put your own phone there first, and what to say when it
   slips. It has one sourced line: the UK Chief Medical Officers (2019) advise
   leaving phones outside the bedroom at bedtime. We make no claim about what
   the rule will do for a child.
3. **Devices Sleep Here**, the station poster: A4 portrait, the friends
   asleep next to their docked devices. Goes on the wall above the chargers.
4. **The family rule, as a stand up sign**: "In our house, phones charge in
   the kitchen, not the bedroom." It folds into a tent card to stand by the
   chargers.
5. **The bedtime charging line**, for the fridge: a row for each person,
   grown ups too, with their name, their docking time and their wake up time
   ("back after breakfast and dressed"). It has a box to tick each night for
   one week.
6. **Charging spot labels**: 12 labels to cut out, one for each family member
   (a friend or DiGi with a blank name), to stick on the shelf or tray. There
   are also 12 cable flags to fold round a lead, so no one takes the wrong
   charger.
7. **Tonight's screen plan**, a wipe clean fridge page for every day. It
   shows who, what, how many stars (one star is five minutes), when it ends,
   and what we do next. Laminate it or put it in a plastic sleeve.
8. **Screen free meal times**: a "Phones in the basket" dinner sign, a label
   for the front of the basket, and a small table tent.
9. **Our family tech rules on one page**, the fridge poster. It has six
   lines, with house rules already printed that a family can keep or cross
   out (devices sleep in the kitchen, no phones at meals, screens go down
   when the timer goes, ask before a new app, grown ups too) and blank lines
   for their own.
10. **The pack-finish.js last page**: the Passport stickers for the Pebble
    and Bloop pages, under the doing, then the free Starter Pack at
    /etsy-bonus, plus one line that the app has a charging shelf in the Den
    (no link, no price).

Formats: A4 and US Letter in colour, an ink friendly version, and a phone set
(the bedtime line and tonight's plan, so a family can send them to the
family chat). Approved cutouts only, from `public/digi-squad/friends/` and
the DiGi star. A small footer URL goes on the fridge pages, per the skill.
