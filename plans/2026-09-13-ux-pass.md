## What the inventory found

Counted on 11 September 2026, so the numbers are a baseline, not a guess.

- 157 parent screens, 32 school screens, 318 components.
- 10,978 inline `style={{` across 569 files. That is the mass of the platform.
- Colour, type and font are fully tokenised. `var(--ink)` alone appears 3,723
  times. Change a colour token and the whole platform moves with it.
- Shape is not tokenised, and that is the whole opportunity:
  - `border: '2px solid var(--ink)'` typed out by hand 1,021 times.
  - `boxShadow: '0 4px 0 var(--ink)'` 429 times, plus 32 at 3px, 19 at 5px and
    one at 2px, all meaning the same thing.
  - Eleven different corner radii in the parent app. The top five are 16px
    (344 uses counting the unquoted form), 100px (314), 14px (305), 12px (251)
    and 20px (176). Then 18, 13, 10, 11, 22 and 8.
  - `--radius-btn: 16px` and `--radius-card: 24px` already exist in
    shared/tokens.css. They are used in nine places in the whole codebase.

So the platform already has a shape language. It just was never written down,
and every screen re invented it from memory.

## The move: tokenise shape first

One reviewable change, zero wiring risk, and every later tweak becomes one line.

Add to shared/tokens.css:

```
--radius-pill: 100px;   /* chips, badges, tab bar */
--radius-btn:  16px;    /* already there, keep it */
--radius-card: 20px;    /* cards and panels */
--radius-tile: 14px;    /* the small tiles inside a card */
--edge:  2px solid var(--ink);
--lift:  0 4px 0 var(--ink);
--lift-deep: 0 5px 0 var(--ink);
--lift-press: 0 2px 0 var(--ink);
```

Then sweep, in this order, with a scripted find and replace and a read of every
diff hunk:

1. `border: '2px solid var(--ink)'` becomes `border: 'var(--edge)'`. 1,021 hits,
   mechanical, safe.
2. The four ink drop shadows become `var(--lift)` and friends. 481 hits.
3. The radii collapse: 18, 20 and 22 become `var(--radius-card)`; 13, 14 and 12
   become `var(--radius-tile)`; 10, 11 and 8 become `var(--radius-tile)` only
   where they sit on a tile, otherwise leave them, because a 10px radius on a
   progress bar is not a card.
4. 100px and 16px become their tokens.

After the sweep, eleven radii are three, and the platform can be re dressed by
editing eight lines. That is what makes the rest of Sunday affordable.

Do not sweep `border-radius: 50%`. A circle is a circle.

## Schools is a different constraint, not a smaller version

A phone is a reading distance of 30cm. A projector is six metres with the
lights half up and a teacher stood at the back. The same type scale cannot
serve both, so schools gets its own.

- A second scale in schools, anchored one step up: nothing under 16px body,
  lesson text at 20px and up, a lesson title that survives being thrown on a
  wall.
- A presenter width. The lesson player should hold its line length at 1920
  instead of stretching to the window, because a full width line is unreadable
  from the back of a classroom.
- Tabs and curriculum are the two Justin named. The curriculum tree needs to
  read as units and lessons at a glance, closer to Teachable's nesting than to
  a flat list, and the class view needs the one number a teacher wants before
  the bell, closer to SchoolAI's mastery panel.
- Check every school screen at 390 as well. A head teacher reads the pitch on
  a phone on the train.

## The camera

`scripts/ux-shots.mjs`, added 11 September 2026. It walks every /dev fixture and
the marketing pages at 390, 1024 and 1920 and writes a PNG each.

```
npx next build && npx next start -p 3200
node scripts/ux-shots.mjs before
  ... do the work ...
node scripts/ux-shots.mjs after
```

Run `before` as the first thing on Sunday, because the container is new and
nothing from Friday survives in it. Output is gitignored on purpose: the point
is the comparison in the session, not 270 PNGs in the repo.

## What must not change

This is a look and feel pass. If a pull request touches any of these, it has
gone wrong:

- Any route, any API handler, any migration.
- Any DiGi prompt or pathway logic. Never allow or deny stays true.
- The check in scoring, the streak rules, the star bank arithmetic.
- The paywall, the founder cap of 50, or where /join sends people.

The gates are the same as always: `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx next build`,
`npm run wiring` at zero new, the dash grep over the staged diff, and both
widths in Chrome DevTools before anything is called done.

## The one thing to decide on the day

Whether the ink border drops from 2px to 1.5px. It is the single change that
would make the whole platform read as more expensive, and it is also the change
most likely to make Justin say it lost its character. Do it behind the `--edge`
token, look at it at both widths, and ask him with a before and after picture
rather than deciding alone.
