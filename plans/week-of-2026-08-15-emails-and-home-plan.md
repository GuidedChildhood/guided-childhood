## 1. Planet Friends in emails, and NOT in all of them

Justin, 15 August 2026: "only is some of the emails relating to emails that need
to have them in so not all emails."

**This is the constraint that shapes the whole job.** The instinct with a new
block is to put it everywhere, and it would be wrong here for a reason worth
writing down: a character that appears on every email stops being a character
and becomes furniture. The Friends mean something on the child's side because
they are earned, one per stage. Stapling one to a trial reminder or a receipt
spends that meaning for decoration.

So the test each email has to pass before it gets art:

- Is this email ABOUT the thing the Friends belong to, the child's journey, the
  stages, the passport, the daily habit? Then a Friend is the messenger and
  belongs.
- Is it about money, the account, a deadline, or a single administrative fact?
  Then it does not. No art on trial ending, on the founder offer, on anything
  with a price in it. A cartoon beside a charge reads as softening bad news.
- Does it go to a LEAD who has no child and no stage yet? Then there is no
  "their" Friend to show, and a random one is a stock illustration.

Emails with no art are the default. Art is the exception that has to earn its
place.

Justin, 15 August 2026: "in email chain at some point we needs research emails

Not started, and deliberately not designed here beyond the shape, because it is
a content job as much as a build one.

What is already true and must be reused rather than rebuilt:

- The six day floor in `lib/email/floor.ts` means one email a week from ALL

  for more weeks.
- `.claude/skills/kids-research` is the six lens researcher with adversarial
  citation checking. Any claim in an email about children and screens has to
  survive it, because an email is the one place a wrong citation cannot be
  quietly edited later.

run to, and does the research email REPLACE a week of the existing programme or
