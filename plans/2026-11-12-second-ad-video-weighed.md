# The second ad video, weighed (12 November 2026)

Justin pasted a talk, "the seven new rules of digital marketing", from an
agency founder (https://youtu.be/xsdoHywBD0I), and asked to take the best of
it and of the first talk into a routine that gathers feedback and keeps our
adverts ahead of the competitors'. Outside advice, so the feedback filter
first. The speaker sells agency services and quotes his clients' sales
($10.2 billion); none of his numbers is checked here and none goes in our copy.

## What both talks agree on, and goes into the routine

1. A reviewer scores every advert before a human sees it (talk 1). Built: the gate.
2. Copy the structure of the proven adverts, never the message (talk 1, and our
   ad studio from 19 October). Built: the swipe file and the nine rules.
3. A broad audience; the words find the people (talk 2).
4. One niche until it is exhausted (talk 2).
5. The loop: make, test, read the numbers, keep the winner, vary it, again
   (both). This is what was missing. It is now Phase 7 of the ad studio skill,
   run every Monday.
