# 24 November 2026: Oak's RSHE online lessons against our 34

Read only, from Oak's open API (open-api.thenational.academy, subject
rshe-pshe, units and lesson titles for KS1 to KS4), pulled 24 November 2026.
Titles only so far: no lesson content was read yet, so nothing here goes into
marketing until the lessons themselves have been checked. Oak's content is
under the Open Government Licence; any use on our pages credits Oak.

## The shape
- Oak RSHE has 73 units across Years 1 to 11. 15 are about online life
  ("Our online lives" and "Media influence"), with 71 lessons.
- Oak has no units listed for Year 6 or Year 9, Year 5 has one unit (not
  online), and there is nothing for Reception or the sixth form.
- Ours: 34 lessons, Reception to Year 13, every one about growing up online,
  each with the words to say on every slide, a parent note home, printables
  and a record.
