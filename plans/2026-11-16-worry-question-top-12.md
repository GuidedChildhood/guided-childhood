# The worry question: the twelve parents actually post about

Justin, 16 November 2026, with screenshots of the stage check worry grid and
the pathway is ready card: review it against what parents raise on Reddit,
Mumsnet and Facebook, top 12 in order, each with a solution; Apple level, not
off putting, clear that we bring the solutions. Then: do not break the
platform. This screen only collects the first worries; the app keeps asking,
logging and bringing them up each day until solved, through DiGi and its own
age prompts (first phone, Switch, WhatsApp, time).

## Evidence

`content/parent-problem-log.md` (14 November, Mumsnet) plus a 16 November
sweep of UK surveys and Ofcom (More in Common 2025, Uswitch 2026, Ofcom
Growing Up Gaming 2026, Children's Media Lives 2026, Ipsos 2025, Internet
Matters). Reddit, Netmums and Facebook groups could not be read; that gap is
stated, not filled.

## The twelve, in order

Coming off screens; Asking for a phone; Who they talk to in games; What they
come across online; Social media; Bedtime screens; Group chats; They get round
the rules; Homework on screens; Endless YouTube; Spending in games; AI
chatbots. Then Something else.

Folded, not deleted: Controller fights and Mood after screens (parents describe
both as the switch off fight), Morning TV (ages 4 to 7 only, parents split).
They move to RETIRED_WORRIES, so every family who already picked one keeps its
label, slug, answer, check in row and focus strip.

## What does not change

The ids still become concerns through ONBOARDING_TO_SLUG, rated at the daily
check in, read by DiGi and Home, exactly as now. New ids get a slug, a label, a
challenge label, a pathway key and an answer in the same commit
(check-focus-labels holds it). No migration.

## The look

Each tile: the drawing, the parent's words, and under it one line of what we
bring. The question asks what they would like help with. Mobbin: Flo ("What
can we help you do?"), Lloyds (a line under each goal), Lifesum ("This helps
us personalise").
