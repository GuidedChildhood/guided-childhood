# Problem then solution homepage, and a lesson per stage to try

Justin, 26 November 2026, after the Mumsnet thread and two research passes.

## Decided

1. Parents stay the main model (£12.99 a month, founder £7.99, annual £99).
   The research recommended schools first; Justin chose parents first. Schools
   carry on as they are.
2. The homepage shows one problem and its fix per section, scrolling (not an
   auto flashing carousel, which parents miss), in this order:
   1. What school teaches this term, so you get there first, with a lesson to try
   2. Jobs earn screen time (the child app, the daily allowance, the timer)
   3. The road to 16 and the Passport
   4. DiGi that remembers your family (back in; the pricing review says this
      is the part a parent is most likely to pay for, and the 25 November
      rebuild cut it)
   5. Homework help and the school calendar
   6. Safe settings by age (last, because it is free elsewhere)
   "Road to 16" stays as the frame, not the headline word.
3. One real lesson per stage opens without sign up, with a short walkthrough of
   how a lesson works at home.

## Why

- Our numbers: 0 paying families ever, 30 real accounts, 56 leads.
- Mumsnet: 42 visitors, 10 finished the check, 0 sign ups. Too small to mean
  anything on its own (0 from 10 at 4 percent happens about 2 times in 3), but
  "basic parenting" and "did I need an app for that" are a real signal: advice
  is free elsewhere, a tool is not.
- Not free anywhere: what school teaches this term, the child app where jobs
  earn screen time, the Passport, DiGi with memory.
- Each section answers a problem in a parent's words with a real screen, so the
  page proves the tool rather than describing advice.

## Build order

1. Homepage sections, problem then fix, with the existing hero kept.
2. Public lesson taster per stage on the parent site (route and allowlist from
   the platform map), linked from section 1.
3. A short walkthrough of how a lesson works at home.

## Not doing now

- Changing prices or the trial length (the research suggested a cheaper annual
  and a longer trial; parked until the homepage and tasters are live).
