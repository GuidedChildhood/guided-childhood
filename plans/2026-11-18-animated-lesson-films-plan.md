## Settled already

## Step 1: the script panel (read only, no credits)

Four reviewers run in parallel on the 1.4 script, each from one lens:

1. **Curriculum.** Is the message right and complete for ages 4 to 7, measured
   against the statutory RSHE rows in `shared/schools-rshe-2026.ts`, Education
   for a Connected World and KS1 computing (using technology safely, where to
   go for help)? What is taught, what is missing, and what is wrong.
2. **Children's TV writer.** Does it hold a four year old: the hook, the joke,
   the participation, the pace, and a reason to love Pebble and DiGi?
3. **Safeguarding and evidence.** Nothing frightening, no claim without a
   source, the never in trouble promise kept, help routes correct.
4. **Animation director.** A shot list: which beats get full motion, where
   real B roll helps (never a synthetic person presented as real), the
   continuity rules, and the credit cost.

I merge their findings into one revised script and one shot list. Every point
goes through the feedback filter: adopt, adapt, park or decline, with the
reason.

## Step 2: Justin approves the script and the budget

The budget is decided before anything is generated. 8.75 credits a shot on
Kling 3.0 pro. Fully animated is about 440 credits; key moments only is about
90 credits.

## Step 3: build, check and show

Isla records every line, and each line is checked by transcript. The shots go
on a contact sheet against the reference art, and anything off model is
regenerated. Then Justin watches it. Nothing goes into the app or onto
YouTube without his yes.
