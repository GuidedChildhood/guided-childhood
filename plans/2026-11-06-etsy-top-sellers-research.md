# Etsy top selling printables in our customer's aisle

6 November 2026. Research only, nothing built. Goal: copy the formats that
sell (structure, counts, price points, what reviews praise), never anyone's
design, art, wording or characters.

## How the evidence was gathered

- Etsy blocks fetch tools, so every listing was read in the browser pane on
  etsy.com/uk, logged out, essential cookies only, on 6 November 2026. Searches
  used the digital download filter. 26 searches, 33 listing pages opened.
- Every number below was SEEN ON THE LISTING unless marked UNVERIFIED.
  "Reviews" means the listing's own "Reviews for this item" count. "Shop" is
  the shop's lifetime sales line. Prices are what a UK visitor sees, already
  including the seller's sale.
- Bestseller is Etsy's badge for recent sales velocity, not size: it sat on
  listings with as few as 3 reviews. Review count plus shop sales is the
  stronger demand signal, so the ranking leans on those.
- Low star reviews are rare on these listings (shops average 4.8 to 4.9), so
  complaints are thin. Where none were visible we say so, and we note what
  sellers pre empt in their descriptions, which is where past complaints show.
- Listing URLs are etsy.com/uk/listing/ followed by the number given.

## 4. Bundle and pricing lessons

- **Price points seen.** Single charts sell at £1.49 to £3.00 after sale.
  Kits and card systems sell at £4.55 to £7.00. Specialist systems reach £18.
  Seasonal "older kids" hunts hold £5.82 while Christmas colouring sits at
  £1.41. Our £4 floor puts us in the kit band, so every listing must look
  like a kit: a count in the title and a contents flat lay in slot 1.
- **Permanent sales are the norm.** Top listings show 25 to 50 percent off
  (row 1 at 25, rows 2, 3 and 4 at 50, row 10 at 30, row 14 at 40). Our
  permanent 25 percent is in line, and on the modest end.
- **Counts sell.** Winning titles open on a number: 210 cards, 215 cards,
  50 posters, 52 cards, 24 pages, 110 activities. The 24 card set drew
  "expecting more". Put our count first in every title.
- **Bundles are cheap and cross linked.** The 251k shop sells each part at
  about £1.50 and the mega bundle at £2.81, linking each part to the bundle
  in its description. Bundles price at roughly two singles, not a premium.
  For us: list the parts, then a bundle near the price of two parts.
- **Editable is table stakes.** Almost every winner offers a Canva link
  ("works with a free Canva account"), a fillable PDF, filled and blank
  versions, and both A4 and Letter. Fixed only sets sit lower (the JPG only
  £18 set at 88 percent recommend). Keep our fixed PDF for quality, add a
  fillable name and text layer, and say plainly in the first lines what is
  editable.
- **Photos.** Strong listings fill 10 to 12 image slots plus a short video
  (rows 3, 4, 5, 7, 10 all show 11 or 12 images and a video). Newer listings
  with 4 images trail. Fill all slots and add the video.
- **Slot 1.** Two patterns win: a contents collage with the count in big
  type (the routine card systems, the 52 card deck, the 24 clue hunt), or a
  filled example with a child's name on it (the teen chore chart shows a
  named chart). Ours: a filled example with a Planet Friend, a name and the
  count, on a real table.
- **Breadth beats one hero.** The biggest shops (196k, 251k, 79k sales) win
  with many variants of one format, each its own listing. Once a format earns
  reviews, list its variants (age band, colourway, classroom version) rather
  than a new idea.
