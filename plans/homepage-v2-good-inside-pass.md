# Homepage v2 — the Good Inside pass

Justin's brief, 12 Aug 2026, from goodinside.com screenshots against our live
page: bigger text in places, keep the characters but add real family
photography, adapt Good Inside's copy patterns, surface the concrete services
(tips, star rewards, school calendar, chores earn device time, lessons in
social media, devices and AI), say science backed without naming researchers
on the front page, and make the whole page the thing a parent worried about
devices, social media and AI would sign up for. Premium, never AI looking.

## A. Type scale (bigger where it sells)

1. Hero H1: clamp goes from (2.5rem, 4.4vw, 3.5rem) to (2.9rem, 5.2vw, 4.2rem).
   Good Inside's headline dominates the first screen; ours should too.
2. Hero subline: 1.05rem minimum, up from text-lg's current render.
3. Section H2s: floor rises from ~1.5rem to 1.9rem, ceiling to 2.8rem.
4. Body copy in the two column sections: .94rem to 1.02rem.
5. The three act labels and stat numbers: one step up.

## B. Copy patterns adapted from Good Inside (theirs → ours)

1. "Parent confidently. Raise confident kids." → our two beat line under the
   hero or as section two's lead: **"Parent calmly. Raise a child who's ready."**
2. "The expert in your pocket" → the DiGi section heading becomes
   **"The expert in your pocket at 11 o'clock."** (Justin: expert in your
   pocket is good here.) The 11 o'clock story stays as the body.
3. "Get started in under 3 minutes" under CTA → ours:
   **"Three questions. Two minutes. Free."** under Start for free.
4. Their NOW / SOON / LATER / ALWAYS method timeline → ours becomes four beats:
   **TONIGHT** (the hallway rule, one calmer bedtime) · **THIS TERM** (the
   fights shrink, the conversations open) · **AT 16** (social media with a
   stamped passport, ready not released) · **ALWAYS** ("I trust myself as a
   parent again."). Replaces the current three act cards with the same content
   plus the ALWAYS close.
5. Their rating line (4.9/5.0, 13,589 reviews) → we do NOT fake numbers. Our
   proof line stays the three real parents plus the founding families counter
   once real: "Built with our first 50 founding families."
6. Their "Featured in" press bar → ours is the honest equivalent we already
   have: aligned with NHS and NSPCC guidance, Online Safety Act, DfE, Ofcom.
   Rendered as a quiet logo style strip under the hero, not buried in the footer.

## C. Science backed, not professor heavy

Front page: remove the four researcher name cards. Replace with one strip:
**"Science backed. Every lesson and every DiGi answer is checked against the
research weekly."** plus a quiet link "See the evidence" to /evidence where
the named bench (Odgers, Orben, Przybylski, Livingstone) lives in full.
The hero proof bar loses the names too: "Built on the research. Aligned with
NHS and NSPCC guidance."

## D. Features said plainly (the sign up drivers)

The inventory section becomes benefit first, one card per worry, in this order:

1. **Daily tips that fit tonight** · a script and a tip for the exact fight
   you are having, ten minutes a day, catch up whenever life happens.
2. **Chores earn screen time** · your child ticks real jobs in their own app,
   stars land in their star bank, stars buy agreed device time. The fight ends.
3. **Balance, watched together** · safe device settings by age matched with
   the reward star system, outside play pays the most stars.
4. **Lessons in social media, gaming and AI** · age by age lessons so they can
   spot an algorithm, a fake and an AI chatbot long before 16.
5. **School calendar reminders** · term dates, homework rhythms and school
   activity messages home, so the plan matches real weeks.
6. **The Digital Passport** · every stage stamped, tap each page open, done by 16.

SEO note: these six headings carry the exact query phrases parents type
(screen time rewards, chores for screen time, AI literacy for kids, social
media lessons for children, safe device settings by age, school calendar app).
