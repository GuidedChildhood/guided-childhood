# 22 November 2026: the French scheme's pupil games, rechecked

Justin: recheck her platform, she has added games from children's feedback, we
could model similar. Her games sites (Year 3, Year 4 and the easier Inclusion
sites) were read on 22 November. We already link to them from the unit pages
and the print room; nothing of hers is copied here.

## What she has added
- 14 games per site, all on the words the class has met: colour the alien,
  what colour is it, my favourite colour, put the words in order, finish the
  sentence, can you, oui or non, how are you, how old are you, fill the gap,
  four phonics games, a word mat that speaks, and the songs.
- **Two ways to play every game:** Practice (no timer, try again until right)
  and a 45 second Round (stars, best score kept on that device only).
- **Instant feedback, tuned from the class:** a right answer moves on fast and
  is not read again; a wrong one shows and says the right answer (her change,
  19 November).
- **Marie v Pierre:** a two player game with a grown up. Both see the same
  pictures; first to tap the word they hear wins the point; first to 10.
- **Challenge:** one folder that mixes every unit's words (retrieval practice).
- **Easier sites:** two pictures, bigger, no timer, no score, first letter given.
- **Privacy:** no names, no sign in, nothing saved but a best score on the device.
- **Sound:** her own recordings first, the computer's French voice only where
  a recording is missing.

## What is worth modelling, and where
1. **Practice or Round on every game**, best score on the device only. Fits our
   no pupil data rule exactly. Our digital lessons and the parent app.
2. **Me v my grown up**, the two player game, as a parent app feature: a child
   and parent race to the right answer on the week's topic. It is "screens are
   better together" made into a game, and it gives a parent a reason to open
   the app with their child.
3. **A home games page per French unit, ours**, with Nova and Bloop and the
   native recordings from the films pilot, in place of linking out to her site.
4. **A Challenge mix** at the end of each half term (retrieval practice).
5. **An easier mode** on every game for SEND: two choices, no timer, no score.
