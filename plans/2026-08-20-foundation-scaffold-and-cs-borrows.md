# Foundation stage: the child scaffold and the Common Sense borrows

Written 20 August 2026. An ADDENDUM to plans/road-to-16-curriculum-plan.md, not a replacement. That
plan is the source of truth for the 4 strand by 5 stage ladder, the Rosenshine format and the DB
mapping, and the Foundation co watch set (parent_lessons 1.1 to 1.10, migrations 049 and 050) already
exists. This doc adds only the three things the Common Sense review (plans/2026-08-19-common-sense-
curriculum-review.md) found we were missing at the young ages. No dashes.

## Why this exists

The review's clearest finding: Common Sense's strongest, most researched work is K to 5, and their
one real advantage over us at that age is a single memorable child facing scaffold, Rings of
Responsibility (Self, Community, World). Our road to 16 ladder has excellent strands and outcomes but
no single spine a five year old carries in their head. This adds ours. And unlike Rings, ours is
machine first and connection first from the very first lesson, which is exactly the axis the market
leader is criticised for missing.

---

## 1. THE SCAFFOLD: Notice, Choose, Tell

Three words. A child can hold them at four, and they grow in depth to sixteen. DiGi teaches them, and
each Planet Friend carries them a little further. They are our answer to Rings of Responsibility, and
they are deliberately different: Rings moves outward from the self to the world, ours moves from
seeing the machine, to owning your choice, to keeping an adult in the room.

- **NOTICE.** Screens are made to hold on to you, and not everything on one is real. Notice what it is
  doing. (Machine first. This is the differentiator. A four year old learns a picture can be pretend,
  an eleven year old learns how the algorithm builds a feed. Same word, deeper each year.)
- **CHOOSE.** You bring the stop. The screen never ends on its own, so you decide. (Agency and
  balance. The timer is the rule at four, running your own permissions at eleven. Screen time is
  earned, not granted, which is our star quest in one word.)
- **TELL.** There is always a grown up to tell, and you will not be in trouble. (Connection is the
  protection. The never share list and telling every time at four, a rehearsed telling plan at eight,
  block and leave and tell at eleven.)

How it overlays the existing road to 16 strands, so nothing is renamed:
- Notice carries the AI and chatbots seeds and the Social media ready seeds (real or made up, spotting
  the sell, the algorithm).
- Choose carries Healthy balance (the stop, sleep, play, people) and the agency half of Safe online.
- Tell carries the Safe online telling half (trusted adult, never share list, report and leave).

The scaffold is a spine, not a fourth strand. Every young lesson already in the ladder gets tagged
with its word, and the child sees the word, not the strand name. DiGi says it, the recap slide says
it, the family agreement is built on it.

### Growth by stage (the same three words, deeper)

| | NOTICE | CHOOSE | TELL |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Foundation, Pebble, 4 to 7** | a picture can be pretend, a screen voice may not be a real person | the timer is the rule, I can stop, screens sleep outside the bedroom | tell a grown up every time something feels wrong, the never share list |
| **Builder, Bloop, 8 to 10** | spot when a screen is selling, AI learns from examples and gets things wrong | what screens push out, sleep play people, strong passwords | a rehearsed telling plan, no fear of losing the device |
| **Explorer, Orbit, 11 to 13 (the bridge into the 13+ set)** | how the algorithm builds a feed, filters are not faces | notice what the feed does to your mood, run your own permissions | mute, leave, report and tell on every app they use |

Each cell is already an outcome in road to 16. This table only pins the word to it.

## 2. THE FAMILY AGREEMENT, age banded (our answer to Common Sense FACE)

Common Sense's most borrowed asset is the Family Media Agreement, grade banded and printable. We
already promise an agreement you write together. This makes a young version, built on Notice, Choose,
Tell, so the scaffold and the agreement are one thing.

- **Foundation agreement (4 to 7).** Six picture lines a child can sign with a drawing. Notice: I know
  a screen can pretend. Choose: when the timer sings, I stop, and screens sleep in the kitchen. Tell:
  if something feels wrong I tell you, and I will not be in trouble. Parent side: I model it, phone
  away at dinner and bedtime.
- **Builder agreement (8 to 10).** Same three headings, more words the child writes themselves. Adds
  passwords, the five second check before sharing, and where the device charges at night.
- Printable in the house style through the printables engine, and multilingual is a natural fit given
  the family's Spanish, one Spanish version to start. This is a Service Friday asset and a schools
  handout, and it maps to the existing Family Media Agreement idea without copying their wording.

## 3. THE SCHOOLS BORROWS

Three things the review found Common Sense does well for the classroom sale that we should match, all
additive to the existing school decks, none a rebuild.

- **A 20 minute drop in version of each young lesson.** Our school modules run about 55 minutes. The
  market now expects a 20 minute mini lesson that drops into assembly, tutor time or a PSHE slot. Add a
  short version alongside each Foundation and Builder lesson, same Notice Choose Tell spine, one idea,
  one activity, one catchphrase.
- **SEL mapping alongside EFCW and RSHE.** Map each young lesson to PSHE Association and to the CASEL
  social and emotional competencies as well as the existing UKCIS EFCW and RSHE anchors, because the
  SEL frame widens the buyer, especially internationally, and Notice Choose Tell already reads as self
  awareness, self management and relationship skills.
- **A one page Implementation Guide for a primary.** A scope and sequence and a roll out plan a head or
  PSHE lead can act on in a minute, which is table stakes for schools and which Common Sense has and we
  do not. Lives in schools/, references the ladder, does not restructure it.

## 4. WHAT THIS IS NOT

- Not a rewrite of road to 16. That plan stays the source of truth for outcomes, format and DB.
- Not new lessons invented from scratch. Every young lesson already exists as a row or a planned row
  in the ladder. This tags them with a word and adds the agreement and the schools wrappers.
- Not a copy of Common Sense. Notice Choose Tell is ours and is structurally different from Rings. The
  family agreement borrows the idea of an age banded printable contract, never their wording. Their
  curriculum is Creative Commons non commercial no derivatives, we learn at the level of ideas only.
