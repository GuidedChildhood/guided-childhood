# Five free printables, the Happy News lead magnets

Justin, 13 October 2026: "start a plan for more printables, really top of the
market ones we can add for children's activities, that super cool Happy News
style. I want 5 new ones that print PDF perfect that I can share on social
media as free lead magnets, so complete Happy News design. Maybe a newspaper
also in Happy News style which teaches using screens for kids. A booklet maybe.
Please review all top competitors to CREATE THE BEST FREEBIE lead magnets."

Status: APPROVED by Justin, 13 October 2026, every recommended option:
the five in this order; the newspaper is The Planet Post; four A4 pages plus
an A5 booklet file (A4 and US Letter); pages preview free, the PDF by email.
Build order: The Planet Post first, then the other four, each its own PR.

## The rules this plan is held to

- THE-STORY.md: each freebie puts one real mechanic on paper and ends at the
  stage check, *What stage is your child? Three questions, no sign up*, at
  `/starter-pack` (non negotiable 9). The star quest leads any list of what is inside.
- The PRINTABLE is full Happy News (white paper, ink lines, crayon fills,
  painted rainbow masthead, ribbons, bursts, smiley dots, Nunito 900), drawn in
  code from `components/kid/HappyNewsBits.tsx`, `shared/happy-news.ts` and
  `components/printables/drawn/HappyPaper.tsx`. Their energy, our drawing, copy nothing.
- The SOCIAL CARD around it follows `brand/GDC_SOCIAL_VISUAL_SYSTEM.md` format 3
  (cream, editorial, the real page preview as hero, no sticker scatter), since
  The Happy Newspaper was retired as a social reference on 5 October.
- Approved cutouts only (`public/digi-squad/friends/`, `DiGi-star.svg`), one
  friend per page, demonstrating. No invented screens. No dashes, UK English,
  Justin's voice, `npm run ai-tells` clean, no outcome or medical claims.
- Not rebuilt, only reused: the seven drawn sheets, the family phone
  agreement, earn your screen time, the passport zine, the starter pack booklet.

## The gaps we can own

1. Nobody gives a child a newspaper about screens; kids papers report them as news.
2. Etsy tickets sell bedtime: every minute is a prize. Three kinds of time is the fix.
3. Nothing measures. No freebie shows a parent whether anything changed.
4. The words are paywalled (Good Inside) or dated (NSPCC). We hold 335 scripts.
5. Agreements are adult forms the child signs but never makes.
6. Boredom lists stop at age 5 and connect to nothing.
7. Few free sheets ship A4, Letter, ink saver and a phone view together.

## The five, best first

### 1. The Planet Post, Issue 1: How Screens Work In Our House

- **For** children 6 to 11 (6 to 7 with a grown up). The primary parent who
  polices every evening and wants the child in on the plan.
- **Format** four A4 pages, single sided: prints from any phone, reads flat on
  a screen. A second file imposes them as an A5 booklet (one A4 sheet, duplex,
  flip short edge, one fold). Open question 2 covers the zine.
- **Page 1, front page.** Painted rainbow masthead (`RainbowArc painted`),
  "Issue 1, free, always". Lead story: "Your brain loves lots of colours",
  the balance wheel idea in kid words with a mini wheel to colour. A weather
  box: "Today: sunny, with a good chance of outside". DiGi as editor, one
  line welcome. An "Inside" strip.
- **Page 2, the big explainer.** "Three jars of screen time": Mine every day,
  Earned with stars, Nobody's (bedtime, dinner, school), drawn from the
  ScreenTimeDealSheet jars. Bloop's column: "Phones go to bed too", with the
  PhonesToBedSheet bed drawing and a line to write their bedtime.
- **Page 3, puzzles.** Orbit's "Real or pretend?" detective corner (three
  messages to judge, answers upside down at the foot), a screen free word
  search, a spot the jobs picture, "Write tomorrow's happy headline about
  you".
- **Page 4, back page.** The jobs page: five jobs worth stars to tick, and the
  family jobs that are just for helping (contribution is belonging). "My
  screen promise" to fill in and sign with a grown up. A cut off strip for
  the grown up: "Want the plan behind this paper?" with the stage check QR.
- **Why it beats them** the only free kids paper on this topic, and every page
  is something to do. **Hook** "A newspaper for kids that explains how screen
  time works in your house, before the argument starts."
- **Proof path** the three jars are the child app star economy
  (`app/k/[token]`, minutes per star, weekly cap, protected windows); the
  jobs are the quest board (`app/(dashboard)/dashboard/quests`); Orbit's
  corner is a real lesson strand (`app/(dashboard)/dashboard/lessons`).
- **CTA** back page strip and last line: "What stage is your child? Three
  questions, no sign up." QR to `/starter-pack?src=planet-post-1`.
- **Build** LARGE. New `NewspaperPage` paper, reusing HappyNewsBits (RainbowArc,
  WavyRule, Burst, StarShape, SunRays, Plate, Ribbon) and the drawings in
  HappyPaper, BalanceWheelSheet, ScreenTimeDealSheet, PhonesToBedSheet. The
  issue is data, so Issue 2 (bedtime) and Issue 3 (real or fake online) are a
  content job, not a build. That is the Big Life Journal series lesson.

### 2. Screen Time Tickets, where bedtime is never for sale

- **For** children 5 to 12. The parent whose Etsy tickets became the evening's currency.
- **Pages, 3.** Page 1: twelve Earned tickets ("5 minutes, earned with a
  star", a star to colour on each) and four My Time tickets (the daily core,
  theirs whatever happens). Page 2: four "Can I ask?" cards for protected
  time, which go to a grown up as a question, never a flat no, and a "Never
  for sale" strip of bedtime, dinner and school to stick above the ticket
  pot. Page 3: a pot label to cut and wrap a jar, plus the parent half page:
  how the three kinds work and the one sentence to say when the tickets run
  out.
- **Why it beats them** the top sellers' cheap joy plus the rule they miss.
  **Hook** "Screen time tickets, with one rule the Etsy ones forget: bedtime
  is never for sale."
- **Proof path** the star quest itself, the thing competitors do not have:
  jobs earn stars, stars become minutes at the child's own rate, the free
  daily core, protected windows (`app/k/[token]`, `lib/kid`), set up by the
  parent in `app/(dashboard)/dashboard/quests`.
- **CTA** pot label foot and parent half page: the stage check line and QR.
- **Build** MEDIUM. New `TicketsSheet`, cut lines per print design system 4.4
  (dashed, scissors glyph, 4mm inner margin), jars and Moon and Star reused.

### 3. The Seven Night Screen Reset

- **For** the whole family, children 4 to 14. The parent who wants to know if
  anything they try actually works.
- **Pages, 4, A5 folded booklet or flat A4.** Cover. Night 1 is the baseline:
  name the one screen worry and rate it on five faces, really tough to going
  great. Nights 2 to 7: one small change a night (phones go to bed, a screen
  free dinner, a job before the tablet, the switch off warning, watch one
  thing together, a bored jar evening), a box for the child to colour when
  done, five faces for the grown up. Back page: draw a line through the
  seven faces, "What moved? What will you keep?", then the stage check.
- **Why it beats them** no freebie measures anything; this is our loop on
  paper. **Hook** "Seven nights, one small change each, and a way to see if
  it is working."
- **Proof path** the check in (`components/daily/ConcernCheckIn.tsx`, five
  faces, first one is the baseline, `concern_events`) and the daily path
  (`components/daily/TodayPathBig.tsx`, picked by `lib/home/next-up.ts`).
  The seven changes are drawn from real scripts' `tonight` lines.
- **CTA** back page: "Your baseline is on paper. The app keeps it for you."
  then the stage check line and QR.
- **Build** MEDIUM. Lift the `Face` drawing out of ConcernCheckIn into a
  shared component so paper and app draw the same faces. DiGi leads.

### 4. The Words For The Five Screen Fights

- **For** parents of children 4 to 14, the fridge door at 6pm.
- **Pages, 2.** Page 1: five cut out cards on A4, one per fight: "Five more
  minutes", "Everyone else has it", switching off mid game, the phone at
  bedtime, "I'm bored". Each card: the moment, Say this, Not this, and one
  line of why, straight from the `scripts` table columns `say_this`,
  `not_this`, `why_it_works`. Page 2: the same five as a wallet card and a
  phone lock screen image (the phone view the engine already promises).
- **Why it beats them** free where Good Inside charges, current where NSPCC is
  dated, and every card a pathway, never a yes or no. **Hook** "Save this for
  6pm: the words for the five screen fights every family has."
- **Proof path** the scripts (`app/(dashboard)/dashboard/scripts`, 335 in the
  table per `lib/content/proof.ts`; the trial opens them all) and DiGi for
  the moment no card covers.
- **CTA** foot of each card set: "These are 5 of 335. What stage is your
  child?" and the QR.
- **Build** SMALL to MEDIUM. Calm parent register (ink edge, one drawn icon
  per card, no scatter). Copy is pulled from the five chosen script rows at
  build time and never rewritten in the component.

### 5. The Bored Jar

- **For** children 4 to 11. The parent who hears "I'm bored" and reaches for
  the tablet because nothing else is to hand.
- **Pages, 3.** Forty slips, five per balance wheel colour (Outside, Make,
  Read, Move, Help, Family, Rest, plus a Wild Card colour), each a drawn icon
  and one line, two age levels marked by one star or two. Help slips carry a
  star: they are jobs and can earn time. A jar label to wrap. A colour in
  tally: every slip done, colour its slice on the mini wheel.
- **Why it beats them** reaches age 11, coded to the wheel, helping slips join
  the stars. **Hook** "Forty slips for the jar, so I'm bored becomes the start
  of something."
- **Proof path** the balance wheel (`BalanceWheelSheet`), the five a day and
  the quest board where a child pitches their own quest (`app/k/[token]`).
- **CTA** jar label foot and slip 40: "Grown ups: what stage is your child?"
- **Build** SMALL. One slip grid component, wheel reused. Pebble leads.

## Print spec, PDF perfect

- **One design, two papers.** Every page sits in the common safe box of A4 and
  US Letter (196 by 265mm), rendered centred on 210 by 297mm and on 215.9 by
  279.4mm. Nothing reflows. 10mm minimum to any ink (home printers lose the
  outer 3 to 6mm). White ground, so no bleed at home; a print shop file with
  3mm bleed and crop marks for the Planet Post only.
- **Rendering.** Chromium print to PDF from a dev fixture route, Nunito and IBM
  Plex Mono embedded from `app/fonts/`, vectors kept, `print-color-adjust:
  exact`, plus the zoom and padding resets from
  `plans/2026-09-16-passport-printable-review.md`.
- **Three files each.** Colour A4, colour Letter, ink saver (line art, no fills,
  text 100 percent black, print design system section 3), greyscale proofed.
  Phone PNGs for anything kept on a phone.
- **Checks.** Extend `scripts/check-print-fit.mjs` (one sheet in, one page out,
  both sizes); `pdffonts` shows Nunito; cut lines survive 97 percent fit to
  page; `npm run ai-tells` on all copy; one real print from an iPhone.
- **Social previews.** 1080 by 1350 (4:5) and 1080 by 1080 per freebie from
  `tools/social-cards`: cream, the real rendered page as hero, Nunito 900
  headline, one gold detail, inside the centre 1013px; a page by page 4:5
  carousel for Instagram, one card for Facebook. Launch on Happy News Saturdays.

## Delivery, checked against the repo

Exists: `lib/magnets/registry.ts` (slug, title, `staticPdf`), `MagnetGate`
(email box, reveals the download), `app/api/magnet/route.ts` (upserts
`starter_leads` with `source: magnet:<slug>`, emails the link, pings Justin),
gates live on `/five-questions`, `/evidence`, `/etsy-bonus`; leads then get
`leadNurtureEmail` into `/starter-pack` under the six day email floor. The
pdf-lib magnet route only has Helvetica, so it is not used for these.

The plan:
1. **Pages.** `/free/<slug>` per freebie in `app/(marketing)` plus a `/free`
   index. Every page preview shows ungated (Google and parents see what they
   get); the email gate is on the download, as the registry comment intends.
2. **Registry.** `Magnet` gains `files: { a4, letter, ink }` beside `staticPdf`,
   served from `public/printables/free/`. The reveal shows A4 with Letter and
   ink saver links; the email carries all three.
3. **After the download.** The reveal adds one card: "While it prints: what
   stage is your child? Three questions, no sign up."
4. **Attribution.** QRs and links carry `?src=<slug>`; `/starter-pack` needs a
   small change to record it against the lead.
5. **Members.** Each also enters `lib/printables/registry.ts` as free, so app
   families skip the gate and the child sheets tick the five a day.
6. **Etsy.** Never listed there; the Etsy bonus stays the starter pack booklet.
