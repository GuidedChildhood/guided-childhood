## Lane boundaries, checked against open PRs on 29 August

PR #922 (open, another session) holds the PLATFORM CODE half of the passport:
passport codes, the public verify page, bridge a (school module codes redeemed
at home through parent_note), graduation, migration 227. **Nothing in this
plan touches any of that.** This lane is the CURRICULUM CONTENT half:

1. **The White Rose standard** applied to the 21 school modules: small steps,
   one scheme of learning, block structure, the artefact set per step.
   Continues the Lesson Standard shipped in PR #858.
2. **Platform and AI coverage audit**: does the scheme actually teach every
   aspect of every platform a UK child meets, including AI models? Gaps named,
   then filled, aligned to the 14 strand map in plans/world-class-curriculum.md.
3. **The living risk layer**: a platform card and risk register in the schools
   schema that lessons read live instead of baking facts in. **Migrations 228
   and 229 are claimed by this plan** (227 is claimed by #922; the gap at 207
   stays a gap).
4. **AI and the job market strand** for KS4 and KS5, Gatsby aligned, with the
   honest evidence and an annual refresh line.
5. **The philosophy page**: our position against the canon (DfE RSHE 2025,
   KCSIE, Common Sense Media, Dr Becky, the van der Linden social media
   passport proposal, Livingstone, the Haidt and Odgers debate, best schools),
   source by source, where we agree and where we differ.
6. **Updater routines**: agents on the existing research updater pattern
   (draft with web search, human gate, only then live) that keep the risk
   register, the platform cards and the careers data current. Nothing reaches
   a classroom without Justin approving it.

## Fixed constraints carried in

name "digital passport" is not used in UK school marketing (locked decision).

lesson makes carries a source. Pupil data stays first name and initial only.
