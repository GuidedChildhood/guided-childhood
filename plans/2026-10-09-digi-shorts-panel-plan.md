# DiGi shorts: the research panel, the series, the hero short

9 October 2026. Asked for by Justin after the 103 second DiGi launch film
(PR 1220, merged).

## What he asked for

Two messages, read together:

1. On the film: zoom into the text on the phone so it reads big on screen;
   show the top issue moments; show the thinking messages that actually pop
   up in the real DiGi; show the pathway as it appears in the app; a new
   video on the screen timer, with research on how it works; a slick home
   page as the backdrop; DiGi as our Pixar style 3D star.
2. Then: review the best of DiGi as a problem and solution story. Research
   Reddit and Mumsnet for the most common parent problems with screens,
   phones and social media, establish the top 3, research how DiGi would
   actually answer each, how to show it in real time, how the platform
   tracks the moment afterwards, and how many videos the series should be
   for the best sign up rate. A panel of researchers (a parent and child
   scientist, a neuroscientist, a head teacher, a UX specialist, a platform
   selling specialist, a marketer, more if needed), sourcing as much as
   possible, talking to each other, not stopping until it is the best
   marketing short there is.

## How it runs

One workflow, five phases:

1. **Research**, nine in parallel: Reddit parent voice; Mumsnet and UK data;
   a code mapper on DiGi, the moments, the pathway, the timer and the
   research pipeline; a parent and child scientist; a neuroscientist; a head
   teacher and DSL; a UX and motion designer; a subscription growth lead; a
   short form marketer. Every one briefed with THE-STORY.md.
2. **Top problems**: one synthesis ranks the top five, the top three lead.
3. **DiGi's answers**: per problem, the answer worked out from the prompt
   code and the scripts table, then attacked by the scientist, the
   neuroscientist and a rails auditor.
4. **Series**: three competing concepts (conversion first, story first,
   proof first), scored by four judges (a panel of real customer parents,
   growth, design, evidence and the ban camp), grafted into one.
5. **Until it holds**: the four judges score the winner out of ten; below
   nine, it is revised; at most three rounds, and the plan panel stopping
   rule applies.

Output: a pack at `content/packs/2026-10-09-digi-shorts/` (research, top
problems, DiGi answers, series plan, storyboards), then the hero short built
in HyperFrames from real captured screens (the `ref-*` preview pages render
the real components with made up data), then the timer video.

## Rules that hold throughout

- Every parent quote is real and linked, or it is not used. No synthetic
  parent, no invented testimonial, no outcome promised for a child.
- DiGi's answers are faithful to its rails and labelled as such, never
  passed off as a logged conversation.
- No dashes in any copy. Every number carries its named source.
- The real product is the authority for every screen.
