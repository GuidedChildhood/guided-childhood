# The evidence and the build. A ten article series, five pairs.

Date: 28 July 2026. A LinkedIn article series that alternates a research led,
data driven, genuinely insightful piece with a founder piece on what we built off
the back of it. Ten articles, five pairs. DiGi stays in the background, this
series is deliberately about the rest of the platform, the services and the
tactics we developed, not the AI.

The rhythm: Research article lands an evidence insight most people have not heard,
usually a benefit of responsible smartphone and app use as a child grows. Then the
paired Build article shows the concrete thing we made because of it. Read together,
the research earns the belief and the build proves we act on it.

All copy runs through the engagement rules (linkedin-engagement.md): first line 12
words or fewer, story not statement, short paragraphs, a real photo of Justin, end
on a question, no external links in the body. And the hidden thread (hidden-thread.md),
one in ten states the full thesis, the rest carry a brick. No dashes. Justin's voice.

Evidence discipline: these are perceived benefits, associations and modest effects,
not proof. Say so. The active versus passive split is contested, hold it honestly.
Readiness milestones are guides, not laws.

## The five pairs

### PAIR 1. Autonomy, not restriction, builds self control
- **Research article.** The counterintuitive finding, children handed graduated
  autonomy and structure self regulate better than children under pure restriction.
  Restriction manages the moment, autonomy builds the muscle.
  - Anchors: Morales-Alvarez et al 2025, Cyberpsychology, supportive parenting
    (autonomy support plus structure) predicts adolescents' digital citizenship
    through self regulation. Self determination theory, autonomy support fosters
    self regulation (Reflection Sciences summary of the SDT literature). Frame with
    Odgers, prepare and support over ban.
- **Build article.** The staged pathway and the passport. Readiness grown one stage
  at a time from age 4 to 16, five stages, foundation to independent, autonomy
  handed over in calibrated steps rather than all at once at sixteen. The passport
  and stamps make the growth visible. Not DiGi, the scaffolding around it.

### PAIR 2. Smartphones meet real needs, so preparation beats prohibition
- **Research article.** Why a straight ban misfires. Smartphones answer three real
  developmental needs, connection, information and autonomy, and the honest question
  is not whether but when and how ready.
  - Anchors: Nature Pediatric Research 2025, "Navigating youth, smartphones and
    policy, a balanced perspective on digital wellbeing," restriction alone is
    insufficient, phones meet underlying needs. Frontiers 2025, "Smartphones through
    children's eyes, perceived benefits." Readiness around 13 to 14 as the prefrontal
    development milestone, held as a guide not a law.
- **Build article.** The school calendar engine and the Year 6 phone bridge.
  Anticipatory guidance built into a product, the order and the timing of the first
  phone, warned weeks early, rules agreed before not bolted on after. Not DiGi.

### PAIR 3. The child who makes, not just watches
- **Research article.** Active, creative use tracks better than passive scrolling,
  and learning apps genuinely move the needle. The reason to reach for a screen can
  be a good one.
  - Anchors: JMIR 2025 (e73098), active social media activities (posting, messaging,
    group chats) associate with lower depression, anxiety and loneliness and more
    belonging than passive use. NASEM, potential benefits of social media. Learning
    apps prepare children for school and lift early maths and literacy (PMC, Erikson
    Institute). Caveat, the active versus passive dichotomy is contested, hold honestly.
- **Build article.** The homework decoder, the lessons and the printables. The screen
  put to work as a making and learning tool, the statutory line behind tonight's
  homework, the star lessons, the craft packs that pay out offline. Not DiGi.

### PAIR 4. The rules a child helps write are the rules a child keeps
- **Research article.** Co created family media agreements beat rules handed down or
  copied off a website. Ownership is the mechanism.
  - Anchors: Moreno et al, Pediatrics, December 2024, "The Family Media Plan," plans
    customised and co created with the child outperform generic copied ones. Children
    and Screens, "All in the Family," media plans in the round. Frame with connection
    is the protection.
- **Build article.** The family phone agreement. A deal written together, the child's
  half in their own handwriting, a ceremony not a lecture, reviewed every term so it
  loosens as trust grows. Not DiGi.

### PAIR 5. It was never the hours, it was the pattern and the balance
- **Research article.** The shape of use, not the clock, and a life full enough
  offline that the screen earns its place. Responsible use is a pattern, not a number.
  - Anchors: Xiao et al, JAMA 2025, pattern (addictive trajectory) predicts harm,
    total hours do not. Przybylski, the Goldilocks middle. "Smartphone use and mental
    health, going beyond school restriction policies" (PMC). Sleep as the one hard line.
- **Build article.** The balance report and the scripts library. Off screen effort
  counted beside screen time, and 100+ scripts for the real moments, the device at
  dinner, the meltdown when it goes off. Managing the pattern, not the minutes. Not DiGi.
