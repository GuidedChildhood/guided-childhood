# How setup and Today work with several children

Justin, 18 August 2026: "how do we deal with set up and today page to work for
several children added? Best solution, or is there a better way to have a sign up
for each child? What does research on other apps give us, and your thoughts."

## WHAT THE RESEARCH SAYS, and it is unanimous

Six apps, including the two CLAUDE.md already names as our references.

**Greenlight** (mobbin.com/screens/7e97e03a-4c8e-47f3-8aab-2798ba5514ab). A
"Family" tab with child avatars beside it. The home page is FAMILY level and
carries the setup checklist: "Get started with these simple steps", 17 percent
complete, with "Finish child setup" as ONE item among three. Children are avatars
you tap into.

**GoHenry** (8c96f881-62da-4f5f-842d-0325a3e06555). Tap a child and you get
THEIR page: "[name] is earning £10.50", their tasks, their allowance. Per child
detail lives behind the child, never on the family home.

**Spotify Kids** (5f31b1f2-0f0c-4724-bc19-ce0dfa521e42). "Plan owner", then "Kids
accounts" as a list, then "Add a Kids account". They are called accounts and they
live inside the parent's plan.

**Duolingo ABC** (9d952a8b-7e1e-43a5-b034-041e84f78c49). "Who's Learning?", an
avatar picker with "Add a child" beside it.

**Amazon Alexa** (01d88b08-9323-4446-bb1c-5b1fff60bbdf). "Add up to 4 kids who
use Echo devices in your home." One list, one add button.

### The three things every one of them does

1. **ONE parent account. Never a signup per child.** Not one of the six asks a
   parent to sign up again. Spotify goes furthest by calling them "Kids
   accounts", and even those live inside the parent's plan and are created from
   it. So: no, there should not be a signup per child, and the research is not
   close on this.
2. **The home page is the FAMILY.** Setup checklists, balances and notifications
   all sit at family level. Greenlight is the clearest: one checklist, one
   progress bar, for the household.
3. **Children are a row of faces you tap into.** The per child detail is one
   level down, behind the child, never spread across the family home.

## SO: SETUP STAYS FOUR STEPS, AT FAMILY LEVEL

Greenlight settles it. Their checklist has "Finish child setup" as a single item,
not one per child.

    1  Build your family agreement       family, built once, copied per child
    2  Home screen and reminders          family, one device, one parent
    3  Add your other children            family, and this IS the multi child step
    4  Share the app with your child       PER CHILD, already built that way

Only step four fans out, and it already does: one code per child, by name, each
row settling on its own, a mix of app and paper allowed. That is the correct
place for the only fan out, because a QR code is the only thing in setup that
genuinely belongs to one child.

Step one is per child in the DATA (section 9) but stays one step in the LIST,
because the copy makes the second and third agreement two taps. Four steps for a
family of three, not twelve.

## AND TODAY STAYS ONE LOOP

This is the decision that matters most and it is the easiest to get wrong.

**A parent of three must never do the daily loop three times.** Ten minutes is
the promise; thirty is a different product and nobody finishes it. So Today is
the FAMILY's day, and the per child material comes to it rather than the parent
going round three times.

Which means, rung by rung:

- **CHECK IN** already does this correctly and is the model for the rest. One
  rung, all children's worries inside it, each row labelled with the child's
  name, and the rung ticks only when every child with a live worry has a number
  today. Built 15 August.
- **QUESTS** should be the same: one rung, "3 jobs waiting" across the family,
  named by child inside. The board already knows whose is whose.
- **MOMENT and SCRIPT** stay single. A moment is what happened in the house, and
  the script is the words for tonight. Neither is per child by nature, and the
  moment source already picks from the family's live worries.
- **AGREEMENT review** follows the check in shape once agreements go per child.

**The rule:** a rung fans out by child only when the ANSWER differs per child. It
never fans out just because the subject does.

## WHERE THE SWITCHER BELONGS

GoHenry's model, and it is the answer to the audit's tier 3: the switcher belongs
on the DEEP pages, the ones where a parent has gone looking at one child.

    stats, devices, lessons, moments, daily, digi, passport

Those are "show me Olga", and each needs the switcher plus `?child=` carried
through the nav. Home and setup do NOT need one, because they are the family.

That also shrinks the audit's tier 3 from "every page needs a switcher" to "seven
pages need a switcher and the nav needs to carry the choice", which is a much
smaller and much clearer job.

## WHAT I WOULD NOT DO

**A signup per child.** No reference does it, it means a second password for a
seven year old, and it breaks the one thing this product is: a family's pathway,
not a collection of individual ones.

**A child chooser as the front door**, Duolingo ABC style ("Who's Learning?").
That is right for an app the CHILD opens. Ours is opened by the PARENT, and
making them answer "which child" before they can see anything would put a
question in front of the ten minute habit every single morning.

**Three of everything on Home.** Three daily loops, three setup lists, three
passports on one page. It reads as three times the work, and the product's whole
claim is that it is ten minutes.
