# The shorts marketer review, weighed

12 October 2026. Justin asked for an agent to review the hero and Short 1 as an
expert marketer: are they usable, will they get stage check starts, and how
should we post them. The review was done from frame sheets at one frame a
second plus a shot list, read against THE-STORY.md, the silent-ugc rules and
the panel's series plan. It was weighed with the feedback filter before
anything was built.

**What it could see:** every frame and every line of on-screen text. It
could not hear the track, and it could not see a platform's real interface
over the frame. The safe zone points are its knowledge of TikTok and Reels,
not a measurement.

## Checked before weighing

- "Earn more stars to unlock more." is real product copy on the timer end
  screen (`components/quests/DeviceTimeCard.tsx:585`). The films show it.
- "Checking our reviewed research, not the open web." is a real thinking line
  (`components/digi/ThinkingReassurance.tsx:54`). DiGi does have a web search
  tool (`lib/digi/tools.ts:246`), and our own attacks file says the films
  never feature that line.
- The panel decided on 9 October that the 1 to 5 year old study appears only
  on the early years cut, with its age range, and never on the nine year
  old's film (`content/packs/2026-10-09-digi-shorts/attacks-and-answers.md:37`).
  The second pass broke that rule.
- "See what to do tonight" on the end card has a proof path: the stage check
  result says "what you can do tonight" for each worry
  (`app/(marketing)/starter-pack/ResultScreen.tsx:357`).
- `guidedchildhood.com/v/<code>` is live and lands on the stage check. It
  starts counting once migration 372 is applied.
