# The parent app in the happy news finish: setup, home, the daily path

Justin, 2 September 2026: "apply that top UX look to the set up, start up
and the dashboard of the parents app, Apple style UX, happy news type
icons, a pathway of day tasks like a Duolingo path, not changing the code
behind it, consistency and tidy design. Make the starting pages this style
also, with buttons like the child's app."

## Rule

Styling only. The finish the child app and the scripts already wear (ink
edges, hard ledges, Nunito 900, butter for the thing to tap, drawn icons in
crayon colours) lands on the parent's first screens and Home. Nothing that
decides what shows, when, or where a tap goes is touched. The parent side
keeps its calmer register: no stickers or dot scatter, one drawn icon per
row, cream and white grounds.

## Surfaces

- The daily path (TodayPathBig): ink edged card, drawn icon on every stone,
  done stones green with an ink ledge, the current stone white with a butter
  ring, the budget chips and the Go button in the child's button style.
- Setup Quest: the live step a white ink edged card with a butter numeral,
  done steps sage with a green ink edged tick, every button chunky butter
  with an ink edge and ledge.
- The first welcome walkthrough: the same buttons and cards.
- Home: DiGi's greeting bubble, the four rows (quests, road, DiGi, week)
  with drawn icons, the Everything else door.
- The bottom tab bar: ink top edge, the active tab a butter ink edged pill.
