# Ad studio skill (19 October 2026)

Justin, 19 October: "yes" to a repeatable skill for paid style adverts, built
from what competitors' best performing ads do, after the two Instagram sweeps
(`briefings/notes/competitor-sightings.md`). Lane: marketing. No migration.

## Why

We made one round of ads by hand (`content/packs/2026-10-14-first-phone-ad/`).
Nothing makes the next round the same way: find the competitor ads that are
working, take their structure, never their message, rebuild in our system,
test free first, then spend only behind the winner, and remember the result.

## What gets built

1. `.claude/skills/ad-studio/SKILL.md`: the phases.
   - Find the winners: Meta Ad Library links per competitor (the library is
     blocked from cloud sessions, so Justin screenshots, or a linked browser
     reads it). How long an ad has run is the best public sign it works.
   - Tear each down: hook, format, offer, proof, what is on screen.
   - Rebuild: our message, one of the six permanent formats, real screens,
     the stage check at the end. Three to five hook variants per angle.
   - Gates: the do not copy list, the evidence rule, the synthetic person
     rule, no dashes, UK ad rules for testimonials and claims.
   - Test: organic first, the winner by stage checks, then the boost brief.
   - Log: a results file the next round reads first.
2. `.claude/skills/ad-studio/ad-library-watchlist.md`: who to watch, with the
   library search links.
3. `content/ads/results-log.md`: every ad that went out, and what it did.
4. A routing line in `CLAUDE.md`.
5. Justin's pick recorded: cards A and C of the first phone ad go first.

## Not in scope

Posting, scheduling, boosting or touching Ads Manager. Justin does those.
