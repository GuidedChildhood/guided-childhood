# The LinkedIn advice, weighed against our own objectives

Justin, 20 October 2026, sent a creator's LinkedIn masterclass transcript (five
rules, 400,000 followers, a paid live training at the end) and asked whether we
should be opening posts with his personal story as it advises.

Run through `.claude/skills/feedback-filter`. Advice is an input, never an
instruction, however confidently it is written.

**What the reviewer could see.** Nothing of ours. This is a general creator
playbook aimed at founders monetising expertise, recorded to sell a masterclass.
So every point is about LinkedIn in general and none of it is about our product,
our audience or our evidence standard.

**One thing to hold throughout.** His numbers, the six million dollars, the
forty three million for five hundred founders, the three hundred thousand
impressions on an early post, are his own and unverifiable. Our evidence or
silence rule applies to advice as well as to copy, so we take the craft and
leave the claims. The craft can be right while the numbers are unchecked.

---

## The verdict, rule by rule

### Rule 1, post at the same time every weekday

> "consistency compounds"

**Adapt.** The principle is right and we already hold it, though not where I
first said: the cadence lives in the viral post skill, three to five a week,
weekday mornings, never twice in a day, because posts less than about eight hours
apart cannibalise each other's first hour. `weekly-rhythm.md` is about the
family account's anchor days and does not set this. His every weekday at ten
is the cadence of someone whose business is LinkedIn and who spends two hours a
month writing. Our posts carry verified figures and an overclaim note, and the
day we trade that for frequency we lose the only thing that makes them
defensible.

**The honest finding underneath his point, and it is uncomfortable.** Our gap is
not writing, it is posting. Three finished, checked posts are sitting unposted
right now, and GDC-038 missed its own planned date yesterday. The bottleneck he
is describing is real for us, it is just at the other end of the pipe.

### Rule 2, tell stories, not facts

> "the highest converting content for me and for every other founder in our accelerator is the personal stuff"

**Adopt, with the rule made precise, and he is right about the post in front of
us.**

His framing is too blunt to copy as written, because Justin's single best
performing post opened on a precise statistic, beta equals 0.061, and did about
73,911 impressions. "Never facts" would have killed it.

But his direction matches our own data. Justin's 28 day numbers to 6 October:
the two posts that opened on an admitted uncertainty did 6,008 and 3,155
impressions and carried most of the month's comments, while the two that opened
on a figure did 1,002 and 698 and got none.

So the rule is not stories over facts. **It is open on the thing only Justin
could have said.** Sometimes that is a scene from his own house. In the Wrong
Villain post it was a number almost nobody had read properly. Both are his and
neither is generic, which is the actual variable.

**Where he is simply right.** GDC-044 holds its three strongest human lines
until paragraphs five, eleven and twelve. The Covid confession, two small girls
at home and a business to keep alive and YouTube used as a babysitter, is the
best sentence in the post and our own `founding-story.md` says so in as many
words. It is currently below the fold by a long way.

**The part of his advice we are not using at all.** He names three sources of
story: your own experience, the experience of people around you, and the
experience of people you have read about. We use the first and almost nothing
else. `founder-scenes-used.md` tracks only Justin's own scenes, and the green
list is finite, which is why the ledger is already three deep in a week. The
second and third sources are untapped and would stop us rationing him.

### Rule 3, pick an enemy

**Already built, and ours is better aimed.** The Wrong Villain series is exactly
this. Our enemy is a misdiagnosis rather than a group of people: screen time as
the thing worth counting, and the blocking apps that make tonight's decision for
a parent and teach a child nothing for tomorrow. THE-STORY and the hidden thread
carry it.

**One adaptation declined.** He repeats his enemy every single week. Our hidden
thread runs at one post in ten on purpose, and our comment standard is to be
generous to the ban it camp rather than to dunk on them. His enemy is the spirit
of corporate, which costs him nothing. An enemy aimed every week at parents who
want a ban would be aimed at our own customers.

### Rule 4, make it snack sized, eight to twelve lines

**Decline the length, on our own data.** The viral post skill is built on van der
Blom's 1.8 million post dataset: the body sweet spot is 1,300 to 2,500
characters, about 27 percent higher engagement than posts under 400 characters.
Eight to twelve lines is roughly 400 to 800. His own best material is a thirty
second hook for a cold audience; Justin's is an argument for a sceptical one, and
dwell time is the signal that actually rewards it.

**Adopt the visual, and this is the real hole he has found.** His point stands
and ours agrees with it: one visual, and on the feed it takes most of the screen.
Our last four packs shipped like this:

| Post | Visual |
|---|---|
| GDC-037 | two images, and they exist in Drive |
| GDC-039 | one image, the timer note, in Drive |
| GDC-038 | none |
| GDC-043 | none |
| GDC-044 | none |

Checked rather than assumed: the Sweden pack contains no mention of an image at
all, and the two hits in the ban teaches pack are both the Discord identity
documents, not a visual brief. The 037 and 039 images were specified in Drive and
never written into the repo pack, which is its own small problem: the brief and
the asset live in different places and only one of them is version controlled.

The viral post skill ships `stat-card.html` and `carousel.html` for exactly this
and we have not opened either for a month. Three finished posts are waiting to go
out with nothing to look at.

**Adapt the third grade English.** His instinct is right and we already have a
better worded version of it: the `ai-tells` read back test is whether it sounds
like Justin saying it out loud to another parent at the school gate. Plain, not
simplified, and without talking down to a reader who has just read a
constitutional court ruling.

### Rule 5, send everybody to a funnel

**Already built, with two declines.**

Already ours: the link in the first comment and never in the body, every CTA to
`/starter-pack`, the newsletter for the email list he is right to say we should
own.

**Decline, put the link in the post.** A body link cuts reach 25 to 60 percent on
our own figures. It stays in the first comment.

**Decline, the webinar as the destination.** Ours is the free stage check, which
is a lower friction ask than an hour of someone's evening and is already the
funnel THE-STORY section 9 is built around.

**Adopt, one withdrawal a week.** Every post a deposit, one ask a week. We have
never set that cadence and should, because at the moment a CTA either appears
because a post happens to suit one or does not appear at all.

**Question for Justin, which only he can answer.** His profile as a landing page
point is sound and we cannot see the profile. Does the banner say who you help,
does the headline read in plain words, and is there one link in the featured
section pointing at the stage check?

---

## What actually changes because of this

1. **GDC-044 opens on the Covid confession.** The scene leads, the argument
   follows. Being checked by two agents before anything is rewritten.
2. **Every post gets a visual from now on**, using the templates we already
   have, and no pack is called finished without one.
3. **The scene problem gets widened** beyond Justin's own life, to the people
   around him and the people he has read about, so the green list stops being
   rationed three at a time.
4. **One ask a week**, written into the rhythm rather than decided per post.
5. **The real bottleneck is named**: three checked posts are unposted and 038
   missed its date. No amount of writing advice fixes that.

## What does not change

The length, the sourcing, the overclaim notes, the enemy, the link in the first
comment, and the refusal to name a group of people as the villain.
