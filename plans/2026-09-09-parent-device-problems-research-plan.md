# The ten problems parents actually have: research plan

Date: 9 September 2026. Lane: research briefing. No migrations, no platform code.
Commissioned by Justin: deep research on the top ten problems parents have with
their children's behaviour around devices, the causes, and our solutions, with
Reddit specifically included as a source.

## Why this is not the standard sweep

Every previous briefing in this repo asks whether devices harm children. This one
asks something different and more useful commercially: what goes wrong in the
kitchen at seven in the evening, why, and does what we built actually answer it.

The nearest existing work is `research/homepage-audience-language.md` (8 August),
which mined what parents SAY, found "policing without a plan" as the core feeling,
and explicitly could not reach Reddit or Mumsnet directly. This sweep goes after
BEHAVIOURS rather than language, and goes where that one could not.

## The six lenses, flexed

The kids-research default six do not fit a question about daily family friction,
so they are swapped, keeping the rule that there is always a sceptic whose job is
to kill weak claims and always the person on the receiving end.

1. **The Parent Voice.** Reddit first, then Mumsnet, Netmums and control app
   reviews. Ranks the problems by how often they actually appear, captures
   verbatim wording, and records what parents already tried and why it failed.
2. **The Clinician.** What each behaviour is developmentally, normal versus
   signal, the red flag threshold for each, and what formal guidance says.
3. **The Academic.** Causes rather than harms: transitions and executive
   function, reactance and autonomy, the parental mediation typologies,
   displacement, sleep mechanism, and what changes between ages 4 and 16.
4. **The Skeptic.** Kills the folk neuroscience parents are sold, attacks the
   addiction framing, and is briefed to attack OUR mechanics hardest of all,
   specifically whether making screen time an earned reward backfires through
   the overjustification effect.
5. **The Child.** The other side of all ten conflicts, including ages 4 to 10
   who are missing from most youth voice work, and what children say adults
   could actually do differently.
6. **The Intervention Evidence.** What has been tested rather than recommended,
   with effect sizes, including the token economy literature both for and
   against, and the adherence rates that decide whether an app changes anything.

Plus the platform mapper against this codebase, so the solutions half lands on
real files and routes rather than themes. A feature described in marketing that
does not exist is worse than no marketing.

## Output

`briefings/2026-09-09-parent-device-problems-v2.html`, built on the kids-research
template, after adversarial citation verification. Three parts: the ranked ten
with causes, the contradiction map, and the honest solutions map marked as built,
partial or missing.

## The discipline that applies

No invented quotes, ever. Every parent quote marked VERBATIM or PARAPHRASE.
Nothing claimed for a feature that does not render. The banned numbers list in
`plans/decisions.md` applies in full. Where the evidence says one of our
mechanics is wrong, the briefing says so.
