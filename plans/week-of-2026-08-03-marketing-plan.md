# Marketing plan: carrying Inspired by Alma over to The Guided Digital Childhood

**Written 30 July 2026 · Marketing lane · No dashes in any copy**

The job: move an Instagram and Facebook audience of 6,000 people, built over ten
years on cake toppers, party supplies and event styling, onto a new thing the
same family built for the same reason. Do it without losing them, and do it by
telling the truth about why.

This file is the plan. The paste ready assets live in `content/brand-story/`.

---

## 1. The decision that shapes everything else

**Rename the account in place. Do not start a new one.**

Three options were on the table.

| Option | What happens to the 6,000 |
|---|---|
| Start a fresh account | Lost. Start at zero with no proof and no warmth. |
| Rename in place, drop the craft | Kept on paper, but the feed stops being for them. Slow bleed. |
| **Rename in place, keep the craft as the printables line** | **Kept, and given a reason to stay.** |

The third one wins, and not as a compromise. Those 6,000 people are parents who
followed a mother making beautiful things for children's birthdays. That is the
exact audience for a product about childhood. There is no better cold list in the
country for this, and it is already warm.

The craft is not being abandoned either. It changes material. We spent ten years
making physical things that made family moments better, and we still do, except
now some of them print at home and some of them run on a phone. The star charts,
the routine charts, the family phone agreement, the activity packs in
`content/printables/` are the same instinct as the cake toppers. Same hands, same
standard, same reason.

**The bridge line, and it should appear in the bio and the first post:**

> It started with Alma. It still does.

### What changes and when

- **Handle and name** change on the morning of post 5, not before.
- **"Formerly Inspired by Alma" goes in the NAME field, not the bio**, for the
  first 90 days. The name field is what shows in search and in the feed, which
  is where recognition actually has to happen. Then it comes out.
- **Grid stays up.** Nothing gets deleted. Ten years of work is proof that we
  finish things.
- **Highlights** get one new first item called Our Story, holding the handover
  posts, so anyone arriving late gets the whole thing in 60 seconds.

### Two mechanical facts that decide whether this goes smoothly

**Followers are not notified when a handle changes.** Nothing tells them. The
only signal they get is an unfamiliar name appearing in their feed, which reads
as an account that was hacked or sold. That is the entire reason the handover
sequence exists, and it is why posts 1 to 4 run before the switch rather than
after.

**The old handle is released the instant we drop it, and anyone can take it.**
So on the morning of the change: **claim `inspiredbyalma` on a second account
within the hour**, and point it at the new one. Ten years of tags, mentions and
word of mouth still point there, and letting a stranger pick it up would be an
avoidable, permanent loss.

### The handle itself

Recommendation: **the person at the brand**. Good Inside runs as
`drbeckyatgoodinside` for exactly this reason. It keeps the human trust and the
search recall while the product name gets learned.

Something in the shape of `nataliaatguidedchildhood`, or `guidedchildhood` with
Natalia's name in the name field. It is the lowest risk structure available for
a handover, because it never asks the audience to swap a person for a brand.

## 2. The voice, and the one thing to settle

The account voice is **we**, the family, and the primary narrator on Founder
Monday is **Natalia**, the person the 6,000 already follow, who started Inspired
by Alma when Alma was born in 2015. Justin is the other half of the we, not the
host. That is the opposite of the platform copy, where Justin is the face, and
it is deliberate. Changing the name of an account is survivable. Changing the
voice of an account is what actually loses people.

**Natalia is from Valencia and the children are bilingual.** That is texture,
never a topic. It shows up as a Spanish word because it is the word that fits,
or a grandmother on a video call, or the five o'clock argument happening in two
languages. One or two touches a month. It makes the account read as a real
family rather than a brand, and that is the whole job it does. We do not post
about bilingual parenting as a subject, because it is not what we sell and it
would split the audience.

> **Still open.** Whether Natalia is happy to be the on camera face on Mondays,
> because the plan leans on it. And her personal tag, if she wants one on the
> account.

The rest of the voice rules are inherited and non negotiable: warm, plain,
direct, UK English, short sentences, no AI isms, and **no dashes in any copy,
ever**. A fellow parent who did the homework, never a guru.

## 3. The story we are actually telling

Full canonical version in `content/brand-story/founding-story.md`. It is the
single source of truth and every post pulls from it. The shape:

1. **2015.** Alma is born. A bedroom, a laptop, and customised cake toppers.
2. **The growth.** Toppers to supplies to events to weddings to the big ones.
3. **The shop in Bristol.** The proof that we build real things and finish them.
4. **Olga.** And with Olga, something we had not seen with Alma: YouTube and
   screens turning into a battle, and the mood of the whole house moving with it.
5. **Covid.** Two little girls, a business to keep alive, and the honest bit
   nobody posts. We used YouTube as a babysitter to survive, and we felt guilty
   about it.
6. **The idea.** Not from a business plan. From needing it ourselves.
7. **Teo, now four,** growing up into a world where the ban lands at 16 like a
   cliff edge, and where AI has changed the road again.
8. **What we built.** The thing we wished had existed when we were sat there at
   nine at night feeling like we had got it wrong.

The guilt is the hinge of the whole story. It is the line that makes 6,000
parents stop scrolling, because every single one of them has done the same thing
and none of them have said it out loud.

**What we do not do with it.** We never resolve the guilt by blaming YouTube, and
we never resolve it by telling parents they were fine actually. We resolve it by
saying the tool was doing a job we needed doing, and nobody had built the version
of that tool that also taught anything. So we did.

## 4. The weekly rhythm

Four anchor days. Four is the number that survives a real week with three
children and a business, and a rhythm that survives is worth more than a rhythm
that impresses for a fortnight and dies.

| Day | Slot | Job | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | Founder Monday | Why we care. One chapter of the story. | Real photo, face to camera, or a then and now |
| **Wednesday** | Research Wednesday | What is actually true. One finding, plain English, what it means tonight. | Carousel or single graphic, always with the tonight line |
| **Friday** | Service Friday | What we built. One service, one real problem, how it hinges together. | Carousel or screen recording, the funnel day |
| **Saturday** | Happy News Saturday | Why this is not doom. Devices used well. | Carousel, warm, shareable |

Read down the column and the whole thesis is there without anyone having to
argue it: Monday is why we care, Wednesday is what is true, Friday is what we
built, Saturday is why it is not the end of the world. **Not a ban, not no ban.
Incremental, educational, fun, sustainable device use, and AI literacy with
safety.** The week says it structurally, so no single post has to shout it.

**Tuesday, Thursday and Sunday stay empty on purpose.** They are the buffer that
keeps the four alive. If there is energy spare, they take Stories, not grid
posts, because Stories are where the link lives and where replies come from.

Full brief for each day, with hooks, structures and rules, in
`content/brand-story/weekly-rhythm.md`.

## 5. Every service gets its Friday

Full map in `content/brand-story/service-post-map.md`. The rule for Service
Friday is that **the post opens on the parent's problem, never on the feature**.
Nobody has ever wanted a digital passport. They have wanted their eleven year old
to stop asking for Snapchat every day for a fortnight.

The rotation covers the passport and the pathway, the social media lessons and
the curriculum, school alerts and the school calendar, DiGi, quests and earned
screen time, device timers and check ins, the ask first flow, the scripts, the
printables and charts, and the family agreement. Each one is written as problem
first, then the thing, then how it hinges into the rest.

The hinge matters more than any single service. A parent who only sees a star
chart sees a star chart. A parent who sees that the chart earns the minutes, the
minutes run on the timer, the timer feeds the weekly check in, the check in
feeds DiGi, and DiGi knows which lesson comes next, sees a system. Every Friday
post ends by naming one hinge.

## 6. The sign up funnel

Social does not sell the subscription. Social sells **one free thing that
diagnoses**, and the product sells itself from there.

```
Post (Fri or Sat)  ->  Link in bio / Story link  ->  /starter-pack
                                                        |
                                            3 questions, 90 seconds
                                            age band, worry, time
                                                        |
                                            Their child's stage,
                                            named, with a script
                                            they can use tonight
                                                        |
                                                 Email capture
                                                        |
                                                    /onboarding
```

The `/starter-pack` quiz is already built and is the best asset we have for this,
because it is not a lead magnet, it is a diagnosis. A parent finds out which of
five stages their child is at and gets something to say at bedtime. That is worth
an email address, and it earns the right to sell later.

**Rules.**
- Every Friday and Saturday post drives to the bio link. Monday and Wednesday
  build trust and do not ask.
- Roughly **one post in five carries a direct ask**. The other four earn it.
- No links in Instagram captions. Link in bio, and Story link on the day.
- One destination at a time. The bio link is `/starter-pack` and it does not
  change week to week just because a new feature shipped.
- Founder rate is capped at 50 and that cap is real. It is the only urgency we
  use, and we only use it once the story has landed, not in week one.

Detail, including the first 30 days of Stories and the exact bio, in
`content/brand-story/funnel.md`.

The handover itself is a sequence, not a single post. It runs over the first two
weeks and it is the only time the four day rhythm bends. Full drafts in

The principle: **explain before you change, not after.** People forgive a rename

## 8. The agent

`.claude/skills/family-social/` is a skill that researches what we have actually
built, in this repo, and drafts the week in the family voice. It exists because
the failure mode of a content calendar is not running out of ideas, it is
running out of Tuesdays where anyone has the energy to write four posts.

What it does, in order: reads the founding story so the facts never drift, reads
the route and migration surface so it only ever describes things that really
exist, checks what has already been posted so it does not repeat itself, applies
the hidden thread filter and the 1 in 10 rule, drafts the four posts, then hands
back a review queue rather than publishing anything.

**It never invents a feature.** Every Service Friday claim traces to a route, a
component or a migration, and the draft carries the file path so it can be
checked in ten seconds.

## 9. What success looks like

Ninety days out, measured monthly.

- **Followers kept.** Losing under 10% through the rename is a good result. Some
  of the 6,000 are event suppliers and wedding people and they will go. That is
  fine and it is not failure.
- **Saves and shares over likes.** A Wednesday post that gets saved is worth more
  than one that gets liked, because saves mean a parent intends to use it.
- **Bio link clicks to starter pack starts.** The real number.
- **Starter pack completions.** The quiz has five steps and drop off between
  question one and the email is the thing to watch.
- **Replies and DMs.** The qualitative signal that the story landed. If parents
  start telling us their own guilt story, the whole strategy is working.

We do not measure reach as a headline number. Reach on a rebrand is noisy for the
first eight weeks and chasing it will push us into posting the panic content we
have spent two years refusing to post.
