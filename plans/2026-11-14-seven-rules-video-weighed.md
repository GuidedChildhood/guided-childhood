# The "seven new rules of digital marketing" video, weighed

14 November 2026. Justin pasted the transcript of https://youtu.be/nv1s7QrpUwQ
as the James Smith advice on email funnels. Weighed through the feedback
filter: advice is an input, never an instruction.

## Two things to know first

1. **The speaker is addressed as Sabri in the transcript, not James.** It
   reads as a marketing agency founder's video. So this may not be the video
   Justin meant.
2. **It does not say "email sequences instead of downloads".** Email appears
   once, in rule seven, as something to rewrite per niche. The email course
   idea in our plan therefore stands on our own reasoning and must not be
   credited to this video or to anyone.

His figures (10.2 billion dollars in client sales, Meta matching Google for
ad spend, the Andromeda change) are his own claims, unchecked. None is to be
repeated by us.

## Point by point

| His rule | Verdict | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Your copy is the targeting: change two or three words to speak to one segment | Adapt, in the next ad round | Our segments are the five stages. A stage named in the first line is a fair test inside ad-studio. Free test first, as the money rule says |
| 2. Be in the feed, and learn which interest pools your customer sits in | Already planned | The creator breakdown and the parent problem sweep are exactly this |
| 3. Post what a chatbot cannot answer: depth, a vantage point, shown visually | Adopt | It is our own review lens, value above AI. A checked figure list and a real product screen are things a chatbot cannot give. Added to the rules as a test every piece must pass |
| 4. Spend 10 percent of ad budget on PR and reward people for reviews | Park the PR, decline the rewards | Our ad spend is five pounds a day, so 10 percent is nothing. Rewarding reviews breaks review sites' own rules and our honesty rule. Asking real families for an honest review, unrewarded, is fine and is parked for when there are families to ask |
| 5. Below three million a year: one niche, one funnel, one problem | Adopt, and note what it argues against | It backs keeping one funnel, the stage check, which is what Justin chose. It also cautions against spreading across many topics and channels at once. One topic a week is the limit, not the floor |
| 6. Clone the funnel into nearby niches | Park | He says himself it is a distraction below three million. A topic email course is a wrapper on the same funnel, not a second funnel, and stays that way |
| 7. Have an AI rewrite every page, ad and email per niche in a day | Decline for now | Follows from 6. Our uploader and gates already exist, and nothing ships unreviewed |

## What changes

One line added to THE RULES in the answer series plan: every piece must pass
the test "could a parent get this from a chatbot, shorter and just as good?"
If yes, it is the wrong piece.

Nothing else changes. No spend, no new funnel.
