## 1. Can Etsy sell a subscription? No. Sell each issue.

Checked on Etsy's own pages on 5 November 2026.

| Question | Answer | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Recurring billing for buyers | None. The Help Centre only knows Etsy's own seller plans (Plus, Pattern) | help.etsy.com search "subscription" |
| A pre sold season sent month by month | Not banned in words, but risky. "Made to order downloads" are completed once, with up to 5 files; sellers must honour processing times; files sent in Messages are limited to 3. A buyer waiting on issue 4 can open a case | help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115015628347, 115015654988, etsy.com/legal/sellers |
| Variations (Issue 1, Issue 2, Season) on one listing | Not possible: "you can't offer variations for digital items" | 115015628347, 115015628707 |
| Do past buyers get updated files? | Etsy does not say. Community reports mostly say buyers keep what they bought. Treat it as no: never use a file swap to deliver a new issue | 115013328108 |
| File limits | 5 files, 20 MB each. Types include pdf, zip, epub. **.html is not allowed**, so the page turning version ships inside a zip | 115015628347 |
| "Subscription" in a title | No rule found, but it promises something Etsy cannot bill for. Do not use the word | 115015628707 |

A real competitor does exactly what we should: MazzysMail sells one listing
per monthly kids' issue at £4.65 and writes "This purchase is for the December
2026 issue only, not a subscription".

**Recommendation: one listing per issue, plus bundles.**

- **Single issues.** Numbered, with the month in the title ("Issue 1, December
  2026"). Collectable, and every issue is a fresh listing that Etsy search
  treats as new.
- **A season bundle** once three issues exist: Issues 1 to 3 in one listing at
  a bundle price. It is its own listing with all three issues' files.
- **A back issues bundle** later (6 issues), the long tail.
- **Repeat buyers come back through Etsy.** The last page says when the next
  issue arrives and asks readers to favourite the shop, so Etsy tells them.
  That keeps the whole loop on Etsy.

### What the policy says about pointing buyers to the website

The fees policy section is now "Off Platform Transactions", and the policy page
was updated on 5 October 2026 (etsy.com/uk/legal/policy/off-platform-transactions-policy/1254654515806).
It bans instructing a buyer to purchase through a personal website, off
platform "free items ... or other financial incentives that encourage members
to purchase off Etsy", and QR codes that send members off Etsy. The seller
policy bans adding buyers to a mailing list without consent.

| Line in the paid paper | Verdict |
|---|---|
| guidedchildhood.com/etsy-bonus, written as text, for a free gift by email | Allowed in practice and common, with one caution: the page itself must not sell anything that is also on Etsy. Today it points once at the founding member offer for the app. That is the grey bit; it is a different product, so I would keep it, but it is Justin's call |
| A QR code to any website page | Not allowed. The paid paper has none (the free Planet Post's QR is fine: it is never sold on Etsy) |
| "Subscribe on our website", "cheaper on our site", any price for a website plan | Not allowed. Never in an Etsy file |
| The apps page describing the Guided Childhood app with a plain web address | Fine if it informs and does not sell: no prices, no "subscribe", no code |
| "The next issue is in our Etsy shop on 18 December" | Safe, it is on Etsy |

## 2. What sells (ideas only, nothing copied)

| Title | Format | Price |
|---|---|---|
| A UK children's weekly | 28 pages, ages 7 to 14 | £2.85 an issue, about £2.20 on subscription |
| A UK weekly for 8 to 14s | 32 pages, fixed regulars, a two sided debate | 6 issues for £1, then about £145 a year |

| Etsy "kids newspaper printable" | Mostly blank templates and Canva birthday papers | £1.59 to £8.38 |
| Etsy Christmas "North Pole" papers | Personalised, yearly | £2.79 to £13.03 (71 reviews at the top) |
| Etsy monthly kids' issue (MazzysMail) | 24 pages, US Letter, puzzles, answer keys | £4.65 an issue |

What makes them sell: a clear age band and page count in the title and first
photo; the cover as photo 1 with the contents printed on it; fixed regulars so
a child knows where things are; a family debate page; good news only, never
scary; puzzles with answers; "screen free", "quiet time", "stocking filler",
"homeschool" in the tags; Christmas above everything.

The gap: nobody on Etsy sells a ready made, real newspaper issue for families,
and nobody joins news with calm tech habits. No Etsy listing we found ships a
page turning version.

## 3. Turning pages on a device

| Format | iPhone | Android | Computer | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PDF set to open as a two page spread | Files scrolls down, not across; "Open in Books" gives swipe page turns. The spread setting is ignored | Drive and most viewers scroll down | Acrobat and Preview honour the spread | Ship it: opens everywhere, prints, no account |
| Single file HTML flipbook (in a zip) | Opens from Files in a preview that may not run scripts, so the file falls back to a swipe left and right page strip with no script at all | Opens in Chrome with the full page turn | Full page turn, two page spreads, arrow keys | Ship it: the "turn the pages" moment, offline, no account |
| Fixed layout EPUB | Apple Books gives a real page curl | Needs an app and an account | Apple Books on a Mac only | Optional extra for Apple families, not the core |

Prototype built and screenshotted today (Chrome, 1400 wide and phone 390
wide): spreads with a 3D turn on a computer or a tablet held sideways, one page
at a time on a phone, swipe, tap the edges, arrow keys, and a reduced motion
setting. One real finding: a whole newspaper page on a phone held upright is
too small to read, so the flipbook needs tap to zoom, and the paid paper's body
text goes up a size from the Planet Post's.

**Recommendation:** the download is five files. A4 colour PDF, US Letter
colour PDF, ink friendly PDF, a screen reading PDF (single pages, linked
contents, opens in Books on an iPhone) and a zip holding the HTML flipbook with
a one page "how to open it" sheet. The first PDF page also says how to read it
on each device.

How it stays clearly different from the free Planet Post: its own name and
masthead, 12 pages not 4, no FREE burst, an issue price and month on the
masthead, a page turning version, and its own signature colour beside the
shared Happy News finish (white stock, ink lines, crayon fills, bursts,
ribbons, Nunito 900).

Build: a standalone pack in `content/printables/<slug>/` (print.html, the
flipbook, listing images) on the same Playwright pipeline as the five Etsy
packs (`build-pack.mjs`), porting the Planet Post's components and its rules
(the 740 by 1000 page that fits A4 and Letter, the type floor, 46 px write in
lines). The pack pipeline renders offline in seconds and gives one HTML source
for the PDFs and the flipbook; the Planet Post itself is not changed.

Price: £5.99 list, £4.49 in the 25 percent sale (above the £4 floor). Season
bundle of three: £13.99 list, £10.49 in the sale.
