## 1. The goal and the maths

£4,000 a month recurring is about 325 paying families, which at the 4
percent benchmark is about 8,000 stage checks, plus whatever the playlist
and the schools add. Parents and the playlist are the main road; schools are
third and are distribution first, revenue in the summer term. Today the
figure is £0 and nobody has paid. The first milestone is the first paying
parent.

| Rung | Monthly recurring | What has to be true |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First paying parent | £7.99 | The funnel closes once |
| 50 founders | £399.50 | The founding sprint lands by 13 November |
| 100 standard families | £1,299 plus founders | 2,500 stage checks, Christmas to February |
| 100 playlist Pro parents | £595 | 1,000 activated playlist parents at a 10 percent upgrade |
| 5 schools | £2,475 to £3,975 a year | Pilots teach in November, budgets in May |
| 325 families | £4,000 | 8,000 stage checks, spring 2027 at the current rhythm |

The council's rule stands: £0 for thirty days, pay only to scale a post that
already moved stage checks, creators after December and gifted first.

## 4. The channels

### 4.1 LinkedIn, Guided Childhood, parents and schools

Justin's own profile is the channel. Posts at 10:00. Four a week during the
sprint (Monday research, Wednesday mechanism, Friday schools, Sunday
founder), three after 13 November. Parents on Monday, Wednesday and Sunday;
schools on Friday with the pilot link in the first comment. The enemy is the
cliff edge and it appears every week in a different sentence. Formats: the
research card for evidence posts (the proven long form), eight to twelve
lines and a real photo for story posts, the explainer film once a fortnight,
a screen recording for mechanism posts. The CTA stack is the 2026 one: a
real question only a parent could answer, the link in the first comment,
the principled repost ask. Reply in the first hour. The weekly research pack
and the register already feed this; the content pack carries the first two
weeks.

### 4.2 Instagram and Facebook, the family account, in Justin's voice

The handover runs over two weeks, six posts, explain before you change.
Justin tells it to camera in the first person; Natalia is named as the
person they knew and is never shown new on camera. The Covid babysitter post
is the one that travels and it runs third, never first. After the reveal the
four day rhythm takes over: Founder Monday (the twelve chapters, told by
Justin), Research Wednesday (the tonight line), Service Friday (a real
screen, the stage check), Happy News Saturday (the carousel). Reels are the
reach format, so Monday and Saturday can each be a Reel. Stories four a week.
Facebook gets the warmer, longer version with the link in the body on
Fridays. The migration measurement gate from the switch checklist applies:
record both platforms at 24 hours and seven days.

### 4.3 Facebook for schools, and the parent groups

Teachers are parents too, so school posts on the family page are the Friday
service post twice a term, no separate schools page. The real school channel
is LinkedIn and named email. Parent groups (Facebook, Mumsnet, the Reddit
parenting communities) run on the playbook's rule: be useful first, one
mention in ten, never as the brand, never scheduled through a tool.

### 4.4 The Social Billboard

Facebook and Instagram run the fourteen day plan written on 30 September,
with Post 1 only after the three fixes. The LinkedIn company page posts once
a week, the evidence post with the Ofcom feed finding first. Justin's own
LinkedIn carries the playlist once in the six weeks, on 12 October.

YouTube is the playlist's natural home and gets a channel plan:

- One audience: a UK parent of a child aged 3 to 14 who is tired of what
  comes after the video ends. One promise: a session with a beginning and an
  end, built around your choices, checked and dated.
- The four video engine: four evergreen videos that refer to each other
  through end screens. The method (how we check a channel), the comparison
  (what happens after the video ends on YouTube Kids against a checked
  playlist), the session (how to give a child twenty minutes of YouTube
  without the feed), and the evidence (what Ofcom found about feeds and what
  a parent can do tonight). Scripts are in the content pack.
- The weekly Safe Watch short: ninety seconds, the playlist of the week, why
  it won, the honest limits, the free link. Cut from the same recording as
  the weekly email, which already has its approval gate.
- Packaging: title first, under sixty characters, problem led; three
  thumbnail lines per video; the first fifteen seconds state the problem,
  the promise and why Justin; the ask arrives by ninety seconds and again at
  the end; end screens send to the next of the four.
- Format: Justin to camera, clean, with real screen recordings of the
  product, no character animation, no stock. The explainer template cuts
  the Shorts.
- Measure playlist activations, not views.

### 4.5 TikTok, Reels and Shorts

The silent UGC batch: ten hooks on a held real shot, cut to the product,
end on the stage check. Thirty seconds of effort a day once the product
scene is recorded once. The explainer vertical cut goes here too. Ten hooks
are in the content pack.

check. The playlist weekly email resumes only on a fresh approved handoff.

## 7. The daily production system

A scheduled task runs every weekday at 06:30 and writes "Today's content"
into the Control Room: the post for each platform due that day, pasted
ready, the first comment, the image or film to attach, the one thing to
film or photograph, and the numbers to read. Justin's twenty minutes at
08:05 is then approve, post, reply. Nothing posts itself. The Friday review
decides what repeats. When the task cannot find a ready asset it says so
and names the evergreen alternative; it never invents a post to fill a slot.
