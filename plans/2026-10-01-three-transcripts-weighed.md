# Three transcripts weighed: app growth, creator deals, LinkedIn rules

Justin, 1 October 2026, pasted three transcripts and asked for the best of
them in the marketing plan: Joseph Choy on organic app growth, Evan
Yadagari on influencer deals, and Toby on LinkedIn. Every point goes through
the feedback filter before anything is built from it. What they could see:
three app and creator businesses in the United States, none selling a child
safety product to UK parents, none bound by our commitments.

## The three things that change because of these transcripts

1. Every Guided Childhood LinkedIn post goes out at 10:00, four a week in the sprint, three after.
2. The enemy is named every week: the cliff edge. Never a platform, never the ban.
3. The LinkedIn profile is rebuilt as a landing page this week: banner, About, one Featured link to the stage check.

Everything else they recommend we either already do, or declined for a reason that is written above.
