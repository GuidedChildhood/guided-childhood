# 18 November 2026: the French lessons rebuilt with our friends and real French voices

Justin: rebuild her lesson video with our own characters and better animation on
Higgsfield, for every lesson, with the engine we used for the digital lessons and
the correct voices. Pilot: Year 3 Colours, lesson 1 (the free taster).

## What her lesson is today (checked 18 November)
- An interactive deck of 14 slides (I can, my sound, Ça va, new colours, the song,
  repeat, remember the word, listen and choose, what's missing, splat, how did it
  go, bravo, where is Pierre). Framed in our teach page.
- One real video: the colours song, `songs/chanson-des-couleurs-1.mp4`.
- Every French word is spoken by the browser's built in speech voice
  (speechSynthesis, fr-FR), so the sound changes from laptop to laptop and is
  often a robot. This is the "correct voices" problem.
- 14 lessons have decks so far (Year 3 Colours, Year 4 Je peux), growing to about
  84 as her units arrive.

## The rebuild, per lesson (the child films engine, not a new one)
1. **Voices.** Every French word and phrase recorded once by a native French
   voice on Higgsfield (text2speech, ElevenLabs), two voices: Nova soft, Bloop
   playful. Transcribed back and checked before use, as the films are. About
   0.15 credits a line, about 20 lines a lesson.
2. **Our own song.** New lyrics on the lesson's words, our tune (ElevenLabs
   Music, about 4 credits), so nothing of hers is copied and we own it.
3. **The film.** Nova and Bloop in their French look, from the approved files,
   animated in code (the whisper or shout motion, GSAP and HyperFrames, as the
   child films) with at most two generated hero moments (Wan 2.7, 7.5 credits
   each for 5 seconds). Captions burned in, the words on screen as they are sung.
4. **Into the lesson.** The song film and the word recordings play from our
   lesson page and teach bar. Phase 2, if the pilot lands: the deck itself in our
   player, with these recordings in place of the robot voice.
