## What Miliga is (read 6 November 2026, our words)

- **Format:** a printable PDF chore chart, emailed on payment, nothing posted.
  Print on A4 (or A3 at a copy shop), reprint each week or laminate.
- **Ages:** 6 to 10.
- **Mechanic:** a football league. Every job done is a goal on the weekly
  chart. On Sunday the child counts goals, is paid in collectible cards, and
  swaps cards for prizes. The prizes are time with a parent, not things:
  choose tea, play a match together, watch the game together.
- **Two tiers:** £6 (chart, 12 player cards, parent guide) and £7 (adds a
  player of the week diploma, 12 achievement medals, a colouring version),
  both shown against a crossed out £12 and £13. Spanish shop, claims 773
  families and 4.8 stars.
- **Photos:** flat infographic tiles on a green pitch background: the chart
  with a fan of cards, what is included, the rules, before and after
  behaviour, three steps. No lifestyle photos.
- **Why parents buy:** it ends the nagging about jobs, the child asks to do
  them, and the reward is time together. The hook is the swap day.

We are making our own product in the same category. None of their art,
names, theme or wording. No football.

## Our version: The Star Swap Shop

**The idea in one line:** your child earns stars all week, and on swap day
spends them in the family shop, where screen time is one shelf and time with
you is the other.

That is the Guided Childhood twist. Earn Your Screen Time makes screen time
the reward. The Swap Shop puts screens next to things that are better, at
prices the family sets, so a child often chooses the park or the bake. And
the collector cards are healthy tech habits, so the thing worth collecting
is the habit, not the screen.

**House rules carried over:** one star is five minutes, the same deal as the
app and every chart. Earned stars are never taken back. Prices are agreed
together and written on the menu.

## Contents (one listing, one tier)

1. Cover
2. Two minute parent guide: how a week runs, swap day, the five shop rules
3. Weekly chart for 4 to 7, Pebble: picture jobs, stars to colour
4. Weekly chart for 8 to 10, Bloop: jobs plus three habit rows, star tally
5. The Swap Shop menu poster: three shelves. Screen time (1 star, 5 minutes),
   Time together (blank tags, with ideas: choose tea, a board game with me,
   a bake, a bike ride), and a Big save shelf (film night, a day out)
6. Swap tickets to cut out: screen tickets and time together tickets
7. 12 habit collector cards over two sheets, each a Planet Friend or DiGi
   doing the habit: screens off at the first ask, ended a game calmly, asked
   before a new app, devices sleep downstairs, played outside, read before
   bed, and six more. A card is given when the habit is spotted, and a full
   set earns the certificate
8. Star Swapper of the Week certificate
9. Last page via `pack-finish.js`: the Passport stickers for the Pebble and
   Bloop pages, under the doing, then the free Starter Pack at /etsy-bonus,
   plus one line that the app keeps the stars for you (no link, no price)

Formats: A4 and US Letter in colour, an ink friendly version (the stars and
jar on the charts are there to colour), and a phone set (both charts and the menu). Approved
cutouts only, from `public/digi-squad/friends/`.

## Stages

Pebble 4 to 7 and Bloop 8 to 10, under the doing. Joins the Go further strip
once listed, by flipping its flag in `passport-data.js`. Orbit and older are
left out: a sticker chart reads young to an 11 year old.

## Price

List at £7.99 with the shop's standing 25 percent sale, so buyers pay £5.99,
level with Miliga's £6. One tier only: a second tier splits reviews. Under £8, so no Etsy ads, per
the launch plan. Listing fees and the sale itself are Justin's to set.

## Rules

No dashes in customer copy. No medical or outcome claims (says what the
family does, never that it fixes behaviour). No links or prices for anything
off Etsy inside the PDF.
