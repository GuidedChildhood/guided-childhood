# The Social Billboard: competitor creative audit and the launch creative set

1 October 2026. Prepared for Justin. Sits beside the carousel plan
(`plans/2026-10-01-tsb-instagram-carousels.md`) and the parent launch plan of
30 September. Two researchers ran in parallel: one read every direct
competitor's live ads, landing pages and app store copy; one read the 2025 to
2026 evidence on which creative formats convert for subscription apps. Both
reports were weighed through the feedback filter before anything was built.

## What the competitors actually run

| Competitor | Leads with | Longest running ad seen | Offer |
|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube Kids | "An app made just for children", simplicity, parent tools | No Google owned ads in GB or US. One creator ad from 3 Sept 2026 | Free with ads |
| Qustodio | "The internet isn't childproof. But your kid's phone can be." Fear then control. CTA "Protect My Child Today" | 12 March 2026 | Free tier, 7 days, no card |
| Bark | "No parent can watch every app... but Bark can." Predator and self harm warning lists. Creator UGC with "comment BARK" | 11 June 2025 | 7 days, from $14 a month. No GB ads |
| Kidslox | "Protect your child from addiction!" | Nothing long running, ten videos all started 30 Sept 2026 | 3 days |
| Yoto | "Stories for them. Five minutes of peace for you." Calm, relief, independence | 13 August 2026 | Hardware, bundles |
| Tonies | "so you don't have to stay for hours", "Childhood is better without screens" | 22 July 2026 | Hardware |
| Moshi | "scientifically proven to help kids sleep faster" | No ads | 7 days, card required (Trustpilot 2.0, billing complaints) |
| Kidoodle, Jellies, Sensical, KIDOZ | "humans not algorithms", "safest free streaming" | No ads. Kidoodle's parent in receivership 31 Aug 2026, Jellies dormant, Sensical US only | Free with ads |
| Streamu, Kivvie, KidScreen, WhitelistVideo (UK whitelist tools) | "No recommendations, no autoplay, no endless scroll" | No ads | £6.99 a month, or free |

Three things fall out of that table.

- **The category sells fear or relief.** Parental controls sell fear then
  control. Audio players sell the parent's relief. Nobody is selling the
  session: the parent choosing its shape and it ending by itself.
- **The claims closest to ours are unadvertised.** "Checked by a person" and
  "no algorithm" are claimed by three free video apps, all of which are
  dormant, sold, or in receivership. In the UK feed right now nobody is
  saying it.
- **Nobody offers what we offer.** Seven days free is the norm, card up front
  is rare and punished. Nobody offers a permanent free playlist with no card.
  That is the offer, and it goes in the image, not just on the page.

What we can own, in the site's own words: it stops when it ends; you set the
shape of the session (age, tone, type, time of day, length), not a channel
whitelist; the videos are still YouTube, only the feed is gone; bedtime and
after school are different playlists; siblings get different shelves; no
comments; no card.

## What the format evidence says

Motion's 2026 benchmarks (578,750 ads, $1.29bn of Meta spend, September 2025
to January 2026, "hit rate" = share of ads that spend ten times the account
median; 5 to 8 per cent is normal):

| Format or tactic | Hit rate |
|---|---|
| Text only asset | 11.60% |
| Hook: newness | 11.37% |
| Hook: price anchor | 10.89% |
| Letter format | 10.83% |
| Hook: offer only | 9.29% |
| Confession hook | 8.74% |
| Offer first banner | 8.68% |
| Founder format | 8.57% |
| UGC | 7.56% |
| High production | 6.97% |
| Us vs them | 6.52% |
| Testimonial | 6.50% |
| Review highlight | 4.89% |
| Animation | 4.57% |

Plain text beats everything. Motion names parenting as a category the letter
format suits best, because "it doesn't scream ad, so people drop their guard
and actually read".

On trust: Harris Poll for the 4As, June 2026, 2,131 US adults: 78 per cent
say AI makes ads feel less authentic, 63 per cent are less likely to buy
from a brand using AI generated ads. Ipsos and Newhouse, May 2026: human
made ads 14 per cent stronger short term. Mosseri, 1 January 2026:
"Rawness isn't just aesthetic preference anymore, it's proof." Pattern
Brands: in three of four tests the uglier ad wins. So: plain colour, big
words, no stock children, no gradients, no synthetic parent, ever.

On the offer: ChartMogul 2026, 200 products: 80 per cent of free trials take
no card; no card trials convert 4 to 15 per cent to paid, card trials 25 to
60 per cent, but no card gets several times the sign ups. For a parent
product at launch, volume and feedback matter more than conversion, so no
card stays and goes in the creative.

## What we take, and what we leave

| Point | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Letter from the founder, text only | Adopt | Top of the evidence, suits parenting, and it is already Justin's voice. |
| Offer in the image | Adopt | Highest hit rate tactic. "First playlist free. No card." on every creative. |
| One line contrast static (Qustodio's most repeated family, Lingokids' "There's YouTube Kids. Then there's this.") | Adopt, in our words | "The videos come from YouTube. The feed around them does not." Never name a competitor on the creative. |
| "What isn't here" list | Adopt | The site's own section; the category's feature lists are the relief after the scene, not the hook. |
| Moment of the day pair (Yoto's bedtime and long drive) | Adopt | The site already splits bedtime from after school. |
| Real parent creator video with a comment keyword (Bark, Yoto, Tonies) | Adopt later | Needs real parents and permission. Never synthetic. Justin's own screen recording Reel (weekly sheet, Post 2) is the first version. |
| Screenshot or in app proof static | Adopt when the screenshot exists | The launch plan forbids an invented interface. Slot built in the template; needs a real phone screenshot of a built playlist ending. |
| Stat card with a named Ofcom source | Park for LinkedIn | The launch plan rules out a frightening statistic in the parent feed, and the numbers are about 11 to 17 year olds. The research card already lives in the LinkedIn lane. |
| Us vs them comparison table | Park for the landing page | Average in the feed, over 10 per cent trial conversion on comparison pages (Podia). A "YouTube Kids vs The Social Billboard" page is a site task. |
| Fear hooks ("Protect your child from addiction!") | Decline | Not our voice, and the category is saturated with it. |
| Review stack, five stars | Decline for now | Lowest scoring format, and we have no permissioned quotes yet. One real quote, when it exists, beats five stars. |

## The launch creative set

Seven statics, 1080 x 1350, in the SB system, rendered from
`tools/tsb-playlist-card/decks/statics-01-launch-set.json`. Each works as a
feed post, a paid static, or the opening slide of a carousel.

1. **The letter.** "A note from the dad who built it. I did not build this
   because YouTube is bad." Text only, white, signed Justin, offer pill.
   Justin should swap the second sentence for one true scene of his own.
2. **Offer first.** "Your first playlist is free. One child profile. No card
   needed. It stops when it ends." Yellow.
3. **The contrast.** "The videos come from YouTube. The feed around them does
   not." Black, the feed line in yellow.
4. **What isn't here.** The site's six line list under "Most of the work went
   into what we left out." White.
5. **Bedtime.** "Calm. Twenty minutes. Then it stops." Black.
6. **After school.** "After school is not bedtime. The playlist should not be
   either." Yellow.
7. **The question.** "Is YouTube a rabbit hole in your house?" then the
   treadmill, the filters, the other way round. White.

Hooks follow the field pattern: a question naming the fight, a confession in
the first person, the offer stated plainly. None uses fear.

## How to run it

- Organic first, as the launch plan says. Post 1 (letter) on Facebook with
  the link in the body, and on Instagram with link in bio. Post 2 (offer) as
  the pinned post on both.
- If the optional £10 a day test runs (launch plan, second phase): three
  ads, the letter, the offer, and the contrast, one audience (UK parents),
  sign up as the event. Kill anything that has not produced a sign up in
  £20 of spend. The letter is the expected winner.
- Track in the launch tracker: reach, link clicks, registrations, activated
  parents (opened a first playlist), per creative.
- Rebuild the set every two weeks from the lines parents quote back in the
  comments. Slide one of carousel two already does this.
