# How apps explain "how it works" in video, and what it changed in the DiGi film

2 October 2026. Researched for the v2 of `videos/2026-10-01-how-digi-works`.

## Per example

- **Duolingo, Birdbrain (blog, 2020).** Duo the owl with a glowing brain,
  then one named learner and a difficulty gauge. The science is a plain rule
  told through a worked example. Reported A/B lift in learning and
  retention, no figures. Their retention post names Ebbinghaus 1885 before
  the new model, and reports a 9.5 per cent lift in retention for practice
  sessions.
- **Duolingo Max (2023).** No animation of the model. Characters personify
  the AI; the copy stresses that humans write the scenarios and review the
  output.
- **Headspace Guide to Meditation (Netflix, 2021).** Mental processes as
  abstract shapes, "feeling over meaning", one orange dot as the hero.
- **Noom.** The inside of a head as the stage for the mechanism.
- **Notion AI (Buck).** The AI is a face; thinking is eyebrows. Buck's rule:
  no sparkle and magic tropes.
- **Grammarly, How Grammarly works.** Six numbered steps with humans named
  at each stage, the feedback loop as step six.
- **Khanmigo, Bark, Good Inside, Finch, Lingokids, Qustodio.** No mechanism
  animation found. Steps and screenshots; trust carried by a badge or one
  stated guardrail.
- **Vox (Joss Fong).** Withhold the result until the viewer understands why
  it is hard. Perfect correspondence between what is seen and what is said.
  One moment per film that sticks. A music change about every twenty
  seconds. One thing animates at a time.
- **Kurzgesagt.** The script is the bottleneck, a dozen drafts, experts
  check it. Science entities become characters.
- **Length evidence.** Wistia 2025 to 2026: under a minute holds about half
  the audience to the end; most viewers drop around the halfway mark.
  Vidyard 2025 (943,305 videos): 65 per cent completion under a minute.
  Front load the key message.

## What it changed in our film

| Pattern | Verdict | In the film |
|---|---|---|
| One named question first, result withheld | Adopt | The pyjamas moment and the parent's message are the first eleven seconds; the answer arrives at 34 seconds. |
| Stage the mechanism inside the head, not a flowchart | Adopt | DiGi's brain lifts out; the research pours into a bubble; six nodes light inside the brain; the labels sit on cream cards over the footage, never boxes and arrows. |
| Name the inputs with a date | Adopt, with more than three | Six study cards from `digi/02-scientists.md`. The research says three is the ceiling for recall; we keep six because Justin asked for the actual studies, and each is on screen for about a second. If the film tests weak at beat four, cut to three. |
| One thing moves at a time, the camera follows it | Adopt | Each clip has a slow push in; overlays arrive one element at a time. |
| Humans visible in the loop | Adopt | "A person approves anything new" on the rails card; "did it work" from real houses. |
| Front load, under 90 seconds, no logo intro | Adopt | 66 seconds, no intro, the brand only on the end card. |
| Music change every twenty seconds | Park | No sound yet. The next pass adds a voice and a bed. |
| Sparkle and magic tropes | Decline | The glow is warm light, not sparkle. |
