# 23 November 2026: call with Oak National Academy (ecosystem partnerships)

Justin spoke with Oak's ecosystem partnerships lead (introduced by Cris
Heartford) for about 20 minutes. Oak's agenda: how it works with EdTech, and
access to its open licence tools. The recording stays with Justin and is not
kept in the repo; these are the useful points.

## What Oak said
- **The home and parent gap is real.** Parents have few places to get sound
  advice, even though government and schools talk about it a lot. Oak backs
  products that carry its resources into the home. Its official line is that
  schooling happens in school, but reach and impact come first.
- **Our tone landed.** "It feels like you're standing alongside the parent
  rather than preaching at them." The parents we want are the ones who want
  to be spoken to that way. Private feedback: do not quote him publicly
  without asking.
- **No contradiction in using online tools to teach safer online use.**
  Parents look for help online, so that is where they will find us.
- **The API** is free, under the Open Government Licence, free for commercial
  use, and **updated for the latest RSHE across the key stages**. Some
  subjects include third party content (for example White Rose Maths), which
  must be cited correctly. Use as much or as little as needed. Oak measures
  only whether a key is active.
- **Oak's GitHub has knowledge graphs (a curriculum ontology)** that link
  prior knowledge across subjects and key stages. This could map what a child
  needs before a lesson.
- **The partnership is light touch:** self service, with a help desk. A next
  tier exists for larger partners and **case studies of unusual uses of the
  API**. He called our use "quite unique".
- **Schools and Academies Show:** everyone talks about the dangers, and the
  research on how young people feel, but very few active solutions are on the
  table. "You'll be pushing against an open door."

## Pilot advice (the most useful part)
- EdTech buying now happens at **trust level**, not school level.
- Implementation managers and heads of IT are the gatekeepers, and each hears
  from 10 to 20 companies a week.
- **Don't open with "a new product".** Lead with what very few do: **PSHE,
  and engagement and relationships with parents**. Both cost schools a lot of
  staff time, and almost no technology addresses them.
- Don't take it personally if getting in is slow.
