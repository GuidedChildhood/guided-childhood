# 22 November 2026: Me v my grown up

Justin, 22 November: "go ahead with recommendations". The first recommendation
from the French games recheck (plans/2026-11-22-french-games-review.md): a two
player game for a child and their grown up, modelled on the scheme's grown up
battle game, built for our product.

## What it is
- On the child's link (/k/[token]/versus), from a tile under Games.
- One phone or tablet on the table between them. The grown up's half is turned
  round to face them, the child's half faces the child. Both see the same
  question and the same answers.
- First to tap the right answer wins the point. A wrong tap freezes that player
  for the rest of that question, so guessing fast does not pay.
- After each question, the lesson's own why line, as "Talk about it".
  That line is the point of the game: a conversation, not a score.
- First to 5 wins. Then: play again, or back home.

## Where the questions come from
The questions this child's own lessons already asked (the same pool the
stage check uses, lib/pathway/child-stage-check.ts), so nothing is new and
every answer was taught. Fewer than 3 questions: the page says to do a few
lessons first. Never the parent facing stage bank.

## Rules
- Nothing saved, no new table, no migration. No stars, so it is never a way to
  farm screen time; it is a together game.
- Big targets, reduced motion respected, house tokens, no dashes in copy.
- Safeguarding flagged questions are left out of the game (they belong in the
  lesson, with the tell page beside them, not in a race).
