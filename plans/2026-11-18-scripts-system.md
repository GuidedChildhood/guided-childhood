# Scripts that help in the moment, 18 November 2026

Justin: "a super easy system of scripts that really helps the parents",
reminding them, encouraging and supporting them.

## Honest state today
- Content is strong: 342 scripts, every one has a Say this line, and the detail
  page has Say this, Not this, Before the moment, Rehearse with DiGi and Did it work.
- Use is weak: 52 script completions in total, ever.
- Why: it is a library a parent has to remember to visit. Nothing brings the
  right words to them at the moment. The page stacks six sections, and the age
  grid is a wall of identical cards with long titles and one tag.

## Research (agent report, this session)
- If/then plans ("When X, I say Y") beat plain goals (Gollwitzer; bias corrected
  effect is modest, so no outcome promises).
- Stress shrinks working memory, so one line first, big.
- Find by moment or feeling (Headspace SOS, Calm, Good Inside, Parently).
- Rehearsal with your own child is linked to bigger effects (Kaminski 2008).
- Just in time prompts from real events, capped and rotated, with "send nothing"
  as the default; prompt effects fade in 2 to 4 weeks (HeartSteps).
- Mobbin: Headspace "What is on your mind", Calm "How are you feeling",
  Quizlet and Duolingo one card stacks.

## Build, phase 1: the page
1. Opens on "What is happening?" with 7 moment tiles (Turning it off, Mealtimes,
   School mornings, Bedtime, Brothers and sisters, The phone ask, Something online).
2. A tile opens a one card at a time stack: the line to say, big; tap for Then do
   and If they push back; swipe to the next; Save to My scripts.
3. My scripts: up to five pinned, shown first, and on Home.
4. The age grid becomes a plain list with short titles, under the moments.

## Build, phase 2: the right words at the right moment
5. When the parent's screen timer ends, the turning it off line shows on their phone.
6. A weekly "words for this week" push from the worry they raised, capped at two
   a week, rotated, never more than one a day, off with one tap.
7. Next morning, one tap: Worked, Sort of, Not this time. It ranks what shows next.
