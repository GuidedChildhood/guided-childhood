# Big tech fines and legal cases: the research package and the six part thriller

Claimed 27 August 2026, this session's lane (research and content). Justin's brief,
in his words tidied: a series on the fines and all government or legal cases on big
tech, covered from the first to the most recent. Research and uncover whether the
political reasons are real or whether the cases are just about the impact on the
companies. If the fines are fair, how should the funds be spent, would that be good
or bad for children, and what is the better answer. Find examples where this has
happened before (tobacco, opioids) and whether the model could be applied to the
real drivers of children's mental health, and why that does not happen. Track where
the money generated actually goes. Deliver contrarian headlines on six part
thriller posts. Also answer Jonathan M. Wicks's settlement comment.

## Pipeline

1. Six flexed STORM lenses in parallel (kids-research structure, lens flexing rule):
   - The Litigator: what each judgment and consent decree actually requires,
     earmarks, enforcement teeth, no admission clauses. This also absorbs the
     Litigator V3 that the Meta settlement briefing named as its missing lens.
   - The Fines Historian: the full timeline from the first case to 26 August 2026,
     precursors included (IBM, AT&T), Microsoft to the Meta settlement.
   - The Political Economist: why cases land when they do, electoral and
     geopolitical timing, EU versus US motives, regulatory capture, fines as a
     cost of doing business.
   - The Money Tracker: where settlement and fine money actually went (tobacco
     MSA diversion, opioid earmarks, the FTC 5 billion to the Treasury general
     fund), and what spending would actually move child mental health.
   - The Skeptic: kills weak claims about materiality, causation, and moral panic.
   - The Child and the Frontline: whether any fine ever changed a feed, and youth
     voice on where money should go.
2. Contradiction map, V1 briefing on report-template.html, citation-verifier
   agents over every ledger row, V2 to briefings/2026-08-27-big-tech-fines-v2.html.

   content/packs/2026-08-27-big-tech-fines/.

5. Reply to Jonathan M. Wicks finalised against the verified record.
