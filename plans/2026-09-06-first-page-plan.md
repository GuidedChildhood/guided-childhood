Justin, 6 September 2026: "We do need to review maybe the most important page
that comes up before they go into the platform. Let's look to add the common
problems the user will have and that we will solve... the common questions
please research these... what we do to solve the problem, quests to develop

want to pay. All in the same /design as the happy news platform design we
have. Ideas welcome for an industry leading first page. Don't change what we

No code changes. This is research, then a canvas, then his pick.

## What the page does today

Ten sections: hero, the 11 o'clock moment, we drive you, inside the platform,
safety without surveillance, three acts, the five stages, Justin and the
research bench, real words and real price, FAQs, final call. It is a good
page. Three things are missing.

1. **The parent's own problem is never named as a list.** The page says what
   we are. It does not say "here is the thing happening in your house
   tonight, and here is exactly what we do about it". A parent scanning for
   their own situation has to translate.
2. **The proof numbers are stale and they undersell.** The page claims 160
   scripts and 100 lessons. The database holds 335 scripts and 147 lessons,
   142 research entries from 130 named sources, and 448 curriculum
   objectives. We are advertising half of what we built.
3. **The newest question has no home.** AI chatbots. 86 percent of children
   use AI and more than four in ten say no adult has ever talked to them
   about it (Common Sense Media, 2026). We have 33 school and AI scripts and
   16 AI lessons. The page mentions AI once, in a chip.

## The two directions on the canvas

**Direction A, your worry answered.** The page opens with a grid of the nine
worries in the parent's own words. They pick theirs and see three things:
what to do tonight, what we teach this term, and what it looks like by 16,
each with the real number behind it. Nobody in this category does a worry
picker, and it is the little brother of the stage check that already
converts. Recommended.

**Direction B, the wall chart.** One picture of ages 4 to 16 with the worry
that shows up at each age, what we teach there, and what the child earns.
Fewer words, understood in three seconds, more Happy News. Weaker on
tonight's problem, which is what makes a parent pay.

Both end on the same price block: four free days, founder 7.99 capped at 50,
standard 12.99, annual 99.
