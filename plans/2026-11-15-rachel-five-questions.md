Thank you. A voice note is fine if that is easier.

## Why these six

They are the questions onboarding researchers ask, in plain words:

- 1 and 2 are the "job" questions: the moment that pushed her, and the result
  she wanted. Products keep people when the first session answers that job.
- 3 finds the exact screen where she stopped without us leading her to it.
- 4 names the usual reasons so answering is one word.
- 5 is the "first win" question: what has to happen early for her to stay.
- 6 is the "would you miss it" test, the plainest read of whether it matters.
