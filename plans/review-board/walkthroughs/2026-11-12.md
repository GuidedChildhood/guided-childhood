# UX walkthrough, 2026-11-12
Verdict: 1 must fix, 0 should fix, 5 okay to ship. Nothing blocks shipping (the must fix is fixed in the same pull request).

**Who and how.** Not the Wednesday persona walk: this is the board's 390 and
1280 pass (ten out of ten plan, step 8), run by a session rather than the
routine. Chromium through Playwright, every `/ref-*` fixture page (56 of them)
at 390 by 844 and then 1280 by 800, against a local dev server with no
Supabase service key and no signed in family. Each page was checked for
horizontal overflow, more than one gold button in the first screen, a tap
target under 44px, and console errors.

## Must fix
- The child's five a day sent the same save again and again when the save failed. With `/api/kid/day` down, `/ref-kid-home` posted to it over a thousand times a minute and never finished loading. Three steps tick themselves (jobs, ask, move): a failed save takes the tick back, the state changes, the effect runs again and saves again. On a child's phone that is a flood every time the server has a bad moment. Proof: `components/kid/KidFiveADay.tsx`, the three auto tick effects; counted requests in Playwright. **Fixed here**: each self ticking step now tries once per visit, which took the count from over a thousand to 2 in eight seconds.

## Should fix
- None.

## Okay to ship
- `/ref-buttons` has eight gold buttons in its first screen. It is the button catalogue, so that is its job. Proof: `/ref-buttons` at both widths.
- `/ref-og-card` is wider than 390. It is the 1200 by 630 share card render target, never a phone page. Proof: `/ref-og-card` at 390.
- The sound toggle (41px) and the three put away ticks on the needs you card (28px) measure small, but each carries `.tap-target`, which stretches the touch area to 44px (`shared/tokens.css`, guarded by `scripts/check-tap-targets.mjs`). Proof: `/ref-kid-home`, `/ref-needs-you`.
- `/ref-email-monthly` logs a hydration warning at the root layout. It is the email preview fixture rendering a whole email document inside the app shell; no parent ever loads it. Proof: console on `/ref-email-monthly`.
- Thirteen fixtures log 401 or 500 responses. Each comes from a component asking a real API for data with no signed in family or no service key, which is what a fixture without a session does. None of them broke the page or overflowed. Proof: `ref-add-job`, `ref-digi-chat`, `ref-quest-board`, `ref-timer-nudge` and others in the run log.

## What I walked
All 56 `/ref-*` pages, both widths: 112 checks. Apart from the rows above,
every page loaded with a 200, held inside the viewport at 390 and 1280, had at
most one gold button in its first screen, and logged nothing. The board lens
asked for designed loading, empty and error states on the parent dashboard
routes: they exist at the dashboard segment (`app/(dashboard)/dashboard/loading.tsx`,
a skeleton in Home's real proportions, and `error.tsx`, a calm page with try
again and reload), so every tab under `/dashboard` inherits both. The hex
ratchet (`scripts/check-hex-ratchet.mjs`) already fails on a new hand typed
colour; its baseline was 35 lower than recorded and is now locked there.

- The Wednesday persona walk (landing page, starter pack, signup): that is the
