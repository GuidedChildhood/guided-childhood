## Mechanics

One migration (259). Each interactive slide inserts immediately before the
module's tryit slide, phase practise, 4 minutes, with a full teacher
script. The timing string in teacher_notes gains 4 minutes and names the
interactive. Idempotent: a module that already has an interactive slide is
skipped, which also protects eyfs-01. Snapshot first, RLS on the backup.
