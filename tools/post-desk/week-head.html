Guided Childhood

Posting Week

Three a week, two days apart, weekday mornings. Nine posts the week of 2 November split the attention and the week ran at half, so the Monday pass cut it back. The Post Desk is the library; this is the plan.

Held, not scheduled

These three wait on a document nobody has been able to open. They are written and checked, and the moment a PDF opens they join the plan.

The rules this plan is built on

  • One post a day at most. Two posts less than about eight hours apart eat each other's first hour.
  • The link goes in your first comment, never in the post. A year of links in the body produced thirteen clicks.
  • Be there for the first sixty minutes. Your own replies count as comments and lift the post.
  • A number in the first line, inside a complete sentence, and one question at the end next to the comment box.
  • Nothing here is posted or scheduled for you. You post, so the first comment lands the second it goes live.
  • Thirteen of these fourteen have no image, which every card says. A text post still works and several of your best were text, but it is the one thing on this page that is missing rather than decided.

Where the full text lives

  • Every post, first comment, share note and all thirteen long form issues are on the Post Desk.
