The rules every post already passed

  • A number inside a complete sentence that contradicts what the reader expects, in the first two lines. Not a bare number, not one delayed to sentence three.
  • No dashes anywhere, checked by machine over the full unicode dash range.
  • No link in the body. A year of in post links produced thirteen clicks, so the link goes in the first comment and the profile button does the work.
  • One closing question, nothing after it.
  • One scene from your own house at most, and never in line one. Every scene led opening in the past year sits in the bottom third.
  • Every figure traced to a primary source before the post exists.
  • Every Copy button puts plain text on the clipboard, never styled HTML, so nothing arrives in LinkedIn or Substack carrying this page's background colour.
  • Contractions, and at least one very short paragraph. Nine posts were rewritten on 5 November because none of them had either, and that uniformity is what reads as machine written.
