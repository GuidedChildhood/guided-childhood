The finding

A peer reviewed meta analysis this year, 46 studies, 79 effect sizes, on social media and adolescent mental health.

β = 0.061

The pooled result.

2

What it means

Below the threshold the researchers set for evidentiary value.

Statistically indistinguishable from noise. Pre registered on the Open Science Framework. Data and methodology public.

3

To be fair

That number is not zero.

Social media has a measurable association with child mental health. But 0.061 is smaller than the effect of wearing glasses.

That is not a metaphor I invented. It is how Andrew Przybylski described his own independent finding.
