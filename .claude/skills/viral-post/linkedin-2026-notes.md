# LinkedIn 2026, the sourced evidence behind this skill

Provenance for Part 5 of SKILL.md. Primary sources are Richard van der Blom's Algorithm Insights
2025 (1.8 million posts) and AuthoredUp's corpus studies (310,000 to 372,000 posts). Agency blog
numbers are folklore, trust the direction not the digits. Verify at source before quoting a figure
in public.

## The load bearing, data backed findings

- Dwell time is the master signal. A 30 second read beats 50 quick likes. (van der Blom)
- Golden hour is 30 to 60 minutes, early velocity sets reach. (van der Blom)
- Comments over about 15 words carry roughly double the weight of a short interaction. (van der Blom)
- Hook must land before the mobile fold, about 140 characters. Shorter hooks win, 1 to 5 word
  first lines earn the most, a number in the first line earns about 35 median likes vs 26 without.
  (AuthoredUp, 310,000 posts)
- Body sweet spot 1,300 to 2,500 characters, about 27 percent higher engagement than posts under
  400. (AuthoredUp, 372,000 posts)
- Format reach hierarchy, strongest to weakest: native PDF carousel (document), multi image,
  native video (contested, one analysis says LinkedIn cut video reach), text plus single image,
  polls (dead after the 2026 update). Carousels carry 2 to 3 times the dwell.
- Body links suppressed 25 to 60 percent. Link in the first comment. (multiple)
- Post 3 to 5 times a week, never two in a day, posts under about 8 hours apart cannibalise. (van
  der Blom)
- Reshare with commentary is treated near original content and far beats a bare reshare.
  (AuthoredUp, about 8 median likes vs 2)
- Topic authority (the 360Brew shift) rewards consistency in one lane over volume. About 90 days
  to compound.
- 2 to 4 hashtags, weak signal now.

## The single biggest correction to our prior guidance

The comment for a keyword DM lead magnet ("comment PATHWAY and I will DM you the link"), which the
beta 0.061 post used and which our older notes encouraged, is being actively throttled by the March
2026 authenticity update NLP classifiers. The penalty is silent and can hit account level reach,
worse for repeat use. Do not anchor the funnel on it. Replace with a genuine open question plus the
link in the first comment. A resource ask that truly serves the reader survives, a mechanic that
exists to manufacture comment volume does not.

## Caveats to carry

- The "comments worth 15 times a like" and "reply within 15 minutes equals 90 percent boost"
  numbers are agency folklore, no primary methodology. The direction holds, the digits do not.
- Format reach percentages vary post to post, the ranking is solid, the rates are not.
- Video reach is genuinely contested in 2026.
