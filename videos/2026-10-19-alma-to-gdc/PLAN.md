## What the research says (October 2026)
- Keep the account and pivot it; it keeps the trust and momentum (Adam Mosseri, via Influencer Marketing Hub).
- Tell the existing audience why, as a story; skip "we're happy to announce"; respect the relationship.
- Tease first, then announce; expect unfollows and let them go. The research says archive off topic posts, but handover post 1 promises nothing is deleted, so keep them up and pin the new posts on top instead.

## Rollout
1. Two days before: three stories, "something's changing here", a behind the scenes of the shop, a poll "are you a parent?".
2. Switch day: handle, name, bio, picture, post the walk and talk with the handover post 5 caption, pin it, stories retelling it casually. Old posts stay up, as post 1 promises.
3. That week: the Ready film, two rough talking heads. Pin Ready and the stage check post.
4. Judge it after two weeks on saves, shares and stage check clicks, not follower count.
