## Intent

A ten second product intro for guidedchildhood.com, to sit on the homepage and
in social embeds. It sells, it does not tour. The one loop the marketing story
tells: the nightly screen time fight, then the plan instead of the ban, then the
hook that every piece of Guided Childhood marketing ends on, identical every
time: *What stage is your child? Three questions, no sign up.*

Tone: Justin's voice. Warm, plain, direct. Big text, few words, one idea per
beat. Like Duolingo for getting your kid ready for a phone.

## Assets

- Captured from the live site by Step 1 (logo, brand tokens, homepage screens).

## Customizations

- No narration: ten seconds is too short for a voice to breathe, so on screen
  text carries the message. A light, warm music bed if one is available.
- Ends on the stage check hook, exactly as written above.

## Notes

- No dashes anywhere in on screen copy. Not in headings, not in buttons.
- Fonts: Nunito for display and body, IBM Plex Mono for eyebrows and labels.
- Colours: the site's own butter and ink palette from the captured tokens.
  No purple gradients, no generic AI patterns.
- Autonomous run: Justin asked for the video to be made and the preview
  opened, so flow is automation, storyboard no, mode autonomous.
