From Inspired by Alma to The Guided Digital Childhood · the video · 30 September 2026

How to use these cards

One video, about ninety seconds, for the Instagram and Facebook account the 6,000 already follow. Portrait, phone at eye level. Natalia opens and closes, because hers is the voice they know. Justin carries the middle. Every fact comes from the founding story canon; nothing new is claimed.

Read one card, stop, next card. Mistakes do not matter. The box at the bottom is the graphic or photo that lands on those words. Where it says PHOTO, that is a real picture from your own camera roll, never a stock image.

The rule that matters most is on card 5. The Covid line is the most valuable sentence we have and the easiest to ruin. Do not blame YouTube and do not say it was fine.

Card 1 of 8 · The heads up
NATALIA
[At the kitchen table. Straight to camera.]

This account is about to change its name.

A lot of you have been here since the beginning, so before it happens I wanted to tell you the whole story.

Graphic: the Inspired by Alma logo sits beside Natalia; a small mono label underneath types "since 2015". Nothing else. The change is not shown yet.
Card 2 of 8 · Where it started
NATALIA
[Fond. Let the first topper photo get its laugh.]

Alma was born in 2015, and I could not sit still.

So I started making cake toppers on a laptop in our bedroom, at night, while she slept.

PHOTO: the first cake topper, taped into a frame with a slight tilt, mono caption "2015. It was not very good." Then a quick stack of early order photos dealing in like cards.
Card 3 of 8 · The growth
JUSTIN
[Take over. Pace up a little.]

Toppers became party supplies. Supplies became events. Events became weddings and the big birthdays.

Then we opened our own shop in Bristol.

We loved all of it. Nothing here went wrong.

PHOTOS: a wedding table, the shop keys in an empty room, the shop front open. Each photo slides in as its word is said, three frames fanning out. On "Nothing here went wrong" they settle still.
Card 4 of 8 · The afternoon the tablet ruined
JUSTIN
[Slower. This is where the story turns.]

With Alma it never really came up. With Olga it did.

YouTube turned into a battle. And the mood of the whole house moved with how the handover went at five o'clock.

PHOTO: a child from behind with a tablet, ordinary living room, no faces. Graphic: a clock face draws to five o'clock and a mood line under it dips as the hand lands. Held.
Card 5 of 8 · The bit we do not usually say
JUSTIN
[Plain. No performance. Do not soften it and do not blame YouTube.]

Then Covid happened. Two little girls at home, a business to keep alive, and we moved house in the middle of it.

And we used YouTube as a babysitter. To survive.

We felt guilty about it then, and there is no point pretending otherwise now.

PHOTOS: packing boxes in the old house, an order pile on the floor, a screen glowing in the corner of the room. Real, unposed, from 2020. No graphic on this card at all. Let the photos and the words carry it. (Moving house during Covid is new to the canon. Confirm it and I add it to founding-story.md.)
Card 6 of 8 · Nine at night
JUSTIN
[Lift. Point at the table.]

The idea arrived at about nine at night, at this table, after one of those days.

Not a way to switch the devices off. A way to manage them, which is a completely different job.

So we built it. For our own family first.

PHOTO: the kitchen table, the real one, at night. Graphic: the DiGi star blooms on the table for the first time and the five Planet Friends pop in along a pathway line, ages 4 to 16 in mono beneath. This is the only place the product appears.
Card 7 of 8 · Not a ban, not no ban
JUSTIN
[Steady. The position, said once.]

Teo is four and mad about football. I cannot keep him off that road until he is sixteen and then wish him luck.

So it is not a ban. And it is not no ban. It is the bit in between.

PHOTO: Teo with a football. Graphic: "Not a ban." lands, "Not no ban." lands under it, then both slide apart and "The bit in between." arrives big in the gap with a butter marker sweep. Held two seconds.
Card 8 of 8 · The new name
NATALIA
[Back to Natalia. Warm. Hold the smile at the end.]

So from today this is The Guided Digital Childhood.

Same us. Same kitchen. Same account you followed for the cake toppers.

We have always made things that made family life a bit better. Some of them still are made of card and sugar. Some of them now run on a phone.
