# Read sheet: the three reels, talking head version

Justin, 15 October: "this is not in the format of the screen guy example."
The Family IT Guy format is a person to camera: a title card fixed at the
top, big word by word captions, one usable fix in 30 to 45 seconds. We cannot
make a person for it (a synthetic parent is the one thing we never do), so
the face is you, filmed on your phone. These are the three stories you picked,
rewritten to be said out loud.

Every fix in here is the app's own wording: the stopping script (free script
9671), the "everyone else" moment (moments library), and the first phone
setup (`/dashboard/phone-setup`). Your scene in each is from the GREEN list in
`content/brand-story/founder-context.md`: general screen struggles, controls
being complicated, the change of mind. Nothing about Alma, Olga or Teo by name.

## How to film (two minutes of setup)

1. Phone **portrait**, at eye level, about an arm's length away. Window light
   in front of you, never behind.
2. Quiet room. A kitchen or a desk is right; a mic in shot is fine.
3. One card at a time. Pause two seconds between cards. If you stumble, say
   the line again and carry on. The cutting tools remove the rest.
4. Say the last line exactly every time: "What stage is your child? Three
   questions, no sign up."
5. Send the three files over. I cut them with `talking-head-video`: title
   card at the top, word by word captions in Nunito with the gold highlight,
   the app screen dropped in where a card says so, and the stage check end.

## What these never say

No "safe", no "ready for social media", no promise about your child, no
platform as the villain, no statistic. Each one gives one thing to try
tonight and points at the stage check. If you want a line in your own words,
change it; just keep the last card exactly as written.
