## Intent

Justin, 16 October 2026: "make music video of Planet Friends characters with
motion graphics to fit like Gorillaz Damon Albarn type look". A virtual band
film: inked comic grit, a night palette, band cuts, one friend lit per verse.
The look nods to that style; the artist is never named in any public copy.

Full plan: plans/week-of-2026-10-12-planet-friends-music-video-plan.md.

## Assets

- Song: content/music/planet-friends-mission-control/outputs (the v2 lyrics
  draft, seed 42), lyrics in content/music/planet-friends-mission-control/lyrics.txt.
- Characters: the APPROVED cutouts only, assets/friends/*.png (copied from
  public/digi-squad/friends) and the DiGi 3D star. Never regenerate a character
  (brand/GDC_SOCIAL_VISUAL_SYSTEM.md section 1).

## Customizations

- House tokens win over any preset: ink #1A1A2E and espresso #2E2818 canvas,
  friend accents (Pebble #C99A28, Bloop #6C9E38, Orbit #3E86BC, Nova #7E5AB0,
  Cosmo #CE7328), brand gold #EDC35F. Nunito 900 and IBM Plex Mono only. No
  marker face, no purple gradient, no dashes in any on screen text.
- Grit comes from texture: ink outline, halftone, grain, misregistration,
  comic panels.
- Ends on the stage check: three questions, no sign up.
