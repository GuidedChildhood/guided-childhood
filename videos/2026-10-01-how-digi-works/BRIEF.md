# How DiGi works, the brain film (v2)

1 and 2 October 2026. Asked for by Justin: a cinematic cartoon where DiGi's
brain comes out to explain how he works, with the real studies going in, the
joining shown the way a brain does it, a worked example (a six year old in
pyjamas putting the TV on at 9pm) taken step by step to the answer, and the
free start at the end. Guided Childhood brand. Higgsfield Seedance 2.5 clips
from the DiGi reference art (`digi-squad/Digi.png`), captions and motion
graphics in HyperFrames.

workflow: faceless-explainer
flow: standard
destination: LinkedIn, YouTube, the DiGi page (16:9 first; 9:16 after approval)
length: 66 seconds, ten beats

## What the research said (2 October, full report in plans/2026-10-02-how-it-works-film-research.md)

- Duolingo's Birdbrain explainer: the owl with a glowing brain, then ONE
  named learner and a worked example. Its retention post names Ebbinghaus
  1885 before its own model: a trusted, dated source first.
- Vox and Kurzgesagt: withhold the result until the viewer has seen why it
  is hard; one thing moves at a time; the camera follows it; one memory per
  film.
- Headspace, Noom, Notion: stage the mechanism inside a head or on a face,
  never as boxes and arrows.
- Grammarly: the clearest pipeline, six numbered steps with humans named at
  each stage, and the feedback loop as the last step.
- Wistia and Vidyard: under a minute averages half the audience to the end,
  most drop around the halfway mark, so the question goes in the first five
  seconds and the answer before the end.

The example answer is written to DiGi's voice rules and the EB-01 pattern
(the timer, the deal, structure not minutes, relationship before rule). It
is an illustration of the shape of an answer, not a transcript of the live
product, and the caption never claims an outcome for the child.

## Rules carried

- Identity from the reference art, never from the prompt. Prompts describe
  action only. The child in beat 1 is a cartoon in the same toy world, never
  presented as a real child.
- No text generated inside the clips. Every word is ours, in HyperFrames.
- No dark tech look, no neon. Warm cream, gold light. The one dim scene is
  the night living room.
- No NHS, no clinician names, no outcome claims.
- No dashes anywhere.

## Production

Seedance drafts at 480p (18 credits a clip, ten clips so far), upscaled and
transcoded to H.264 with a keyframe every second (Seedance returns HEVC 10
bit, which Chromium cannot play). Finalising at 1080p is 72 a clip. Vertical
cut after Justin approves the landscape. `archive/index-v1.html.txt` is the
first cut.
