comes out to explain how he works. Guided Childhood brand, not The Social
Billboard. Built with Higgsfield Seedance 2.5 from the DiGi reference art
(`digi-squad/Digi.png`), stitched in HyperFrames with our captions.

## Rules carried

- Identity from the reference art, never from the prompt. Prompts describe
  action only.
- No text generated inside the clips. Every word is ours, in HyperFrames.
- No dark tech look, no neon. Warm cream, gold light.
- No NHS, no clinician names, no outcome claims for a child.
- No dashes anywhere.
