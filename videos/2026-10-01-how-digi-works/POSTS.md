## LinkedIn (Justin's profile, 10:00)

People ask me what DiGi actually does when a parent types a question.

So we drew it.

A parent asks something real. DiGi pulls the six research findings that fit,
from sixty five we can point at and name. Then it reasons over them. A few
days later it asks the parent whether the suggestion worked, and that answer
feeds the next one, as a count, never as their words.

Then the rails. Safety first. Never allow or deny. Never invent a study. A
person approves anything new that goes in.

That is the whole machine. Forty eight seconds.

What would you want it to never do?
