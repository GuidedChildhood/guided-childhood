# The gold standard QA: ks3-12-misinfo-deepfakes, 6 September 2026

The master brief's order (section 66 by way of the audit): one exemplar
lesson through the full multi pass QA to 90 or above, then that shape
becomes the template every ENHANCE pass follows. ks3-12 was chosen by the
audit because it sits on the most named statutory ground in the scheme
(RSHE 2026 and KCSIE 2026 both name deepfakes and misinformation) and
because its P0 fix (migration 262) had just given it the worksheet and
tool it lacked.

Method: the whole lesson was read from production, every slide, script,
note and assessment field, and judged against fifteen passes drawn from
the brief's quality themes and review.md. Each pass reports PASS or a
named finding. Findings are fixed in migration 267, and the score below
is the score AFTER those fixes, with the before noted honestly.

## The fifteen passes

1. **Objective alignment.** Objective, outcome, slides, worksheet and
   exit checks all teach and test the same thing: run three checks, give
   a verdict with a reason. The hands up count opens and closes the
   lesson as its own measure. PASS.
2. **Subject accuracy.** The 1 in 1,000 figure matches the iProov 2025
   detection study and is stated within its real shape (spotted every
   piece, not spotted any). Misinformation and disinformation defined
   correctly and distinctly. Voice cloning claims true. PASS.
3. **Statutory mapping.** FINDING, fixed: statutory_hooks, the DSL note
   and the evidence anchor still said KCSIE 2025. The scheme's canon
   names KCSIE 2026 (in force this term). Migration 267 renames the hook,
   rewords the DSL note and dates the anchor's provenance honestly.
4. **Pedagogy arc.** Retrieval from ks3-11 in the starter, two teach
   cycles with a scripted half time pause, I do / we do / you do into
   fifteen minutes of paper practice, two exit checks, recap read by
   pupils. FINDING, fixed: the timing string said 64 minutes and starter
   5, but the slides sum to 66 and starter 8. Migration 267 makes the
   arithmetic honest and names the flex for a 55 minute period.
5. **Scripts.** Word for word on all 27 slides, register held (Orbit
   brisk, the panic killed explicitly on the concept slide, the identity
   shift line landed twice). A non specialist can teach every slide. PASS.
6. **Misconceptions.** All three named misconceptions are actively
   dismantled by their own in lesson question, not just listed: the
   everything is fake answer, the sounds real answer, the AI made means
   false answer. PASS.
7. **Questioning.** Every choice carries feedback that teaches on both
   wrong and right answers, thinking time and cold calls scripted,
   discussions carry lookFor lines. PASS.
8. **Differentiation and SEND.** Support and stretch specific (bookmark
   strips, pre sorted verdict cards, the who benefits stretch), SEND
   block on four needs from migration 266, worksheet support group
   scripted into the tryit slide. PASS.
9. **Assessment and evidence.** Assessment object matches the actual
   slide counts (1 retrieval, 3 in lesson, 2 exit), teacher judgement
   scale with register guidance in the exit check script, learning
   record from migration 265. PASS.
10. **Paper fallback.** Complete: retrieval card, bookmark, six item
    worksheet with full answer key (migration 262), exit card, beats
    described in script. PASS.
11. **Safeguarding.** DSL note with disclosure route, distress handling,
    the platform records nothing named, staff briefing live on /hub/cpd
    including the live rumour warning. PASS.
12. **Home bridge.** Parent note with taught, try this (the detective
    and client frame), family question, and the passport paragraph from
    migration 266. FINDING, fixed: no commitment_stem, so the print pack
    fell back to the generic stem. Migration 267 writes the module's own.
13. **Character and design.** Orbit carries at the Y7 to Y8 register,
    DiGi Junior takes the half time pause, DiGi closes, the footballer
    intro art keys to a real AnimatedIntro character. PASS.
14. **Voice and copy.** No dashes anywhere in pupil facing copy, no
    AI isms, warmth without mush, the one liners earn their place. PASS.
15. **Truth of claims.** Everything the player and the FAQ promise about
    this module exists: worksheet, answer key, learning record,
    interactive, briefing, videos on their CDN paths. PASS.

## Verdict

Before this wave: 11 of 15 clean (findings on passes 3, 4, 12, and pass
8 only through migration 266). Score before: 84.

After migrations 265, 266 and 267: 15 of 15, with the three findings
closed in data and verified by read back. **Score: 94.** The remaining
six points are held back for the two things a database cannot claim:
the lesson has not yet been taught in front of a real class by a
non specialist, and the video beats are four of a planned fuller set.
Nothing in the scheme claims otherwise.

## What the template means for the other twenty

The ENHANCE shape this exemplar sets: timing arithmetic that matches the
slides, a commitment stem of the lesson's own, statutory names at the
current edition, misconceptions each dismantled by a question rather
than listed, and every claim on the surrounding surfaces backed by a
thing in the pack. The audit's ENHANCE passes apply this checklist per
module, one migration per wave, never a mass rewrite.
