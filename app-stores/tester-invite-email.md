# The tester invitation email

Google will not let a new personal developer account go public until at least
12 testers have been opted in to a closed test for 14 days in a row
(https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465).
Invite 15 or more, because somebody always forgets.

**Before sending:** put the testers' Google account emails (the one on their
Android phone's Play Store, usually a Gmail) into a Google Group, attach that
group to the closed test in Play Console, and copy the closed test's opt in
link into the email where it says [OPT IN LINK].

**Who to ask:** parents already using Guided Childhood on an Android phone
first, then friends and family with an Android phone. An iPhone cannot help.

---

**Subject:** Could you help me get Guided Childhood onto Google Play?

Hi [first name],

I'm putting Guided Childhood on the Google Play store, and I need a small favour.

Google asks every new app to be tested by at least twelve real people for fourteen days before it can go public. I'd love you to be one of them. It takes about two minutes, and then you just leave it on your phone.

What to do, on your Android phone:

1. Open this link while signed in to the Google account you use for the Play Store: [OPT IN LINK]
2. Tap "Become a tester".
3. Tap the link on that page to get Guided Childhood from Google Play, and install it.
4. Open it once and sign in, or create a free account.
5. Keep it installed until [DATE, 15 days from sending].

That's it. You don't need to use it every day, though if you do, I'd really like to hear what you think. Just reply to this email.

If the link says you're not invited, reply and tell me which email your Play Store uses, and I'll add it.

Thank you. This genuinely helps.

Justin
Guided Childhood
